# IRDAI Guidelines: Original Documents for Reimbursement Claims

## Why This Reference Exists

TPAs (Medi Assist, etc.) frequently ask policyholders to courier **original medical records** (discharge summary, bills, reports) for reimbursement claims — and to use their portal instead of accepting emailed submissions. This reference equips you to push back with specific IRDAI regulatory citations.

## Key IRDAI Directives

### 1. Document Collection — Insurer/TPA Responsibility

**Master Circular on Health Insurance Business, 29 May 2024**
Ref: IRDAI/HLT/CIR/PRO/84/5/2024

> "Insurers / TPAs must collect all documents from the **hospital**, not from the insured."

This is the single most powerful rebuttal when a TPA asks you to courier originals. The regulatory direction is clear: the TPA should be collecting documents from the hospital directly. Asking the policyholder to courier them is contrary to the circular's spirit.

### 2. No Mandate for Original Records

There is **no IRDAI regulation** that requires a policyholder to submit original medical records for a reimbursement claim. TPAs may request them for verification, but:

- Policyholders are entitled to retain custody of their original medical records (personal health data)
- **Notarized copies** carry the same legal evidentiary value and should be accepted
- Scanned/photographed copies submitted via email or portal are the modern standard

### 3. Protection of Policyholders' Interests

**Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 5 Sep 2024**
Ref: IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024

Chapter on "Servicing of policyholders and settlements of claims" reinforces:
- Grievance resolution within **14 days** from acknowledgement
- Claims settlement within **30 days** from receipt of last necessary document
- Delays beyond 30 days attract **interest at 2% above RBI bank rate**

### 4. Portal Submission Not Mandatory

When a TPA says "use our portal":
- The Master Circular does NOT mandate a specific submission channel
- Email submission with all attachments is a valid and complete submission
- Asking a policyholder to duplicate an already-complete email submission on a portal is a procedural hurdle, not a regulatory requirement

## Rebuttal Email Template (key paragraphs)

```
Per the IRDAI Master Circular on Health Insurance Business
(Ref: IRDAI/HLT/CIR/PRO/84/5/2024, dated 29 May 2024), the regulatory
direction is clear — insurers and TPAs must collect documents from
hospitals directly, not from the insured. The Master Circular on
Protection of Policyholders' Interests (Ref: IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024,
dated 5 September 2024) further reinforces that the responsibility of
document collection rests with the insurer/TPA.

There is no IRDAI requirement mandating that a policyholder must submit
original medical records for a reimbursement claim. The policyholder's
original medical records are their personal health data, and they are
entitled to retain custody of them. I am willing to provide notarized
copies of all documents in lieu of originals.
```

## Additional IRDAI Protections to Cite

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Claims settlement timeline** | 30 days from receipt of last document (interest payable after that) |
| **No repudiation without CRC** | No claim can be rejected without Claims Review Committee approval |
| **Moratorium** | After 5 years continuous coverage, claims cannot be contested except for fraud |
| **Grievance timeline** | TPA must resolve grievances within 14 working days of acknowledgement |
| **Escalation path** | GRO → IRDAI Bima Bharosa portal → Insurance Ombudsman (award up to ₹50L) |
