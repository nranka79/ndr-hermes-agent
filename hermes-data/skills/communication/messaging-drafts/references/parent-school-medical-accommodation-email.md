# Parent-School Medical Accommodation Email Drafting

## When to Use
User asks to draft an email to a school/teacher about a child's medical condition, requesting accommodations, study plans, or collaborative support.

## Typical Scenario
- Child has a chronic/recurring medical condition (asthma, autoimmune, post-infection recovery)
- Child missed exams or school days due to health episodes
- School has already been supportive (email thread exists)
- Upcoming parent-teacher meeting is approaching
- Goal: Draft a polite, collaborative email requesting a study plan + in-school medical support

## Workflow

### Phase 1: Analyze Existing Email Thread
1. Search Gmail for the school email thread using `q="school_name OR teacher_name child_name (medical OR absent OR exam)"`
2. Read the full thread to understand:
   - Timeline of medical episodes and absences
   - What the school has already offered (nebulization, nurse support, exam rescheduling)
   - Tone of previous communications (supportive, formal, concerned)
3. Summarise the timeline + key offers to the user before drafting

### Phase 2: Draft the Email

Structure (from worked example — Jun 2026, MAIS/Ruhaan Ranka):

```
To: Class teachers
CC: School admin, spouse
Subject: [Child Name] — Std [X] — Collaborative Support Plan & Upcoming Parent-Teacher Meeting

Paragraph 1: Thank the teachers for their past support
- Reference their specific offers (nebulization, nurse, mask, exam flexibility)
- Say you want to take them up on those arrangements

Paragraph 2: In-school medical support
- Request specific accommodations (nebulizer in infirmary, scheduled medication)
- Keep it practical — these are discussions for the meeting

Paragraph 3: Study plan for episodic absences
- Explain the condition briefly (frequency of episodes, recovery time, impact on academics)
- Request a flexible study plan — not lower expectations, but a pre-agreed roadmap
- Ask if the school has existing frameworks for such situations

Paragraph 4: Collaborative brainstorm
- Frame the meeting as a team effort (parents + teachers + school)
- Offer to share any medical documentation the school needs
- Request advance sharing of curriculum schedules or materials

Closing: Thank them again, express eagerness to meet
```

### Tone Guidelines
- **Polite and collaborative** — "we would like to explore together," "we would be grateful if," "your guidance will be invaluable"
- **NEVER confrontational** — don't demand, don't imply the school is failing
- **Medical details at parent's discretion** — share what the parent authorizes (lung capacity %, diagnosis, medication schedule)
- **Keep it parent-initiated** — "we want to ensure" not "you need to ensure"

### Key Phrases from Worked Example
- "Your response has been deeply reassuring to us as parents"
- "We would appreciate if [accommodation] could be kept available for emergencies"
- "The idea is not to reduce expectations, but to have a clear, pre-agreed roadmap"
- "If the school has any existing frameworks or recommendations, we would love to hear them"
- "We would like to use the meeting as an opportunity to collaboratively think through"

### Pitfalls
- **Don't include escalation threats** — this is a partnership email, not a complaint
- **Don't over-share medical details** — share only what the parent has already disclosed or explicitly authorizes
- **Don't skip the thank-you** — the school's existing support should be acknowledged explicitly
- **Don't use Chinese characters** — (real pitfall from Jun 2026: accidentally included characters from a web search snippet in a draft; always review generated text for non-ASCII artifacts)
