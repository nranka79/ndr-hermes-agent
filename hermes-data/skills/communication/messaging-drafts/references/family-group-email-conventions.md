# Family Group Email Conventions (Nishant Ranka)

Conventions for drafting group emails to Nishant's family (Dharmesh Ranka, Manish Ranka, Mamtha Rathod/maadi32).

## 1. Greeting Format

**Use:** `Hi everyone,`
**Not:** `Family,` or `Dear family,`

## 2. Proposal Attribution — CRITICAL

When a **third party** (lawyer, developer, consultant) has proposed a course of action:

✅ **Correct:** "L&T has suggested that we move them to the escrow custody of their Advocate Anantram, ultimately to be handed over to the Association."

❌ **Incorrect:** "Alternatively, we could move them to Anantram's escrow..."

**Rule:** Always attribute the proposal to the party who made it. Do NOT present a third party's suggestion as the sender's own alternative/framing. This maintains accuracy about who is driving the decision and avoids the impression that the sender is generating options they haven't discussed.

## 3. Tone

- Partnership-oriented, inclusive ("if everyone approves...")
- Present your own view clearly, then invite input
- Keep it warm but businesslike
