# Instruction-Heavy Third-Party Email — Worked Example

**Trigger:** Nishant says "create a detailed instruction sheet" or asks you to draft an email to a third party (Raghu, Manohar, partner, client) explaining a multi-step process with a website portal involved.

**Pattern:** This is a real template from Jun 2026 — a step-by-step DSC application guide sent to Raghu Iyer for Farida's DSC via eMudhra.

---

## Email Structure Template

```
Subject: [Topic] — Detailed Instructions

[Name],

Why [action/step] is needed
(1-2 paragraphs explaining the motivation, the blocker that will be resolved, and what happens if it's not done)

---

Detailed Step-by-Step Instructions — [Process Name]

We recommend using [Portal Name] ([portal URL]) — [brief why this portal]. The entire process is online and paperless.

Step 1 — Visit the [Portal Name] portal
- Go to: [exact URL]
- Select [specific option/product]

Step 2 — Fill in the application
- Enter [Person]'s details: [what fields]
- Select [specific options]

Step 3 — Upload documents
Prepare the following documents as scanned copies or clear smartphone photos:
- [ ] [Document 1] (both sides if applicable)
- [ ] [Document 2]
- [ ] [Document 3]
Upload all documents on the portal

Step 4 — Make payment
- Pay online via [payment methods]
- Cost: approximately Rs [X]–Rs [Y] for standard ([timeframe])
- If urgent/same-day is needed (Rs [X]–Rs [Y]), select the expedited option

Step 5 — [Verification step, e.g., VBI call]
- After submitting, a [duration] [type of verification] will be scheduled
- The [platform] representative will call on [WhatsApp/Zoom/portal]
- During the call, [Person] needs to:
  - [Action 1 — show documents on camera]
  - [Action 2 — confirm details verbally]
  - [Action 3 — state intent/purpose]
- The entire call is recorded — this is the legally valid audit trail

Step 6 — Receive [outcome] via email
- Once [verification step] is successfully completed, the [outcome] will be issued
- You will receive an email containing:
  - A download link for the [file type]
  - A [password/credential] to access it
- Download the file and save it securely

Step 7 — [Post-receipt action, e.g., Install]
- [Instructions for what to do with what was received]
- [Specific steps — double-click, enter password, etc.]

Step 8 — Use the [outcome] to [final action]
- Once [prerequisite is met], [person] can [do the final action]
- [Alternative approach if applicable]

For yourself ([Name]):
- [Your specific instructions — Aadhaar OTP, etc.]

Summary of what needs to happen:

| Person | Method | Who does it |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| [Person A] | [Method] | [Responsible party] |
| [Person A's signature] | [Method] | [Responsible party] |
| [Person B] | [Method] | [Responsible party] |

Please prioritize this. If you need any help or assistance with any step, let me know and we're happy to assist.

Nishant
```

## Key Principles

1. **Assume zero prior knowledge** — The recipient has never done this process before. Every step must be self-explanatory.
2. **Every URL must be exact** — `https://esign.emudhra.com/` not "the eMudhra portal". Test mentally: can the recipient start at Step 1 without asking a question?
3. **Explain the WHY for each significant step** — "The video verification call is recorded — this is the legally valid audit trail."
4. **Call out friction points explicitly** — "There may be a small video verification call she will have to set up." "Post that once documents are uploaded, a DSC will arrive via email with a link and password."
5. **Provide cost and timeline upfront** — Recipients need to know: how much will this cost, how long will it take.
6. **End with a summary table** — Quick reference showing who does what and by which method.
7. **Close with offer of help** — One line, after the complete instructions. Not as a crutch for missing detail.
8. **Verify portal/website names via explicit confirmation before finalizing** — Voice transcription easily mangles portal names (eMudhra → "MUTRA", esign.emudhra.com → "Mutra portal"). After drafting but before presenting for approval, quote the exact portal name and URL back to the user for confirmation. A wrong URL means Step 1 doesn't work, and correcting it costs a full round trip.

## Concrete Worked Example — eMudhra DSC for E-signing

**Context (from Jun 2026 session):** Nishant needed Raghu Iyer's wife (Farida) to get her own DSC because the sub-registrar system does NOT allow a person to double E-sign as both themselves AND as a representative. Since Raghu was only signing as himself (Aadhaar OTP), Farida needed her own Class 3 DSC to sign as landowner.

**Email structure used:**

```
Subject: Farida's DSC & E-signing of Sharing Agreement — Next Steps

Raghu,

BRIEFING NOTE — Why Farida needs her own DSC

The sub-registrar will only accept E-signatures for this document.
Unfortunately, the system does NOT allow a person to double E-sign as
both themselves and as a representative/authorized signatory for someone else.
Since you (Raghu) will be E-signing via Aadhaar OTP as yourself, Farida
needs her own Digital Signature Certificate.

Getting Farida her own DSC also helps for ALL future documentation
associated with this project — particularly when you sell units to
your customers/leads. Having a DSC ready will save time on every
subsequent transaction.

---

DETAILED INSTRUCTIONS — Apply for Farida's Class 3 DSC via eMudhra

We recommend using the eMudhra portal (esign.emudhra.com) — an
authorized Certifying Authority recognized under the IT Act 2000.
The entire process is online and paperless.

Step 1 — Visit the eMudhra portal
- Go to: https://esign.emudhra.com/
- Select "Apply for DSC" or "Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate"
- Choose: Individual / Signing only

Step 2 — Fill in Farida's application
- Enter Farida's full name (as per passport/PAN)
- Enter her email address (where DSC will be delivered)
- Enter her mobile number (for OTP verification)
- Select: Individual Applicant
- Select: Class 3 DSC (Signing only)

Step 3 — Upload documents
Prepare the following documents as scanned copies or clear
smartphone photos:
- [ ] PAN Card (both sides)
- [ ] Passport-size photograph
- [ ] Aadhaar Card (both sides) OR Passport (for OCI holders)
- [ ] Proof of address (if different from Aadhaar)
Upload all documents on the portal

Step 4 — Make payment
- Pay online via credit/debit card, net banking, or UPI
- Cost: approximately Rs 1,500–2,500 for standard (2-3 days)
- If urgent/same-day is needed (Rs 3,000–5,000), select the
  expedited option

Step 5 — Video-Based Identification (VBI) call
- After submitting, a short video verification call will be scheduled
- The eMudhra representative will call on WhatsApp or the portal
  itself within 24-48 hours
- During the call, Farida needs to:
  - Show her original PAN card and Aadhaar on camera
  - Confirm her details verbally — name, address, purpose
  - State that the DSC is for E-signing legal documents
- The entire call is recorded — this becomes the legally valid audit
  trail for the DSC issuance

Step 6 — Receive DSC via email
- Once the VBI is successfully completed, the DSC will be issued
- You will receive an email from eMudhra containing:
  - A download link for the DSC (.pfx or .cer file)
  - A one-time password to access the file
- Download the file and save it in a secure folder

Step 7 — Install the DSC on a computer
- Double-click the downloaded .pfx file
- Enter the password received via email
- Follow the certificate import wizard (default settings work)
- Once installed, the DSC will be available in the system's
  certificate store (visible in browser certificates)

Step 8 — Use the DSC for E-signing
- Once I upload the Sharing Agreement document for E-sign on the
  portal, Farida will receive a signing link
- She opens the link, selects her installed DSC, and signs
- The portal supports this — she just needs the DSC installed on the
  computer she is using

For you (Raghu):
- You can E-sign using your Aadhaar OTP — no DSC needed
- Same as what I'll be doing from my end
- Aadhaar OTP-based E-sign is supported for all Indian citizens

Summary of what needs to happen:

| Person | Method | Who does |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Farida's DSC | eMudhra Class 3 | Farida applies, Raghu assists |
| Farida's signature | DSC (after install) | Farida signs on her computer |
| Raghu's signature | Aadhaar OTP | Raghu signs on his device |
| Nishant's signature | Aadhaar OTP | Nishant signs remotely |

Please prioritize this. If you need any help or assistance with any
step, let me know and we're happy to assist.

Nishant
```

## When NOT to use this template

- Internal coordination emails (Gowri, Anbu, Roshni) — use the brief direct style
- Status updates or progress reports
- Calendar invites
- Follow-ups where the process is already known
