## IRDAI (Insurance Regulatory & Development Authority of India)

### Grievance Portal
- **IGMS (Integrated Grievance Management System):** https://igms.irdai.gov.in
- **Complaints Email:** `complaints@irdai.gov.in`
- **Toll Free:** `1800-425-4732` / `155255`
- **Website:** https://www.irdai.gov.in

### Escalation Process
1. **NIC Internal Grievance** — Email `grievance@nic.co.in` with CC to `nodal.officer@nic.co.in` and `cgo@nic.co.in`
2. **IRDAI IGMS** — Register on https://igms.irdai.gov.in if NIC does not resolve within 15 days, or if the claim is delayed beyond 30 days from survey report
3. **Insurance Ombudsman** — For claim repudiation, partial settlement, or delay beyond 30 days after exhausting internal grievance

## Key IRDAI Regulations for Motor Insurance Claims

| Regulation | What it says |
|---|---|
| **PPI Reg 10(4)** | Surveyor must submit report within **30 days** of appointment |
| **PPI Reg 10(5)** | Insurer must settle/reject within **30 days** of receiving survey report |
| **PPI Reg 10(5A)** | **Interest at 2% above RBI bank rate** payable on delayed settlement from the date it ought to have been settled |
| **Consumer Protection Act 2019, Sec 2(11)(c)** | Delay in claim settlement = "deficiency in service" |
