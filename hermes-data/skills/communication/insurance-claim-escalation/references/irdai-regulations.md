# IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, 2017

Key regulations for motor insurance claim escalation. References use the **2017 Regulations** (replaces 2002 version).

---

## IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, 2024 — Life Insurance

The **PPI Regulations 2024** replace the 2017 Regulations and govern life insurance policy issuance, servicing, revival, and grievance handling. Key provisions for revival disputes:

### Policy Revival (Regulation 8)

| Provision | Requirement | Timeline |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| **Reg 8(1)** | Insurer may revive a lapsed policy subject to: (a) payment of arrears with interest, (b) declaration of good health or other evidence of insurability, (c) any condition in the policy contract | As per policy terms |
| **Revival TAT** | Decision on revival application must be communicated | **Within 7 days** of receipt of all required documents |
| **Reg 8(2)** | Insurer cannot demand documents beyond what the policy terms and regulations prescribe | At the time of revival application |
| **Revival scope** | "Other evidence of insurability" refers to **health/mortality risk**, not financial re-underwriting | The term "insurability" in life insurance means mortality risk — income verification was already done at inception |

### Grievance Redressal (Regulation 17)

| Provision | Requirement | Timeline |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| **Reg 17(1)** | Every insurer must have a Board-approved Grievance Redressal Policy | Published on website |
| **Reg 17(2)** | Acknowledge receipt of grievance | Immediately |
| **Reg 17(3)** | Communicate final decision on grievance | **Within 14 days** |
| **Reg 17(4)** | If grievance NOT resolved, communicate the policyholder's right to approach Insurance Ombudsman / Consumer Court | Within 14 days from receipt of complaint |
| **Reg 17(5)** | Insurer's Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO) is the single point of contact | Name and contact published on website |

### Fair Treatment (Chapter II)

| Provision | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Fair Treatment** | Insurer must act with utmost good faith (*uberrima fides*) |
| **No unreasonable conditions** | Cannot impose unreasonable or repeated conditions on a policyholder |
| **Communication** | All communication must be in writing and on record |
| **Delay reasons** | If redressal is delayed, the insurer must communicate the reasons in writing |

### Premium Handling

| Provision | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Reg 3(3)** | If a premium payment instrument is dishonoured, the insurer must give at least **15 days' notice** before treating the policy as lapsed |
| **Reversal without notice** | Unilateral reversal of a validly paid premium without notice is a violation of PPI Regulations and the duty of good faith |

### Key Regulatory Distinction — Life vs Motor Insurance

| Aspect | Life Insurance (PPI 2024) | Motor/General Insurance (PPI 2017) |
|--------|--------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Key issue** | Revival rejection, lapse dispute | Claim settlement delay |
| **Critical time limit** | 7 days for revival decision | 30-45 days for claim settlement |
| **Strongest legal weapon** | Section 45, Insurance Act 1938 (3-year bar) | Reg 10(5) interest for delayed payment |
| **Evidence type** | Policy bond, premium receipts, bank statements | Surveyor report, repair invoices |
| **Remedial forum** | NCDRC (unlimited jurisdiction for deficiency) | Insurance Ombudsman (up to ₹30L) |

## Motor Insurance Claim Regulations (Original content below)

### Claim Registration & Acknowledgment

| Reg | Requirement | Timeline |
|-----|-------------|----------|
| **10(1)** | Acknowledge receipt of claim | Within 24 hours |
| **10(1)** | Assign claim number | Upon registration |

## Survey & Assessment

| Reg | Requirement | Timeline |
|-----|-------------|----------|
| **10(3)** | Appoint IRDAI-licensed surveyor | Within 72 hours of claim intimation |
| **10(4)** | Surveyor to submit report (cash loss claims) | Within **7 days** of appointment |
| **10(4)** | Surveyor to submit report (all other losses) | Within **30 days** of appointment |
| **10(4)** | Surveyor can seek extension (max 6 months) | Applied to insurer in writing |

### Surveyor qualification notes
- Surveyor must hold a valid IRDAI license
- Surveyor is the insurer's representative — liability for surveyor's actions/errors rests with insurer
- Policyholder cannot be penalized for surveyor's failure to submit report on time

## Claim Settlement Timelines

| Reg | Requirement | Timeline |
|-----|-------------|----------|
| **10(5)** | Insurer to pay or reject claim from survey report receipt | Within **30 days** |
| **10(5)** | Interest payable if delayed beyond 30 days | **2% above bank rate** from date of survey report |
| **10(5A)** | Absolute maximum timeline (with documented reasons for delay) | Within **45 days** |
| **10(6)** | If claim is rejected, full reasons must be communicated | Immediately after decision |

## Document Custody

| Reg | Requirement | Timeline |
|-----|-------------|----------|
| **10(2)** | Insurer to maintain confidentiality and custody of all documents submitted | Continuous obligation |
| — | If documents are lost, insurer bears the cost of replacement | Implied duty |

## Interest Calculation

Interest under Reg 10(5) is calculated as:
```
Interest Rate = RBI Bank Rate + 2%
```
- Runs from the date of survey report (25 May 2026)
- Compounded monthly
- Payable automatically — no separate demand needed

As of June 2026, RBI Bank Rate is approximately **6.50%**, making the applicable rate ~**8.50% per annum**.

## Common Violation Patterns

1. **Silence periods >7 days** — Insurer is obligated to keep policyholder informed
2. **Document loss** — Direct violation of Reg 10(2), also delays claim processing
3. **No acknowledgment of resubmitted documents** — Procedural failure
4. **Payment beyond 30 days** — Triggers interest liability automatically
5. **No written rejection** — Silence is not a valid rejection under Reg 10(6)

## Legal Backstops

| Forum | Limit | Limitation |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Insurance Ombudsman | Claims up to ₹30 lakhs | Must file within 1 year of insurer's final response (or 1 year from when response was due) |
| IRDAI Bima Bharosa | Any amount | Online portal complaint |
| Consumer Court (District) | Up to ₹50 lakhs | Within 2 years of cause of action |
| Consumer Court (State) | ₹50 lakhs to ₹2 crores | Within 2 years |
| Consumer Court (National) | Above ₹2 crores | Within 2 years |
