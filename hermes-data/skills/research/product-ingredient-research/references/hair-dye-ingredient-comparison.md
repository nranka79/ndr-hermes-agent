**Key features:**
- No ammonia (uses MEA/ethanolamine instead)
- 60% oil-based (mineral oil) — protective barrier during processing

**Carcinogenic concerns:**
- Toluene-2,5-Diamine — PPD cousin; possible mutagen
- Resorcinol — IARC not classified, EWG moderate cancer concern
- Ethanolamine — can form nitrosamines with nitrosating agents
- P-Aminophenol, M-Aminophenol — some mutagenicity data

## Grey coverage on resistant/100% grey hair

**Henna + Indigo approach tips from research:**
- First application on virgin grey hair may need 2-3 sessions for full saturation
- Let henna sit 1-2 hours (not less); indigo sits 30-60 min (not more — indigo fades if over-processed)
- Mix with warm water (not boiling — kills dye properties)
- Add 1 tbsp coconut/olive oil to counteract dryness
- Color settles/oxidizes over 48 hours — may look coppery or greenish initially
- Re-touch roots every 2-4 weeks (same as chemical dyes)

## Shade guidance for natural henna+indigo blends on light brown hair

**Avoid too-dark options:**
- Vegetal Dark Brown — will overpower light brown hair
- Deyga Black — too dark for light brown
- Khadi Dark Brown/Black — too dark

**Good lighter-brown fits:**
- **Radico Light Brown** (~₹719) — best pre-mixed light brown option
- **DIY 40% henna + 60% indigo** — most control over shade depth

**Henna+indigo ratio cheat sheet for brown hair:**
| Desired shade | Henna % | Indigo % |
|---|---|---|
| Light warm brown | 50% | 50% |
| Medium brown | 40% | 60% |
| Dark brown | 30% | 70% |
| Ash brown (cool) | 35% | 65% + 1 tsp amla |

**Dryness prevention tips for henna/indigo:**
- ✅ Mix 1 tbsp coconut oil (or olive oil/curd) into the paste with water
- ✅ Rinse with conditioner after, not shampoo
- ✅ Follow with a light oil (coconut/argan) on damp hair
- ❌ Don't shampoo for 24-48 hours after application

## Quick reference: hair dye chemistry classes

| Chemistry class | Examples | Cancer concern |
|---|---|---|
| **PPD** | p-Phenylenediamine | Moderate (bladder cancer studies) |
| **PTD** | Toluene-2,5-Diamine | Similar to PPD (cousin molecule) |
| **Aminophenols** | P-Aminophenol, M-Aminophenol | Low-moderate (some mutagenicity) |
| **Resorcinols** | Resorcinol, 4-Chlororesorcinol | Moderate (endocrine disruptor data) |
| **Ethanolamines** | MEA, DEA, TEA | Low (nitrosamine formation risk) |
| **Peroxides** | Hydrogen Peroxide, Sodium Perborate | Very low (oxidative stress, not carcinogenic) |
| **Henna** | Lawsone (hennotannic acid) | None known |
| **Indigo** | Indigotin | None known |
