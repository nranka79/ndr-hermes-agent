## Mortgage Timing Issue — User's Decision

**Original finding:**
- Joint Simple Mortgage (Doc 7049/2025): 13 Oct 2025
- Plot 65 Sale Deed: 22 May 2026 (now changed to 27 Jul 2026)
- Discharge Receipt: 28 May 2026

**User's explicit instruction:**
> Do NOT reference the mortgage or discharge in the sale deed. The sale deed only says "no encumbrance as on date." This is cleaner. The discharge must be completed before the sale deed execution — that's the key operational requirement.

**For future deeds:** Execute sale deeds ONLY after mortgage discharge is complete. The date of the sale deed must be after the discharge date.
