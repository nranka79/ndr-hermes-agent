## Changes Applied (highlighted yellow in Google Doc)

### 1. Clause 3.1 — Completion Period
**OLD:** `within ____ months` (blank)
**NEW:** `within 18 (Eighteen) months (the "Completion Period") from the date of registration of the Sale Deed... with an additional grace period of 6 (Six) months (the "Grace Period") beyond the expiry of the Completion Period.`

### 2. Clause 6.1 — Defect Liability
**OLD:** 1 year defect liability only
**NEW:** 5 years structural (RERA Sec 14(3)) + 1 year non-structural (fittings/finishes/workmanship)

### 3. Clause 1.4 (NEW) — Variation Order Mechanism
Added a formal variation mechanism after the "no modifications" clause. Allottee requests in writing → Promoter assesses cost + timeline → signed Variation Order.

### 4. Clause 2.3 — Payment Details & Receipt (expanded)
**OLD:** Brief mention of payment
**NEW:** Full breakdown:
- (i) Construction Cost: ₹64,64,600 — paid upfront

- (iii) Total received: ₹67,87,830
- Promoter acknowledges receipt; Annexure II planned for individual transaction details

### 5. Clause 8.1 — Allottee Default (rewritten per DRA Group standard)
**Framework:**
- No refund of any amount paid
- Default charges payable
- Allottee acknowledges non-completion harms project aesthetics/reputation/sales
- Promoter authorised to sell plot+villa to third party at its discretion
- Sale proceeds appropriation: (i) construction costs → (ii) default charges → (iii) plot cost to allottee → (iv) surplus to promoter
- Allottee to execute transfer documents to new purchaser
- New purchaser + Promoter enter fresh agreements

### 6. Clause 8.2 — Promoter Default (rewritten)
**Framework:**
- Mutual remedy pathway
- Jointly identify third-party purchaser at prevailing market price for plot+constructed area
- OR Promoter within Grace Period either completes construction OR exits at market price
- Allottee executes transfer on payment

### 7. Clause 11.3 — Arbitration (fixed)
**OLD:** Sole Arbitrator appointed by Promoter
**NEW:** Sole Arbitrator mutually appointed; fallback to Civil Court at Krishnagiri/Hosur if no agreement within 30 days

### 8. Recital A & Schedule A — Phase 1 language
**OLD:** `7.53 Acres (out of the larger layout extending to 12.50 Acres)` + `"Said Land / Larger Property"`
**NEW:** `7.52 Acres (Phase 1 of the residential layout "RANKA OASIS")` + `"Said Land"`
