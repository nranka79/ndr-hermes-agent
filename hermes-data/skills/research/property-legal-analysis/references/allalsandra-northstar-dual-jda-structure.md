## Pitfalls
- Do NOT treat "Ranka North Star" GPA as covering Site 10, or vice versa.
  Any GPA must state which JDA it relates to.
- Site 10's Sub-Registrar record is **Gandhinagar/Malleshwaram** — the same
  office family as JDA 3034, but a distinct document number series (MLSD-04-...).
- The 18.08.2003 GPA is the operative one for both, but an unregistered GPA is
  no longer accepted — a fresh, registered/adjudicated GPA is required.
- Both 2021 assignments were stamped Rs. 200 under Article 12 (Bond) with nil
  consideration. Any SRO demanding full duty on the assignment must be answered
  with s.34 (department registered it), s.46A(1) (5-year bar expired 15.03.2026)
  and *Rajeshwari v. State of Karnataka* (KHC, 25.06.2026 — limitation is
  jurisdictional; notice beyond the outer limit is void).
