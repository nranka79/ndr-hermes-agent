# Lease Renewal / Extension Risk Analysis — "can the tenant stay after expiry?"

NDR's question when DRA takes a building turnkey and needs the ground-floor tenant out:
*"does this lease deed give the tenant any right to extend / stay, even if the owners want them to vacate?"*

## Clause-hunting checklist (scanned lease deeds)

1. **Duration clause** — fixed term? Watch for landlord-favourable language: "shall hand over the actual
   vacant possession … *without seeking further extension of time*".
2. **Renewal/extension** — any "option to renew", "renewed term", "further period on same terms", "mutual
   consent to extend", "right of first refusal"? Absence = no contractual right.
3. **Termination clause** — expiry by effluxion of time as automatic termination.
4. **Vacate-on-expiry clause** — explicit delivery of vacant possession at expiry; notice periods usually
   apply only to EARLIER determination by the lessee, not to natural expiry.
5. **Security-deposit refund hook** — the common tenant-side continuation right: "if the Lessor fails to
   refund the SD at expiry (lessee ready to hand over), the Lessee may continue to occupy rent-free until
   the SD + interest is paid". This is a REFUND-TIMING risk, not an extension right — but it is the one
   lawful foothold. Mitigate: simultaneous possession + SD refund (net of arrears) + deed cancellation.
6. **Holdover** — post-expiry renewal under s.116 Transfer of Property Act 1882 only arises if the LESSOR
   accepts rent after expiry. Instruct the owner to refuse any post-expiry rent.
7. **Statutory shield check** — Karnataka Rent Act 1999 protects only premises below a monthly-rent
   threshold (BBMP area; the commonly cited ceiling is ~₹3,500/mo — verify current threshold). A ₹1L+/mo
   commercial lease is OUTSIDE the Act → no statutory tenancy protection, ejectment follows the TP Act.
8. **Subletting/assignment clause** — if the physical operator ≠ named lessee (operating brand company vs
   franchise entity), that can be a subletting breach under the deed → an early termination route for the
   owner before expiry.

## Worked example: No. 70/3 Millers Road (Silver tip Foods / "India Chai") — analysed 2026-10-12

- Deed of Lease executed 01-Dec-2018, registered 21-12-2018 (Book 1, Doc 5133); 15-page CamScanner scan,
  no text layer → OCR at 200 dpi, tesseract `--psm 6 -l eng` (out: `/tmp/lease_ocr.txt`), filed at
  `DRA Realty / Millers Road Office Building / Title / 20181201_MillersRoad70-3_LeaseDeed_SilvertipFoodsPvtLtd.pdf`.
- **Lessors:** Mrs A. Mohsina Begum, Mrs M. Sara Banu, Mr M. Akber Hussain, Mr M. Akther Hussain — owners of
  No. 70/3 Millers Road, Bangalore 560052, all represented by GPA holder Mr M. Akber Hussain (the "Akber"
  of NDR's dictation). **Lessee:** M/s. Silver tip Foods Pvt Ltd (PAN ABACS2328B, Dir. Aslam Ahmed Sardar) —
  the entity running the ground-floor India Chai outlet.
- **Demised:** ground floor, 2,675 sq ft built-up + one car park in front. **Term:** 9 years, 01/02/2019 →
  **31/01/2028** (NDR estimated "end Dec 2027" — always quote the deed date when correcting).
- Rent ₹1,00,000/mo from 01/03/2019 + 5% p.a.; rent-free 2 months; lock-in 3 years; SD ₹6,00,000 (NEFT refs
  listed in clause 5.1); TDS by lessee; 18% p.a. late interest.
- **Clause 1:** fixed 9 years; at expiry the Lessee "shall hand over the actual vacant possession of the
  Demised Premises **without seeking further extension of time**".
- **No renewal/option clause** anywhere (all 23 clauses reviewed).
- **Clause 5.5 (the only hook):** if Lessors fail to refund SD when the Lessee is ready to hand over at
  expiry/earlier determination, Lessee may continue to occupy **rent-free** until SD + 18% interest paid.
  Clause 5.3: refund + vacant possession + cancellation of the lease deed are simultaneous.
- **Clause 19:** vacant possession on expiry "peaceably and without demur"; 2-month notice applies only to
  earlier determination by the Lessee. **Clause 20.1:** termination on expiration of the term.
- **Clause 6:** no subletting/assignment/parting with possession without written consent.
- **Verdict delivered to NDR:** no contractual right to renew or remain; the only risk is refund timing
  (₹6L) at 31/01/2028; Karnataka Rent Act does not apply (rent ≫ threshold) → no statutory shield; s.116
  holdover only if the owner accepts post-expiry rent.

## Action bundle for DRA turnkey takeovers (recommend to NDR)

- Written no-extension / possession-due notice 3–6 months before expiry (paper trail).
- At expiry: simultaneous possession handover + SD refund (net of arrears) + lease deed cancellation.
- Owner must REFUSE any post-expiry rent acceptance.
- Verify who physically operates vs the named lessee (subletting breach = early exit route).
