## Red Line Phase 3A (Hebbal–Sarjapur) — the Sarjapur line

Stations Sarjapur → Hebbal: Sarjapur (town, southern terminus), Kada Agrahara
Road, Sompura, Dommasandra, Muthanallur Cross, Kodathi Circle, Ambedkar Nagar,
Carmelaram, Doddakannelli, Kaikondrahalli, Bellandur Gate, Ibbalur (Blue
interchange), Agara (Blue), Jakkasandra, Koramangala 3rd Block, Koramangala
2nd Block, Dairy Circle (Pink), NIMHANS, Shantinagar, Town Hall, KR Circle
(Purple), Basaveshwara Circle, Bengaluru Golf Course, Palace Guttahalli,
Mekhri Circle, Ganganagar, Hebbal (Blue + Orange).

Key facts:
- Terminal is AT Sarjapur town (12.86N 77.79E) — top end of the Sarjapur–Attibele road.
- Revised DPR (SYSTRA): cost cut ₹28,405 cr → ~₹25,485-25,999 cr; Veterinary College and Kodati Gate stations dropped; Sarjapur Terminal station added (28 → 27/28 station counts vary by source).
- Last stretch approaches Sarjapur from the NW via Dommasandra/Sompura/Kada Agrahara — NOT along the Sarjapur–Attibele road.
- Double-decker viaduct sections planned: Sarjapur→Ibbalur (~14 km) and Agara→Koramangala 3rd Block (~2.45 km).
- OSM has an unofficial sketched alignment (5 ways named "Red Line (Sarjapur ⇔ Hebbal)", ~706 points, chains Sarjapur→Hebbal) — usable for indicative KML.

## Electronic City connectivity

- Yellow Line (operational): stations incl. Electronic City, Infosys Konappana Agrahara, Huskur Road, Biocon Hebbagodi, Bommasandra (Delta Electronics terminal). Interchanges: RV Road (Green), Tavarekere (Purple), Central Silk Board (Blue, when open), Jayadeva (Pink).
- Yellow Line extension Bommasandra→Attibele: 10.5 km elevated, 9 stations — Narayana Institute of Cardiac Sciences, Chandapura Circle, Hale (Old) Chandapura, Thirumagondanahalli, Balagaranahalli, Attibele Industrial Area, Yadavanahalli, Attibele, Attibele Terminal (DH 12 Aug 2026). Feasibility done; state cleared DPR; tenders to be invited. Along right side of Hosur Road NH44.
- Inter-state Bengaluru–Hosur metro: BMRCL says "technically not feasible" — 750V DC third rail vs TN 25kV AC, 6-car vs 3-car trains, land acquisition and fare-sharing unresolved. Proposed compromise: both states build to the border and connect the two Attibele stations with a 90 m footbridge.

## 72 km "longest line" proposal (feasibility only, Oct 2025)

- Corridor: Kalena Agrahara (Bannerghatta Rd) → Bannerghatta → Jigani → Attibele → Sarjapur → Dommasandra Circle → Varthur Kodi → Kadugodi Tree Park (Whitefield). 50+ stations, mixed elevated/underground.
- 3.5–4 km spur branches after Jigani toward Anekal to the international sports complex at Surya City Phase 4, Indlavadi (80k cricket stadium, 24 sports, 30k auditorium).
- Feasibility report (Introsoft Solutions) submitted Oct 2025; no approval; construction 2029 at earliest, 5–10 years after. Part of BMRCL 210.9 km expansion → 467.69 km network vision.
- Exact Attibele–Sarjapur alignment NOT published; if it follows the Sarjapur–Attibele road it would pass Rathibele.

## Sarjapur–Rathibele–Attibele corridor (DRAAS land context)

- No metro line runs on the Sarjapur–Rathibele–Attibele road itself.
- Nearest metro: Red Line terminal at Sarjapur town (proposed, ~2030-31) at the north end; proposed Yellow Line Attibele Terminal at the south end (~8–10 km from Rathibele via that road).
- Rathibele ("Ratibalei"): lake/locality on the Attibele–Sarjapur road, NOT indexed in OSM/Nominatim/DDG; approximate position 12.82N 77.78E (~4–5 km south of Sarjapur town). Only corroboration found: news footage of a truck falling into a lake on Attibele–Sarjapur Road.
