# BBMP Betterment / Improvement Charges — Legal Framework

**Context:** Betterment charges (also called improvement charges) are statutory levies imposed by municipal authorities on properties that gain value due to public infrastructure improvements. Under BBMP (Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike), these are levied at building plan sanction and occupancy certificate stages.

## Governing Law

**Primary:** Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act, 1976 (KMC Act)

| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Section 202** | Power to levy betterment charge on lands/buildings benefited by any public improvement scheme |
| **Section 203** | Calculation — based on increase in market value from the improvement |
| **Section 204** | Notice and hearing procedure before imposition |
| **Section 205** | Appeals against assessment |
| **Section 206-210** | Collection and recovery mechanisms |

**Full Act:** Available at Karnataka DPal — search "Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act 1976"

## When Betterment Charges Apply (Two Stages)

### Stage 1: Plan Sanction / Building Permit (Commencement Certificate)
Levy includes:
- **Plan Sanction Fee** — % of estimated building cost
- **Betterment / Improvement Charge** — per sq.ft of built-up area (varies by zone)
- **Scrutiny Fee** — plan examination
- **License Fee** — building license
- **Labour Cess** — 1% of construction cost (Building and Other Construction Workers Act)
- **Parking Fee / Signature Charges**

### Stage 2: Occupancy Certificate (OC)
Before OC issuance, BBMP reconciles:
- Final betterment charge based on actual built-up area vs. sanctioned area
- Deviation penalty if actual > sanctioned
- Any shortfall in earlier fee payments

## Key Distinction: BBMP vs BDA Betterment

| | BBMP | BDA |
|---|---|---|
| **Governing Law** | KMC Act 1976 | BDA Act 1976 |
| **Applied when** | Building plan sanction / OC | Layout formation / land allotment |
| **Purpose** | Infrastructure within city limits | Development of new layouts, parks, roads |
| **Calculation** | Per sq.ft built-up area | Per sq.mt of land area |

Both can apply to the same property — BDA charge at layout stage, BBMP charge at construction stage.

## Rate Framework (Indicative — varies by BBMP circular)

Betterment charges vary by:
- **Zone** (A, B, C — property value zones)
- **Usage** (residential, commercial, industrial)
- **Built-up area / FAR**

Typical fee heads (approximate, subject to revision):
| Fee Head | Indicative Rate |
|---|---|
| Plan Sanction Fee | ~₹5-15/sq.ft |
| Betterment/Improvement Charge | ~₹20-50/sq.ft (zone-dependent) |
| License Fee | ~₹10-20/sq.ft |
| Scrutiny Fee | Fixed + per sq.ft |
| Labour Cess | 1% of estimated cost |
