# Indian Passport & ID Procedure Research

Research workflow for Indian passport name change/rectification, document mismatch resolution, and government ID procedure navigation. Covers the distinction between "name change" (newspaper + gazette required) vs "change in existing personal particulars" (simpler, document-specific) vs "deletion of spouse name" (death certificate only).

## Key Distinctions

### "Change in Existing Personal Particulars" (Simpler)

Per the Passport Seva portal, select "Re-issue of Passport" → "Change in Existing Personal Particulars" → "Appearance/Name" or "Spouse Name."

**Does NOT require newspaper publication or gazette notification:**
- Spelling correction (phonetic changes only — e.g. D'Souza → De Souza)
- Variation of name as in birth certificate (e.g. Sanjiv → Sanjeev)
- Shifting/changing arrangement of name parts (without deleting/inserting words)
- Splitting given name into first name + surname when surname field is blank
- **Deletion of spouse name** (due to divorce or death of spouse) — Death Certificate only
- **Addition of spouse name** — Marriage Certificate or Annexure J only

### "Full Name Change" (Requires Newspaper + Gazette)

Per the VFS Name Change Affidavit (Section 2): "Gazette notification of the state government concerned or clippings of two newspapers."

**Required for:**
- Complete change of name (new first name, new surname)
- Married women reverting to maiden name when NOT divorced/widowed
- Change of name upon attaining adulthood
- Minor change in name (adding/removing words from the name field)
- Change/addition of surname

### "Name Mismatch / Same Person" (Alternative to Full Name Change)

When the passport name differs from Aadhar/PAN/Voter ID but the person is the same:
- A **"One and the Same Person" affidavit** (notarized on stamp paper) can bridge the gap
- Explains that the variation is due to marriage, naming conventions, or administrative variance
- Often accepted when the PSK would otherwise demand newspaper publication
- Not a substitute for gazette notification when the passport officer insists

## Senior Citizen Specifics

- **Walk-ins**: Generally NOT allowed at PSKs. A prior online appointment is required.
- **Exceptions**: At the PSK in-charge's discretion for emergency/medical cases. Senior citizens (60+) may get priority queue once inside, but still need a booked appointment.
- **Best approach**: Book a regular appointment online, then request to speak to the RPO/senior officer in person at the PSK.

## PSK vs RPO Authority

| Level | Authority | Can Override? |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| PSK Counter Officer | Applies standard rules, often errs on side of caution | Base level |
| PSK Supervisor / Senior Officer | Moderate discretion | Yes, can override counter officer |
| Regional Passport Officer (RPO) | Full authority over passport issuance in their region | Yes, can override all lower levels |

If a PSK counter officer insists on newspaper + gazette for a situation that should be a simple "change in personal particulars," **ask to speak to the RPO or senior officer**. RPOs have the authority to apply the rules correctly.

> (i) Spelling correction/change of spelling which phonetically does not result in change in name
> (ii) Variation of a name as mentioned in the birth certificate

> **1. Following marriage, re-marriage or divorce… no proof of marriage/dissolution… would be required in the following cases:**
> (c) Divorcees applying for change of name OR for deletion of spouse's name in existing passport

## Common Scenarios & Recommended Approach

### Scenario: Widow — Passport has husband's name in name field, Aadhar/PAN/Voter ID don't

**Example**: Passport = "Kanta Dinesh Ranka" (with "Dinesh" as middle name/husband's name, spouse entry = "Dinesh Devraj Ranka"), New documents = "Kanta Ranka"

**Recommended approach**:
1. Apply for re-issue under "Change in Existing Personal Particulars" → select "Spouse Name" change
2. Submit Death Certificate of late husband as supporting document
3. Carry all "Kanta Ranka" documents (Aadhar, PAN, Voter ID) as proof of consistent name
4. If PSK officer insists on newspaper publication, ask to speak to RPO/senior officer
5. If RPO also insists, use the **two newspaper ads + affidavit** route (simpler than gazette)
6. **Last resort**: Gazette notification (4-6 weeks, ₹2,000-3,000)

### Scenario: Name mismatch across documents (Aadhar vs PAN vs Passport)

Fix Aadhar FIRST (easiest to correct), then use the corrected Aadhar to update Passport. Attempting both simultaneously creates a document loop.
