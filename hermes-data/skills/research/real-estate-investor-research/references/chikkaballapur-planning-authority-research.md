# Chikkaballapur Planning Authority — Master Plan & Regulations

## Researched: June 2026 — Yaluvahalli Village, Nandi Hobli, Chikkaballapur Taluk & District

## Planning Authority

| Authority | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Primary planning body** | **Chikkaballapur City Municipal Council (CMC)** — a City Municipal Council under Karnataka's Directorate of Municipal Administration (DMA), **not** a City Corporation |
| **Urban Development Authority** | Chikkaballapur Urban Development Authority (UDA) — site unresponsive |
| **Rural planning** | **ADTCP (Assistant Director, Town & Country Planning, Chikkaballapur)** under DTCP Karnataka — for revenue villages outside CMC limits |
| **DTCP Karnataka** | dtcp.karnataka.gov.in |
| **CMC Contact** | 08156-275557 / 274742 / 270450 |

**Important:** For land in revenue villages (outside municipal limits), the planning authority is the Sub-divisional/Assistant Director of Town & Country Planning (ADTCP) under DTCP, Government of Karnataka — NOT the CMC.

## BDA RMP 2031 — Does NOT Directly Apply

Yaluvahalli (Chikkaballapur District) is **outside the BDA (Bangalore Development Authority) area**. The BDA Revised Master Plan 2031 (RMP 2031) covers only the **Bangalore Metropolitan Area (BMA)**. Chikkaballapur has its **own Master Plan** under the Karnataka Town & Country Planning Act, 1961.

## Applicable Building Bye-Laws

1. **Karnataka Municipal Corporations (Model) Building Bye-laws, 2017** — primary document for CMC areas
2. **Karnataka Town and Country Planning Act, 1961** — planning permissions and layout approvals
3. **Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act, 1976** — building permit procedures
4. **Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964** — land conversion matters

## Height Restriction — Airport Funnel Zone (⚠️ CRITICAL)

**KIA Airport Funnel Zone applies regardless of planning authority.** Yaluvahalli is ~40-50 km from KIA, but the approach path extends northward.

- Max permissible height: **~15m to 30m AGL** depending on exact location relative to runway approach paths
- **Must obtain an AAI NOC** (No-Objection Certificate from Airports Authority of India) before any construction
- This is a binding limitation that caps development potential

## Development Process

```
1. Sec 95 Conversion → DC Chikkaballapur (Ag→Non-Ag under KLR Act)
2. CLU Approval → ADTCP Chikkaballapur / CMC (Change of Land Use)
3. Layout Plan Approval → ADTCP / DTCP
4. Building Permit → CMC or ADTCP
5. AAI NOC → Height clearance from Airports Authority of India
```

## Recommended Next Steps

1. **Visit ADTCP / CMC Chikkaballapur office** — ask for: (a) Master Plan zone for the specific survey number, (b) Applicable FAR for that zone, (c) Layout approval requirements
2. **Obtain RTC/Pahani** from Tahsildar for the survey numbers — confirms current land use, cultivation, ownership
3. **Contact AAI** for height NOC (call KIA engineering dept or AAI Bengaluru regional office)
4. **Engage a local architect/planner** familiar with Chikkaballapur CMC processes
