## RANKA OASIS COMPETITIVE POSITIONING

- **Selling Price:** ₹12,000/sq.ft (built-up) — matches Bangalore premium segment
- **Yield:** 63% vs corridor average 25-35% — nearly double
- **Land cost advantage:** Krishnagiri (TN) side vs Bangalore side
- **100% DRA owned:** No JV/landowner share — full control
- **DTCP approved:** Clear titles, no legal encumbrances
- **Upcoming catalysts:** SWIFT CITY (3-5km), Attibele Metro (1-2km), Prestige Forum Mall (8-12km)
- **Operational infra:** Golf resort, university, 6 schools, 3 hospitals within 3 km
