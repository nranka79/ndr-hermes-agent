## Legal shortcut (KCS Act 1959, text from mysocietyclub mirror)

- s.16(1) membership open to individuals (a), other co-ops (b), govt (c), approved institutions (d), **firms/companies/body corporates (e)**, market committees (f), local authorities (g), SHGs (h); subject to by-laws per s.16(2).
- s.17(1)(c) disqualifies anyone carrying on the same kind of business as the society → developer company risks disqualification from full membership.
- s.18 nominal/associate membership for companies — nominal has no share in assets/profits; no office-bearing.
- s.23 transfers valid only ≥1 yr holding + to a member + committee approval.
- **Conclusion:** bulk buy via direct conveyance (registered sale deeds to an SPV), not via membership.

## Pricing sanity numbers

- ₹999/sqft × 43,560 × 0.60 (saleable ratio) ≈ **₹2.6 Cr/ac gross**; ₹1,080 → ≈₹2.8 Cr/ac gross. Per saleable sqft: ₹4.35–4.70 Cr/ac of plots.
- KIADB comp ₹2.70 Cr/ac (Jun 2026, The Hindu); raw floor ₹1.7–4 Cr/ac; Nandi Hills Rd frontage ₹5.5–8.5 Cr/ac.
- Guidance (findcirclerate, Devanahalli Airport Area 2026-27): residential plot ₹45,208/sq.m ≈ ₹4,200/sqft.
- Villa comps at Karahalli/Kundana (MagicBricks snippets): Prestige Sanctuary ₹9.08–15.2 Cr; Prestige Golfshire ₹20.53–33.85 Cr (4 BHK).

## Commercial model (240,000 sqft = 200 × 30×40 plots ≈ 5.51 ac)

- SBI Mid Town: land ₹23.98 Cr + redev/soft ₹5.5 Cr + interest ~₹6.0 Cr = ₹35.4 Cr → **breakeven exit ≈ ₹1,475/sqft**.
- Nandi Greens: land ₹25.92 Cr + redev/soft ₹7.0 Cr + interest ~₹6.5 Cr = ₹39.4 Cr → **breakeven exit ≈ ₹1,642/sqft**.
- Exits: ₹1,800 → 1.25x; ₹2,500 → ~1.7x (net ₹20.6–24.6 Cr); ₹4,000 (SBI, optimistic) → 2.7x; villa path 85×1,800 sft @ ₹9,000 → 1.9x; @ ₹12,000 → 3.2x.
- Supply-cap risk: promoter still selling identical plots at ₹999–1,080 through same network → caps re-price.
