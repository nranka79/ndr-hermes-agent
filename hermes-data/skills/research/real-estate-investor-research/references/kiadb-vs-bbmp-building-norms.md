# KIADB vs BBMP Building Norms — Bangalore Commercial Development

**Verified June 2026** for Garudacharpalya, Whitefield (KIADB land)

## 🔴 Critical Distinction

KIADB-developed land follows **different building norms** from BBMP-ruled land. Plan sanction goes through **KIADB Chief Architect** (single-window, faster), NOT BBMP.

## Parameter Comparison

| Parameter | KIADB | BBMP |
|-----------|-------|------|
| **Governing Bye-laws** | KIADB Building Bye-laws 1985 (Amended 2019) | BBMP Building Bye-laws 2019 (Amendment 2022) |
| **Plan Sanction Authority** | KIADB Chief Architect / Planning Cell | BBMP Town Planning (respective zone) |
| **Process** | Single-window, generally faster, less bureaucratic | Multi-clearance, often slower |
| **Base FAR** (Commercial) | **2.00** | 2.00–3.25 (zone-dependent) |
| **Premium FAR Available** | **+1.00** (capped at 50% of base — KIADB/TP/PLN/47/2022-23) | Varies (TOD bonus + premium FAR) |
| **Max Achievable FAR** | **3.00** | 3.50–4.50 (with TOD) |
| **TOD Policy Applicability** | ❌ **NOT applicable** (KIADB is under Commerce & Industries Dept) | ✅ Applicable (GoK UDD 179/2018) |
| **Max Height** | **30–33m** (G+8/G+9) | 33–40m |
| **Ground Coverage** | **40%** | 40–45% |
| **Front Setback** | 8m (road <30m) / 12m (road >30m) | 12m typical |
| **Side Setback** (building >15m) | **6m** | 9m |
| **Rear Setback** (building >15m) | **6m** | 9m |
| **Premium FAR Fee** | KIADB fixed rates (often lower) | BBMP variable rates |
| **Property Tax** | TUFS / KIADB | BBMP |

## Key Rules

1. **Front setback does NOT reduce FAR** — FAR is a direct multiplier on plot area. The setback determines where the building sits, not how large it can be in total.
2. **8–12m front setback is fully compliant** — 8m minimum for roads <30m width, 12m minimum for roads >30m. Whitefield Main Road is >30m, so 12m recommended.
3. **Metro buffer:** 10–15m no-construction zone from nearest metro pillar — deductable from net plot area if within this zone.
4. **TOD policy does NOT apply to KIADB land** — GoK UDD 179/2018 covers BBMP/BDA areas only. KIADB is under the Commerce & Industries Department with its own bye-laws. **Correction source: KIADB Circular KIADB/TP/PLN/47/2022-23.**

## Approvals Required

| Clearance | Authority | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Plan Sanction | KIADB Chief Architect | Primary approval — NOT BBMP |
| Fire NOC | Karnataka Fire & Emergency Services | Required for height >15m |
| Environmental | KSPCB | CTE/CTO if >20,000 sqm built-up |
| Premium FAR Fee | KIADB | Payable to KIADB at fixed rates (not BDA/BBMP) |
| Rainwater Harvesting | KIADB | Mandatory recharge structures |
