# KIADB Building Norms — Commercial Development (Bangalore)

## Land Type Identification

Not all Bangalore land is BBMP/BDA land. **Always verify land type** before doing any FAR/building research.

| Land Type | Characteristics | Approving Authority |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------|
| **KIADB** (Karnataka Industrial Areas Development Board) | Industrial area development — Whitefield, Peenya, Electronic City, etc. | KIADB Chief Architect |
| **BBMP** (Municipal corporation) | Standard city land | BBMP Town Planning |
| **BDA** (Bangalore Development Authority) | BDA layout land | BDA |

**If unsure, ask the user:** "Is this KIADB land or BBMP land?" Many Whitefield properties (especially near IT corridors and industrial areas) are KIADB land.

## 🔴 BREAKING: KIADB Norms Overhaul — February 2026

**Government Order CI 99 SPQ 2025, dated 06-02-2026**

The Karnataka government introduced sweeping relaxations to KIADB building norms in February 2026, allowing FAR up to 5.2 on wider roads. This supersedes earlier KIADB Circular KIADB/TP/PLN/47/2022-23 norms.

### FAR Based on Road Width (New, Feb 2026)

| Road Width | Max FAR (including premium FAR) |
|---|---|
| **> 30 metres** | **Up to 5.2** |
| 24 m – 30 m | Up to 4.8 |
| 18 m – 24 m | Up to 4.0 |
| 12 m – 18 m | Up to 3.6 |
| < 12 m | 2.45 – 2.8 |

### Other Key Changes (Feb 2026)

| Parameter | New Norm (Feb 2026) | Old Norm (Pre-2026) |
|---|---|---|
| **Base FAR** | 3.25 (on roads >30m) | 2.0 |
| **Premium FAR** | Up to 5.2 with premium purchase | Capped at 3.0 |
| **Ground Coverage** | Up to **75%** | 40% |
| **Setbacks** | Reduced for smaller plots & buildings <7m height | Standard 6-12m |
| **Stilt Parking** | **Exempted** from FAR calculation | Counted in FAR |
| **Mechanical Parking** | **Exempted** from FAR calculation | Counted in FAR |
| **Additional FAR** | Up to **25% extra** where full FAR can't be achieved due to setbacks | Not available |
| **Premium FAR Cost** | ~50% of allotment value | ~50% of allotment value |
| **Height** | Effectively increased to utilize full FAR. G+10+ possible. | 30-33m (G+8/G+9) |

### TOD Policy Still NOT Applicable to KIADB Land

Even with the Feb 2026 FAR overhaul, KIADB land remains under the **Commerce & Industries Department**. The GoK Transit Oriented Development policy (UDD 179 BDA 2017) still **does NOT apply** to KIADB land. The new FAR comes directly from KIADB's own revised byelaws, not from UDD/TOD rules.

**When using these norms:** The FAR boost comes from KIADB's own new byelaws. Do NOT reference TOD policy. Simply state: "Per KIADB Government Order CI 99 SPQ 2025 dated 06-02-2026, the applicable FAR on [road width] roads is up to [X]."

## KIADB vs BBMP — Comparison (Post-Feb 2026)

| Parameter | KIADB (NEW — Feb 2026) | BBMP |
|---|---|---|
| **Max FAR** | **Up to 5.2** (on >30m roads) | 3.50–4.50 (with TOD) |
| **TOD Policy** | ❌ NOT applicable | ✅ Applicable |
| **Plan Sanction** | KIADB Chief Architect | BBMP Town Planning |
| **Ground Coverage** | Up to 75% | 40-45% |
