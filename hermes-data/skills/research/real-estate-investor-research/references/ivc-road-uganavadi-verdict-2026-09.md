- Plot mix: 1,500 / 2,400 / 2,920 / 4,930 sft tiers; 12 themed parks,
  2 CA sites, 1 commercial parcel; 12 m internal roads; handover 31-Mar-2027.

## Leg 5 — structure test

Outright @ ₹8.75 = **₹58.4 Cr cash**. JD at ₹1 Cr/acre goodwill + ₹1 Cr/acre
advance on 7 ac = **~₹14 Cr** (₹13.3 Cr in the final wording). Same land, same
exit, **~₹45 Cr less capital at risk.**

## Ruling delivered

**Worth pursuing after negotiation — not at the ask.**

1. Preferred: **JD at 60:40–62:38**, goodwill ₹1–1.5 Cr/ac + advance
   ₹1–2 Cr/ac → ₹14–21 Cr deployed instead of ₹58.4 Cr.
2. If outright is mandatory: **cap ₹7.25–7.50 Cr/acre** (~17% off ask) to
   restore 28–30% margin at a realistic ₹10,000/sft. Anchors to quote: the
   ₹8.50 Cr/ac 22-acre broker ask, the ₹4.50 Cr/ac 1acre.in median.
3. **Register-then-pay.** Price only the 4A registered as Phase 1; balance ~3A
   priced and paid only on per-sy.no registration.

**Skip if:** landowner won't move below ₹8.5 Cr/ac outright AND won't entertain
a JD — at that point it is a ₹58 Cr bet at ~22% land-to-revenue with Prestige,
Sobha and Provident setting the price ceiling above us.

**Pre-commitment items:** advocate opinion currently third-hand (via Bharat
Babu) — get it in writing; B-Kharab 13.00G physically verified (it sets
saleable area and the entire model moves with it).

## Method notes carried into the skill

- The headline can be "ask is 15% BELOW what a tier-1 developer paid" and the
  verdict still a negotiate/walk, because the end product can't clear margin.
  Both statements belong in the answer; the second decides.
- `web_extract` is NOT available in this environment — use `browser_navigate`
  then `browser_console` innerText slicing for portal pages. 99acres and
  MagicBricks locality pages both rendered fine via the local stealth browser.
- 99acres `div[class*="tuple"]` gives duplicate/partial cards; the reliable
  extraction is innerText regex on `₹([\d,]+)\s*per sqft` with a
  preceding-context slice for the project name.
- 1acre.in pagination via `?page=N` did NOT change content — all 20 lands came
  from page 1 (the pager is JS/client-side). Don't burn turns on page params;
  read page 1 fully.
