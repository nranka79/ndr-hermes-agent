# KIADB Feb 2026 Norms Overhaul — Research Notes

## What Changed

Government Order **CI 99 SPQ 2025**, issued **06 February 2026**, completely overhauled KIADB building norms:

| Old (Pre-2026) | New (Feb 2026) |
|---|---|
| Max FAR: 3.0 | Max FAR: **5.2** (on >30m roads) |
| Ground coverage: 40% | Ground coverage: **75%** |
| Stilt parking counted in FAR | Stilt parking **exempted** from FAR |
| Mechanical parking counted | Mechanical parking **exempted** |

## Why This Matters for DRAAS

- **Devasandra Industrial Area** (62,392 sqft, next to Garudacharpalya Metro, Whitefield Main Road >30m) can now achieve **FAR of 4.0–5.2**
- Built-up potential: **2.5 to 3.2 lakh sqft** (vs the ~36K sqft I incorrectly used before correcting)
- Revenue potential (@ ₹8,500/sqft): **~₹130-160 Cr**

## TOD Policy Still Excluded

Even with the new FAR, KIADB land is under **Commerce & Industries Department**, not UDD. The Transit Oriented Development (TOD) policy still does NOT apply. The new FAR comes from KIADB's own revised byelaws.
