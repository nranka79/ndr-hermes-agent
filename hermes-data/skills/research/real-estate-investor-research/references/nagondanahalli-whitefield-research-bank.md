## INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS

### 1. Namma Metro Purple Line — Fully Operational (March 2026)
- **Length:** 43.49 km, 38 stations
- **Route:** Challaghatta ↔ Whitefield
- **Status:** Fully operational since March 2026
- **Key Station:** Whitefield Station (~5 km from subject land)
- **Price Impact:** 156.5% plot price growth in Whitefield over 5 years; full-line opening accelerates repricing of peripheral sub-markets including Nagondanahalli
- **Source:** bengalurumetro.in, karnataka.com (Mar 2026)

### 2. Namma Metro Yellow Line — Partial Open (August 2025)
- **Length:** 18.82 km, 16 stations
- **Route:** RV Road ↔ Bommasandra (Electronic City)
- **Status:** Partially open since August 2025
- **Interchange:** Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road (Purple ↔ Yellow)
- **Benefit:** Connects Electronic City IT hub to citywide metro network
- **Source:** Bangalore Metro official, news reports Aug 2025

### 3. NH448 / Bellary Road — Airport Connectivity
- **Role:** Signal-free access from Whitefield to BLR Airport
- **Status:** Operational
- **Note:** Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR) Phase 2 extends orbital connectivity east — Whitefield is primary gateway for airport access from East Bangalore

### 4. BBMP Whitefield Zone Infrastructure Upgrades (2025–2027)
- Storm water drain upgrades on Immadihalli Main Road
- Road widening on Varthur Main Road
- New signal-free junctions
- Whitefield is a civic priority zone for BBMP's 2025–2027 infrastructure push

### 5. BWSSB Water & Sewage — 24x7 Supply Expansion
- Bangalore Water Supply & Sewerage Board expanding 24x7 water supply to Whitefield and Varthur zone
- New trunk sewer lines and STP capacity additions

### 6. BESCOM Power Upgrades
- 33kV feeder capacity and transformer upgrades in Varthur and Whitefield
- New HT cable routes along Immadihalli Main Road
- Direct benefit to Nagondanahalli plots with improved power reliability

## INVESTOR THESIS SUMMARY (Subject Property)

**Entry pricing:** Below prevailing Nagondanahalli market (₹6,300–₹8,500/sqft range)
**Exit target:** ₹7,500–₹9,500/sqft in 12–18 months

### Price Drivers
| Driver | Status | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Purple Line Metro fully operational | Live since March 2026 | +15–25% repricing in 12 months |
| Whitefield supply scarcity | Zero new plot land available | Structural floor on pricing |
| 100,000+ white-collar jobs | Live today — ITPB, ITPL, Bagmane | Guaranteed demand floor |
| BBMP infrastructure upgrades | Active 2025–2027 | Improved liveability |
| 3–4% rental yield | Above Bangalore average (1.5%) | Income floor for investors |
| Branded layout premium | DTCP/BBMP approval vs unbranded | +₹1,500–₹3,000/sqft premium |

### Buyer Profile
- IT professionals at ITPB/ITPL earning ₹15–80 LPA
- NRIs seeking Bangalore address with clear title
- Young families upgrading from apartments to plot + villa
- Investors attracted by 3–4% rental yield + 156.5% historical appreciation

### Risks
- Macro slowdown (IT layoffs) — mitigated by diversified employment base (400+ companies)
- DTCP approval delay — mitigated by established consultant relationships
- Title complexity in Varthur Hobli — mitigated by 6-level title check
- Metro-induced supply response — mitigated by first-mover layout approval
