# SARFAESI / Distressed Auction Due Diligence

Workflow for evaluating bank/ARC auction assets (teaser memorandum → bid decision). Tested on Whitefield Towers, Kannamangala (ANA ARC, 2025–26).

## 1. Identify the actual asset behind the teaser
- Teaser memos (broker OM/PDF) often carry stale or rounded facts. Match the asset by numbers: saleable area, project name, land survey nos.
- Search `"SARFAESI auction <project name>"` and check auction aggregators: findauction.in (has full auction-history tables), auctiondunia.com, anaarc.com (download the statutory notice PDF), HUDCO/Deutsche Bank etc. e-auction pages.
- Extract from the statutory notice: borrower, charge holder (ARC + trustee = first pari passu), land survey nos + village/hobli/taluk, area (mortgagor's share vs total project), possession status, reserve, EMD, bid increment, tender no.

## 2. Read the auction history — it tells you the seller's position
- Build the reserve-price timeline across rounds. Falling reserve = motivated seller; possession upgrade (symbolic → physical) = materially better terms.
- Multiple failed rounds (5+ on Whitefield Towers: ₹148.3→₹138→₹132 Cr) = thin bidder depth → opportunity, but also signals why (undivided share, completion risk, illiquid mix).

## 3. Verify teaser claims before believing the "discount"
- The "market is ₹X, we sell at ₹Y" claim usually quotes the TOP band (stabilized, fully-leased institutional Grade A). A stalled, un-leased shell with hotel/museum components will NOT achieve that without full completion + leasing.
- Cross-check per micro-market bands (e.g. Whitefield: EPIP/ITPL ₹12–13k vs Main Rd ₹10–11.5k vs u/c corridors ₹9–10k; see whitefield-bangalore-market-data.md).
- Always compute the true implied rate: reserve ÷ saleable area (₹132 Cr ÷ 4,81,620 sq.ft = ₹2,741/sq.ft, not the memo's rounded "₹3,000").

## 4. Structural risks checklist (before EMD)
- **Undivided share**: "50% undivided share" sales mean a co-owner exists — identify them, their consent/position, and shared-infra funding (basements, MEP, lifts, statutory obligations).
- As-is-where-is, as-is-what-is basis; physical possession still needs on-ground verification (no tenants/encroachments, utilities intact).
- Completion % is self-reported — get a BOQ for the balance works; build ±40% contingency.
- Statutory stack: sanctioned plan, OC/CC history, fire NOC, deviation penalties, RERA status.
- Auction terms: EMD deadline, 1-hour bid window, payment schedule.

## 5. Economics template (all-in vs stabilized exit)
- All-in = auction consideration + completion (remaining % of built-up × ₹/sq.ft, prorated to your share) + stamp/registration ~7–8% + carrying cost 12–18 months.
- Exit per component (office/retail/hotel/amenities) at conservative AND upside ₹/sq.ft; blend; present honest multiple (Whitefield Towers base case was 1.7–2.6x, NOT the memo's 4–5x which assumed top-band pricing on hotel+museum too).
- Include operator-fit-out capex for hotel components (₹8–12k/sq.ft extra) and museum/amenity repurposing as optionality, not underwriting.
