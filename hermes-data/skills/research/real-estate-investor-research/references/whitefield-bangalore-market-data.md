# Whitefield / Bangalore East — Verified Market Benchmarks (Jul 2026)

Condensed market data for Whitefield-area research. Every figure carries its source + period; re-verify before underwriting.

## Office market (Grade A, Whitefield / PBD East)
- Rent **₹65–89/sq.ft/mo**, +3% YoY — Knight Frank India H2 2025 business-district table (warm shell, ex-CAM).
- Whitefield share of city leasing ~15% (H2 2025), 2nd after ORR 44%. ORR rents ₹105–130; CBD ₹150–245; city average ~₹97.
- City vacancy: 7.8% (Cushman & Wakefield Q1 2026) to 12.8% (Knight Frank 2025); Whitefield vacancy declining; city rent growth +6.6% YoY (JLL Bengaluru Q4 2025).
- Capital values (Fulin Space, Jun 2025): EPIP/ITPL ready Grade A **₹12,000–13,000/sq.ft**; Whitefield Main Rd **₹10,000–11,500**; Bagalur Rd / under-construction ₹9,000–10,000; SJR iPark T5 pre-launch ₹9,500 → ₹11,500 expected; RMZ Infinity pre ₹10–11k → ₹13k ready; Pritech resale ₹11k.
- Rental yields: EPIP/ITPL 6.5–7% net; Whitefield Main Rd/Hoodi 7–7.5%; outskirts 8–8.5% projected.
- Project rents/occupancy: Sattva Knowledge Court (EPIP) ₹75–95, 1.3–1.6 msf; Brigade Tech Park ₹70 gross + CAM 12, ~95%; SJR iPark T4 ₹65, 90%; RMZ NXT ₹75, 95%; Prestige Tech Park ₹70, 98%; Embassy TechVillage Ph3 pre-lease ₹70; Brigade's new 11-ac park (2025) pre-leased 40% at ₹75 — proof of fresh demand on/adjacent to the corridor.

## Corridor / infrastructure facts (verified via Wikipedia, Jul 2026)
- Namma Metro Purple Line extension to Whitefield (Kadugodi) terminal opened 26 Mar 2023 — stations: KR Puram → Garudachar Palya → Hoodi → Seetharam Palya → Kundalahalli → Nallurhalli → Sadaramangala → Pattandur Agrahara → Whitefield (Kadugodi).
- Bengaluru–Chennai Expressway (NE-7): starts at Hoskote; Karnataka section (71 km) OPENED Dec 2024; full 258 km ~2027; ₹17,930 Cr.
- BSRP suburban rail (K-RIDE, 4 lines): openings slipped to ~2030 as of 2026 (was end-2026).
- Metro Phase 3 (Union Cabinet Aug 2024, ₹15,611 Cr): Kempapura–JP Nagar (ORR-West) + Hosahalli–Kadabagere — **NO Whitefield/Hoskote line**. Phase 3A Hebbal–Sarjapur (35 km) state-approved Nov 2024. Kadugodi–Hoskote extension = proposal only, no DPR — don't underwrite.
- Whitefield–Hoskote Road (SH 35) 4-lane widening: DPR stage, stalled on land acquisition (verify with KRIDL/PWD).

## Location pitfalls (verified the hard way)
- Assetz 63 East is on **Sarjapur Rd (Varthur Hobli)** — NOT Whitefield-Hoskote Rd.
- Prestige Lakeside Habitat is **Varthur** (~11 km from Kannamangala), NOT on SH 35 near Kadugodi.
- Sattva Knowledge City (BLR, Pattandur Agrahara, SH35) must not be confused with Sattva Knowledge Court (EPIP Zone) or the Chennai campus of the same name.
- Kannamangala (13.0275, 77.7614) is ~3.5 km NE of Whitefield (Kadugodi) metro on SH 35 — the area is semi-urban; the dense office/hospital/mall core of Whitefield is 5–8 km south.
