## INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS

### 1. Hosur Greenfield International Airport
- **Project:** Greenfield International Airport (proposed)
- **Capacity:** 3 crore (30 million) passengers/year
- **Investment:** ₹19,000 crore
- **Location:** Hosur–Berigai–Bagalur–Shoolagiri quadrilateral
- **Land:** ~2,979 acres across 12 villages; ~800 acres state-government-owned, ~2,134 acres private
- **Status:** Land acquisition in progress; TN government may begin formal acquisition within ~3 months (early 2026)
- **Price Impact:** Land in Berigai/Bagalur already up +25–40% in 12 months; now trading at ₹1,500–₹3,500/sqft
- **Source:** OneIndia, The Indian Express (Feb 2026), Project Today (Dec 2025)

### 2. Bangalore Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR)
- **Owner:** NHAI
- **Total Length:** 280.2 km
- **Completed:** Packages 1 & 2 (Dobaspete–Hoskote) — operational since March 2024; toll active
- **In Progress:** Hoskote–Tamil Nadu border (~102.6 km) — targeted mid-2026
- **Final Completion:** December 2025 (Financial Express)
- **Strategic:** Links Doddaballapur–Devanahalli–Hoskote–Shoolagiri into Bengaluru orbital network
- **Source:** OneIndia (April 2026), Financial Express, The Metro Rail Guy

### 3. Bommasandra–Hosur Metro Rail
- **Length:** ~20.5 km (11.7 km Karnataka + 8.8 km Tamil Nadu)
- **Implementing:** CMRL (Chennai Metro) + Karnataka counterpart
- **Status:** Feasibility study tender awarded; report expected shortly → DPR on approval
- **Significance:** South India's first inter-state metro
- **Source:** MoneyControl, The Hindu (June 2025), OneIndia, Metro Rail Today

### 4. Namma Metro Yellow Line
- **Status:** RV Road → Bommasandra operational (18.82 km, 16 stations)
- **Next:** Bommasandra → Silk Board (under construction)
- **Benefit:** Bengaluru metro reaches Electronic City/Bommasandra; connects to NH44 for Hosur commute

### 5. Road Widening / Other
- NH44: Periodic upgrades ongoing; lane additions in sections
- Railway: Bengaluru–Hosur section fully electrified
- Hosur City Ring Road: Proposed, planning stage
- **Source:** Multiple sources

## EMPLOYMENT GENERATORS (Major Employers)

### Tata Electronics — Hosur
- **Role:** Apple's largest contract manufacturer in India (by headcount)
- **Workforce:** ~75,000 (scaled from ~15,000 in two years)
- **Operations:** iPhone enclosure → Full iPhone assembly (started May 2025)
- **Facility:** 2 assembly lines running; expanding to 4; each line = 2,500+ workers
- **Location:** Kuthanpalli village, Krishnagiri district (Hosur Industrial Town)
- **Source:** India Today (April 2026), The Week (May 2026)

### Ola Electric — Krishnagiri Gigafactory
- **Capacity:** 6 GWh (India's only functioning gigafactory)
- **Milestone:** 1 millionth vehicle rolled out September 2025
- **Latest:** In talks to supply lithium-ion cells/battery packs to global and domestic automakers (May 2026)
- **Investment:** ₹2,000 crore board-approved capital infusion into battery/EV subsidiaries
- **Source:** ET Auto, Fortune India, Financial Express, NDTV Profit (May 2026)

### Foxconn — Devanahalli (Karnataka)
- **Investment:** ₹1,500 crore (₹1.5 billion) planned for Indian unit expansion
- **Devanahalli facility:** Ramping up; expected to regain top position in Apple supply chain headcount
- **Relevance:** ~60 km from Shoolagiri
- **Source:** Moneycontrol (April 2026)

### TVS Motor — Hosur
- **Hosur role:** Mainstay manufacturing plant; premium motorcycles + EV production
- **Karnataka MoU:** ₹2,000 crore (Feb 2025) — expansion, R&D, EV innovation
- **Capacity:** Adding 1.5 million units in next 12 months
- **Future:** New manufacturing facility; Hosur to play larger role in Norton motorcycles for India + global
- **Source:** ET Now, Autocar India, Bikewale, Motoroids

### DCX Systems — Hosur (Defense)
- **MoU:** Signed Sep 11–12, 2025 at TN Rising Investment Conclave, Hosur
- **Facility:** Radar manufacturing in Hosur
- **Significance:** Catering to Indian Armed Forces; positions Hosur in aerospace/defense corridor
- **DRDO AMCA:** ₹15,000 crore mega plan (3,000 acres) for Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft manufacturing also proposed near Hosur
- **Source:** Indian Defense News (Sep 2025), IDRW (Dec 2025)

### SIPCOT Industrial Area — Hosur
- **Organization:** State Industries Promotion Corporation of Tamil Nadu
- **Focus:** Automobiles, auto components, electronics, engineering goods, EV supply chain
- **Shoolagiri note:** Upcoming SIPCOT estate in Shoolagiri area — region projected as EV vehicle hub with ancillary units
- **Companies present:** TVS, Bosch, LEGO, Asian Paints, + dozens of SME units
- **Source:** Upkar Developers, AskLaila, digitalgriot.com

### Zetwerk — Electronics (Hosur)

- **Source:** NewsonProjects (Sep 2025)
