## PRODUCT IDENTITY

**What it is:** Plain, no-frills residential plot. Fully completed. RERA approved. Ready to register.
**What it is NOT:** Farm plot, villa, weekend home, guaranteed rental product.
**Developer support:** DRA provides design, approvals, contractor selection & monitoring, construction at ₹48L cap, and initial tenant sourcing.

**Investment model:** Buyer purchases plot → builds 8-unit rental building → earns ~₹80,000/month (8 × ₹10K/unit) = ~10% target yield.

**Why it exists:** Land economics make plot development in this corridor structurally impossible for other developers. Only 38 plots. Price makes sense because DRA acquired land before corridor prices exploded.

## CORE MESSAGING PRINCIPLES

### The Unfair Advantage Line
> *"The only raw plot investment inside Bangalore's highest-employment-density growth corridor — where land is structurally unavailable to anyone else, and your yield is backed by 2,000+ workers who have nowhere to live."*

Or compressed for ad copy:
> *"38 plots. Next to Electronic City. 10% yield. Done."*

---

### What NOT to Say

| Avoid | Use Instead |
|---|---|
| "Guaranteed yield" | "Target yield" / "potential yield" / "market-backed rental income" |
| "Farm plot" or "weekend home" | "Raw residential plot" / "investment plot" |
| "Luxury" or "premium" | "Plain" / "no-frills" / "investment-grade" |
| "Future appreciation" without grounding | Show approved infrastructure, construction timelines, demand drivers |
| "Best investment" | "Smart investment" / "compelling yield based on demand fundamentals" |

---

### Tone of Voice
- **Rational first, emotional second** — Run numbers before feelings
- **No hype, no exclamation marks** — Sophisticated audience; calm confidence beats enthusiasm
- **Direct and honest** — If there's a risk or caveat, say it. Honesty prevents refund requests
- **Local and grounded** — Not luxury marketing to HNIs. Smart investment for people who know the corridor. Ground everything in real geography: specific landmarks, actual distances, visible infrastructure
