## Layer 1: Imaging (detect the disease directly)

| Test | What It Detects | Emrani's Rationale | Indian Cost |
|------|----------------|--------------------|-------------|
| **CCTA with AI Plaque Analysis** | 3D coronary arteries; quantifies total plaque (soft + calcified). AI differentiates rupture-prone soft plaque from stable calcified. | Standard EKG/Echo/Stress Test miss ~75% of future heart attacks. CCTA finds non-obstructive plaque that causes most events. Medicare now reimburses AI-enhanced plaque analysis. | ₹8,000–15,000 |
| **CAC Score** | Coronary Artery Calcium — low-radiation CT, 5 min | "Most prognostic test in preventive cardiology." Reclassifies risk better than any traditional calculator. Zero score = very low 10-year risk even if LDL is high. | ₹2,000–3,500 |
| **Carotid US with Plaque Imaging** | Soft plaque in carotids (mirrors coronary plaque). No radiation. | Shows soft plaque early. Plaque in carotids ≈ plaque in coronaries. | ₹1,500–3,000 |
| **DEXA for Visceral Fat** | True body composition — visceral adiposity drives systemic inflammation | "Standard BMI is a lie." Visceral fat is an independent predictor of cardiac events. | ₹2,500–4,000 |

| 6 | **Lp(a)** | **<30 mg/dL** | <30 | 100% genetic, test once. 1 in 5 elevated. Triples heart attack risk independently. 2026 ACC/AHA recommends universal screening. |
| 7 | **Ferritin** | **50–150 ng/mL** | 30–400 | Iron storage — both low and high are pro-inflammatory |
| 8 | **B12** | **>500 pg/mL** | 197–771 | Methylation, homocysteine metabolism |

## How Emrani's Framework Differs from Standard Indian Cardiac Panels

| Aspect | Standard Indian Panel (e.g. Thyrocare Cardiac Profile) | Emrani Framework |
|--------|------------------------------------------------------|------------------|
| Insulin resistance | Fasting glucose only | **Fasting insulin** (<5 target) |
| Lipid assessment | Total Chol, LDL, HDL, TG, VLDL | + **ApoB, Lp(a), Trig/HDL ratio** |
| Inflammation | None or hs-CRP alone | hs-CRP + **Homocysteine + Lp-PLA2 (PLAC) + Fibrinogen** |
| Structure | None | **CAC + CCTA + Carotid US + DEXA** |
| Genetics | None | **ApoE4 genotyping** |

## Where This Framework Applies Strongest

- **Indian / South Asian populations**: Higher rates of premature CAD, higher Lp(a) levels, higher insulin resistance at lower BMIs, paradoxical "normal LDL but high risk" phenomenon → ApoB and fasting insulin are even MORE important
- **Family history of early heart disease**: Nishant has this + high Lp(a) → CCTA should be strongly considered despite CAC=0
- **Pre-diabetic (HbA1c 5.7%)**: Fasting insulin becomes the critical metric — tells us if metformin is sufficient or if insulin resistance is progressing
- **Already on statin + PCSK9i**: The remaining risk is Lp(a) driven + non-calcified plaque + metabolic → need CCTA and fasting insulin

## NURA Screening Blindspot Warning

NURA full-body packages (Jul 2024, Bangalore) include: BMI, BP, CBC, HbA1c, kidney panel, lipid profile (non-fasting), liver panel, H.Pylori, thyroid, urinalysis, plus CT-based screenings (CAC, visceral fat, lung, liver, kidney, pancreas, bone density, fatty liver, colorectal, prostate, oral) + DEXA, ECG, fundus photography, audiometry.

**They do NOT include:** Lp(a), ApoB, ApoA1, hs-CRP, fasting insulin, homocysteine, ferritin, magnesium, or any advanced cardiac risk markers. These require a separate Thyrocare Cardiac Risk Markers (₹1,200) or equivalent panel.

A patient who did NURA and got CAC=0 may still have severe soft plaque risk — especially with elevated Lp(a) or strong family history.
