## Supplement Safety Notes
- **NAC**: Generally safe up to 3g/day. May interact with nitrates (vasodilation additive). Can cause GI upset — start low (600mg), increase gradually.
- **Berberine**: Potentiates metformin — may need to reduce metformin dose. Can cause GI issues. Cycle 8 weeks on/1 week off.
- **Vitamin D3**: Above 10,000 IU/day requires monitoring of calcium/parathyroid. Test 25-OH D after 8 weeks.
- **Omega-3 high dose**: 4g/day EPA requires prescription (icosapent ethyl) or careful brand selection for purity. Look for brands with IFOS certification on Amazon India.
- **CoQ10/Ubiquinol**: Statins deplete CoQ10 — ubiquinol is the active, better absorbed form, especially >40 age.
- **Lp(a) is medication-stubborn**: Statins and PCSK9i (inclisiran) barely lower Lp(a). Evolocumab reduces it ~25-30%. Emerging therapies (pelacarsen, olpasiran) target 80-90% reduction but aren't in India yet. Lifestyle changes don't directly lower Lp(a) but reduce the INFLAMMATION it causes.

## Where to Find Specialised Biomarker Tests in Bangalore

**Source:** Dr. John Sekhar, Baylor College of Medicine (2019–2024 studies). Published in journals including *Nature* and *Nutrients*.

### Key Findings
- GlyNAC (Glycine + NAC) for 16 weeks in older adults restored **RBC glutathione to youthful ranges**
- Improved mitochondrial function (ATP production measured)
- Reduced oxidative stress (8-OHdG dropped ~50%)
- Improved endothelial function (FMD increased)
- Reversed 7 of 9 hallmarks of aging

### Protocol
| Component | Dose | Timing |
|-----------|------|--------|
| NAC | 100mg/kg body weight (~7–10g for most adults) | Divide into 2–3 doses/day |
| Glycine | 100mg/kg body weight (~7–10g) | Divide into 2–3 doses/day |

### What to Measure Before & After (12–16 weeks)
1. **RBC Glutathione (GSH)** — primary endpoint. Expect 40–80% increase.
2. **8-OHdG (urine)** — oxidative DNA damage. Expect 30–50% decrease.
3. **ATP** — mitochondrial function marker (harder to get in India — research labs only).
4. **FMD** — endothelial function, if accessible.

### Alternative: Liposomal Glutathione
If GlyNAC compliance is difficult (large powder volumes), liposomal glutathione is an alternative but expensive:
- **Available:** NutriJa Liposomal Glutathione ~₹1,500–2,000/mo
- **Evidence:** Less well-studied than GlyNAC; lower bioavailability claims
