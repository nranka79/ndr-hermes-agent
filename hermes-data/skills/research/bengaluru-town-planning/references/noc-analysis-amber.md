## NOC Analysis

### Condition #4 (Sanctioned Plan, Sheet X — Condition 4)
Text: *"Applicable development charges payable to B/s BESCOM, BWSSB shall be paid in NP和其它 / other (sic) concerned departments and obtain no objection certificate from them, submit copy to this office at the time applying for occupancy certificate / commencement of construction."*

**Interpretation**: This is about PAYMENT of development charges, not a pre-requisite NOC for plan sanction. BESCOM/BWSSB are mentioned as departments to pay charges TO, and the NOC is for occupancy certificate stage, not plan sanction.

### KSPCB / Pollution Control Board
**NOT mentioned anywhere** in the licence or any of the 50+ sanctioned plan conditions.

### AAI Height Clearance
**NOT mentioned** — building height at Ranka Amber is 14.99m (below standard trigger height).

### Fire Department NOC
Condition states: *"Periodic fire safety NOC every 2 years"* — this is a post-occupancy recurring requirement, not a pre-sanction NOC.

### Labour NOC (ONLY mandatory pre-sanction NOC)
Explicit condition: *"Obtaining NOC from the Labour Department is a must before commencement of construction."*

## Key Precedent
Since this licence was fully sanctioned by BBMP with zero queries about KSPCB/AAI/EIAA NOCs, it proves conclusively that these NOCs are NOT prerequisites for building plan sanction in GBA (Greater Bangalore Area). The only NOC condition that is a MUST before construction is the Labour Department NOC.

## STP Notification Overlay
GoK Notification FEE 43 EPC 2022 (12 March 2024):
- Projects with **120+ units** in sewered areas must have mandatory STP
- Projects below this threshold in sewered areas are exempt from KSPCB/STP requirements
- Ranka Amber = 20 units, Whitefield = sewered area → well below threshold
