# Ranka Amber Building Licence — NOC Analysis

**Licence:** BBMP/CC/4247/26-27 (GBA East City Corporation, 18 May 2026)
**Site:** Raghu Iyer & Farida Iyer / DRA Realty Pvt Ltd
**Location:** D'Silva Layout, Pattandur Agrahara, K.R. Puram Hobli, Bangalore East
**Project:** GBA/BECC/0540/25-26

## Project Metrics
- Site area: **1,300.58 sq.m** (~14,000 sqft / 0.32 acres)
- Built-up: **3,383.17 sq.m** (~36,400 sqft)
- Building: Stilt + Ground + 3 floors, 20 units, 12m height
- Use: Residential Apartment

## Conditions from Sanctioned Plan (full conditions list)
The sanctioned plan includes ~40 conditions covering:
- Building use restriction
- Car parking not to be converted
- Development charges to BESCOM/BWSSB "if any"
- Labour welfare compliance
- Worker insurance
- No debris on roads
- Tree planting (min 1 tree for site >2400 sqft, 2 for >4000 sqft)
- Rain water harvesting (mandatory for plots >240 sqm)
- Electric transformer (required if floor area >500 sqm)
- Fire safety measures for high-rise
- Lift requirements (for buildings >15m height)

## Key Takeaway
This licence proves that **BESCOM, BWSSB, and KSPCB NOCs are NOT pre-conditions for GBA plan sanction** even for a 36,400 sqft project. If this rule existed (e.g., a threshold exemption under 30,000 sqft or 14,000 sqft), it would govern Amber too — but no such condition exists in the licence.
