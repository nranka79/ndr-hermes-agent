# ZR 2015 — Basement Regulation Deep-Dive

Research date: 2026-07-17
Source documents: RMP 2015 ZR Vol. 3 (drive id `0B1Oc8cSaJXPGUlcxM05kTVpLR0U`), BBMP Byelaws 2003, 2026 Gazette amendment

## The Core Question

*"Is there any restriction on creating clubhouse / common amenity / indoor swimming pool in the basement of a residential apartment building in Bangalore?"*

## Answer Summary

**YES — there is a clear restriction.** Under ZR 2015 Section 3.9(v), the permissible uses for basements in buildings *other than 3-star+ hotels* are limited to parking, utility equipment, bank safes, and X-ray dark rooms. Clubhouses, common amenities, gyms, and swimming pools are **not allowed** in basements for residential apartment buildings.

Only **3-star+ category hotels** can use spare basement area for health clubs, gyms, banquet halls, swimming pools, etc. (Section 3.9(vi)).

However, if the structure projects **>1.2m above average ground level**, it may not legally be a "basement" at all — and the restrictions would not apply.

## Full Section 3.9 Text (ZR 2015)

```
3.9) Basement:
I.    Means storey which is partly or wholly below the average ground level and with a
      height not exceeding a projection of 1.2 m above the average ground level and overall
      height of the basement under any circumstances should not exceed 4.5 m between the
      floor and the ceiling of the basement in case of normal parking.

II.   If a site is measuring less than 200sqm, then car parking shall not be permitted in the
      basement floor.

III.  If the minimum setback is more than 2.0 m, then the basement may be extended on all
      sides except the side abutting the road, provided the minimum setback between the
      basement and property boundary is minimum 2.0 m.

IV.   Basement floors up to a maximum of 5 (five) levels may be permitted for car parking.

V.    Permissible uses in the Basement for buildings other than 3 star and above category:
      • Dark rooms for X ray and storage of light sensitive materials
      • Bank Safes / Strong room included in the FAR
      • Air condition handling units/equipment, utilities and services connected with the building.
      • Parking

VI.   In case of 3 star and above category of hotels, the spare area in the basement after
      catering to the requirement of parking facilities may be allowed to be used for other
      purposes incidental to the running of the hotel, such as; health club, shopping arcade,
      dining area, with or without kitchen facilities, with gas cylinder, administrative office,
      gym rooms, banquet/conferencing facility, swimming pool, discotheque etc. subject to
      reckoning of the same for FAR calculations.

VII.  Parking area if misused is liable to be municipalised / taken over by the local
      body/authority without any compensation.

VIII. Basement in a residential building shall be allowed without taking into FAR
      calculations subject to the condition that it will be used only for the purposes of Home
      Theater or Gym or a combination of both for personal use of the occupant on a site
      which does not exceed 500sqm and should be in Residential (Main) area as per Revised
      Master Plan 2015. In such cases, it should be single dwelling unit only and the entry to
      the basement shall be from inside the main building itself.

IX.   When Basement is used for Car parking, the convenient entry and exit shall be
      provided. Adequate drainage, ventilation, lighting arrangements shall be made to the
      satisfaction of the authority.
```

## 2026 Gazette Amendment (Draft — UDD 338 MNJ 2026)

Published 04 June 2026. Key changes to Section 3.9:

1. **After clause (iii):** Basement setback clarification — "Except on the side abutting the road it shall be minimum setback required for the building."
2. **Clause (x) substituted:** Car lifts allowed for plots up to 6,000 sqm instead of ramps. Above 6,000 sqm, one ramp of 6.0m clear width + car lift required. Number of car lifts based on parking count (2 lifts for 100–200 parking, +1 per additional 100).
3. **Setback area uses (Section 3.1):** UG sump, STP (only in basement floor/below ground), and **Swimming Pool** may be allowed in setback area after reserving space equal to required basement setback.

**IMPORTANT:** This is a DRAFT notification. It "proposes to make certain amendments" and invited objections within 30 days (deadline ~04 July 2026). Not yet in force unless confirmed by user.

## BBMP Building Byelaws 2003 — Section 18

```
18.1  Basement floors shall not be used for purposes other than parking and for locating
      machines used for service and utilities of buildings.

      a) Strong rooms only in case of bank buildings. The area to be used for this purpose
         shall be reckoned for FAR purposes.

      b) Installation of Radiology and X ray equipment only in case of Hospital buildings
         having a capacity of more than 50 beds without office. The area to be used for this
         purpose shall be reckoned for FAR purposes.
```

Section 18 also covers: height (2.4–2.75m), ventilation (mechanical if natural insufficient), fire safety, waterproofing, max 11.25m to exit, and max 2 basements (3 for 3-star+ hotels).

## Key Distinction: "Basement" vs "Floor Above Ground"

If the structure projects **>1.2m above average ground level**, it exceeds the ZR 2015's own definition of basement. In that case:
- The restrictive use list (V) does not apply
- The space is treated as a regular floor
- It counts towards FAR
- Normal floor-level regulations apply
- Common amenities, clubhouse, swimming pool ARE permissible
