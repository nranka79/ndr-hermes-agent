# Bangalore Metro Network Snapshot (verified Aug 2026)

Compiled from Wikipedia line pages (wikitext), Metro Rail Guy, Deccan Herald,
Times Now, MagicBricks News, The Hindu, and X posts (via syndication API).

## Operational
- **Purple Line**: Whitefield (Kadugodi) – Challaghatta (east–west).
- **Green Line**: Silk Institute – Nagasandra (north–south).
- **Yellow Line** (opened 11 Aug 2025): Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road – Bommasandra
  (Delta Electronics), 19.15 km, 16 stations, fully elevated. **This is the
  Electronic City line** — serves Electronic City Ph1/2, Infosys, Hosa Road,
  Huskur Road, Narayana Hospital, Delta Electronics Bommasandra via Hosur Road.
- **Pink Line partial** (Aug 2026): Kalena Agrahara – Tavarekere elevated
  stretch opening; full Pink (Kalena Agrahara–Nagawara, 21.4 km, 18 stn) under
  construction.

## Under Construction
- **Blue Line** (Phase 2A/2B): Central Silk Board – KR Pura – KIAL Terminals
  (Airport). Crosses Sarjapur Road corridor with stations at HSR Layout, Agara,
  Iblur, Bellandur area. Interchange with Red Line at Agara and Ibbalur.
- **Orange Line** = Phase 3 (Union Cabinet approved Aug 2024):
  - Corridor 1: JP Nagar 4th Phase – Kempapura, ~32.5 km, 21 stn, along ORR.
  - Corridor 2: Hosahalli – Kadabagere, ~12.5 km, 9 stn, Magadi Road.
  (Hindustan Times/others call the ORR piece "28.4 km"; official figure ~32.5 km.)

## Phase 3A — Red Line (Hebbal–Sarjapur) ← the Sarjapur-town line
- 36.59 km (22.14 elevated / 14.45 underground), **28 stations**, depot at
  Sarjapur. Cost ~₹28,405 cr (incl. land). DPR by RINA (2022–23).
- Status: **GoK Cabinet approved 6 Dec 2024; awaiting GoI approval** (as of
  Mar 2026 The Hindu: "awaits nod amid design objections" — NOT under
  construction despite Wikipedia infobox saying so). Construction expected
  2027–2033. Two double-decker viaduct segments planned: Sarjapur–Ibbalur
  (14 km) and Agara–Koramangala.
- Full station list (north→south), from Wikipedia wikitext + Metro Rail Guy:
  Hebbal (Blue/Orange), Ganganagar, Veterinary College, Mekhri Circle, Palace
  Guttahalli, Bengaluru Golf Course, Basaveshwara Circle, KR Circle (Purple
  interchange), Town Hall, Shantinagar (TTMC), NIMHANS, Dairy Circle (Pink
  interchange), Koramangala 2nd Block, Koramangala 3rd Block, Jakkasandra,
  Agara (Blue), Ibbalur (Blue), Bellandur Gate, Kaikondrahalli, Doddakannelli,
  Carmelaram (railway stn), Ambedkar Nagar, Kodathi Circle, Muthanallur Cross,
  Dommasandra, Sompura, Kada Agrahara Road, Sarjapur (+ Sarjapur Terminal in
  some lists = 29 points). Serves Sarjapur town from the NW via Sarjapur Road;
  does NOT reach Electronic City — change at Agara/Ibbalur → Blue → Silk Board →
  Yellow.

## Proposed — 73-km "longest line" (Kalena Agrahara – Kadugodi Tree Park)
**THE answer for Sarjapur–Attibele–Electronic City direction.**
- ~68 km main line + 5 km spur = 73 km, 50+ stations, fully elevated.
- Anchor route: Kalena Agrahara (Bannerghatta Rd, Pink/Green end) → Bannerghatta
  → Jigani → **Attibele** → **Sarjapur** → Dommasandra → Varthur Kodi → Kadugodi
  Tree Park (Whitefield). The Attibele→Sarjapur leg runs the Sarjapur–Attibele
  Road corridor (NDR's "Sarjapur Ratibalei road"). Attibele is ~7 km south of
  Electronic City on Hosur Road.
- Spur: branches after Jigani toward Anekal taluk — 2 stations at Indlavadi and
  the proposed international cricket stadium (Surya City Ph 4, Indlavadi;
  ~₹2,350 cr state cabinet approved complex).
- Status: feasibility study completed (Deccan Herald, Oct 2025; draft report by
  Introsoft Solutions); **DPR not sanctioned — GoK to decide**. Realistic
  post-2029/2030. Sometimes tagged "Phase 4" / "longest line".
- News coverage: Deccan Herald X post id 1981058413372055728; X post id
  2051697722705707072 (3 map images); MagicBricks News article 148650.

## Proposed — Bommasandra–Attibele extension (Yellow Line)
- 10.5 km, 9 stations: Chandapura, Yadavanahalli, Attibele Industrial Area,
  Attibele Terminal. Would extend Yellow Line beyond Electronic City to Attibele
  (southern end of Sarjapur–Attibele Rd). X post id 2087741380382928961 (map).

## Phase 4 feasibility (8 corridors, The Hindu)
Challaghatta–Bidadi (~15 km), KR Pura–Hoskote (~15 km), Madavara–Tumakuru
(~52 km), Kadabagere–Tavarekere (~6 km), Doddajala–Devanahalli (~6 km), plus
Harohalli and others. No dedicated Sarjapur–Electronic City line beyond the
73-km corridor.
