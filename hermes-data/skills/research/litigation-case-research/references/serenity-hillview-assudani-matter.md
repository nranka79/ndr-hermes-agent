## Legal Opinion 15.08.2026 (Adv. Vinod Kumar) — 5 questions
1. **Title** — GBJT has *prima facie* registered title; NO unqualified "clear & marketable title" opinion yet (minor vendors in 2024 Sale Deed need scrutiny; extent discrepancy: 6A-28G incl. A-Kharab vs checklist 6A-26G + 20G B-Kharab + 2G A-Kharab vs ~7A-7½/8G historical).
2. **Authority to sell** — NOT established (only s.92 leave order); insist on final decree.
3. **Reversion BIAAPA CLU/conversion → agricultural** — not established as legally/administratively available (S.95 Karnataka Land Revenue Act; S.14 KTCP Act).
4. **11E subdivision** for individual plots without residential layout approval — NOT lawful without BIAAPA compliance.
5. **Partnership → dissolution/reconstitution** — OK as acquisition vehicle, but does NOT avoid conveyance/planning/stamp duty (Karnataka Stamp Act Art. 40: 5% on reconstitution/dissolution).
- **Recommendation**: no unconditional purchase / substantial non-refundable payment until conditions precedent satisfied.
