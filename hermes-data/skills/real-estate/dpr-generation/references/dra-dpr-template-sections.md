# DRA DPR Master Template — 10 Sections (lender appraisal)

Source: user-provided template (Aug 2026) + DRA-branded build pattern. Each DPR doc shows: cover (DRA logo, gold rules, charcoal title, CONFIDENTIAL bar) → Contents → sections.

## Executive Summary
- Project Overview: title, location, type, developer profile.
- Key Financial Highlights: Total Project Cost, Debt-Equity Ratio, IRR, DSCR, NPV.
- Funding Requirement: loan amount, tenure, moratorium, security offered.

## 1. Developer & Promoter Profile
- 1.1 Background & Track Record — company history, completed projects, sqft delivered.
- 1.2 Legal Entity & Management Structure — constitution, directors/promoters, KMT, org structure.
- 1.3 Financial Soundness — promoter net worth, last 3 yrs audited financials, credit ratings, banking relations.

## 2. Project Description & Scope
- 2.1 Land & Location Details — address, plot area, connectivity, ownership status (freehold/leasehold, title cert), zoning, master-plan alignment, conversion status.
- 2.2 Project Specifications — BUA, saleable/carpet area, towers/floors, unit breakdown (2BHK/3BHK/commercial), amenities, parking, green standards (LEED/IGBC).

## 3. Regulatory Approvals & Statutory Clearances
Table: Approval | Status (Obtained/Applied/Pending) | Reference No.
Title Clearance Report · Sanctioned Building Plan / Master Layout Approval · Environment Clearance · RERA Registration · Fire NOC, Airport Authority NOC, PCB NOC · Utility commitments (water, electricity, sewage).

## 4. Technical & Engineering Aspects
- 4.1 Architectural & Engineering Design — structural standards, MEP planning.
- 4.2 Project Execution Strategy — EPC / item-rate / in-house.
- 4.3 Contractor & Consultant Details — architect, structural, MEP, main contractor profiles.
- 4.4 Project Timeline — Gantt/bar chart milestones (foundation, superstructure, finishing, handover).

## 5. Market Analysis & Commercial Viability
- 5.1 Industry & Micro-Market Overview — demand-supply, trends, absorption.
- 5.2 Competitor Analysis — benchmarking vs nearby projects (pricing/amenities/sales velocity). Fill from Drive R&D decks (property-pricing-sources/references/ranka-project-pricing-rnd-index.md); always add a "Positioning:" callout comparing own price vs band + evidence.
- 5.3 Marketing & Sales Strategy — target segment, pricing plan, pre-launch, channel partner strategy, sales schedule.

## 6. Project Cost & Means of Finance
- 6.1 Cost breakdown table: Land/Land Dev · Civil Construction · MEP & Infra · Architectural & Consultancy · Statutory & Approval · Marketing & Admin · IDC · Contingency · Total.
- 6.2 Means of Finance table: Promoter Equity · Bank Debt/Construction Finance · Customer Advances/Collections.

## 7. Financial Estimates & Projections
- 7.1 Revenue assumptions (price/unit/sqft, revenue realization by milestone).
- 7.2 Quarterly/annual cash flow statements.
- 7.3 Profitability statement (projected income statement).
- 7.4 Balance sheet projections through loan tenure.
- 7.5 Key metrics: DSCR (gross/avg), Project IRR & Equity IRR, break-even (% sales to service debt).

## 8. Risk Analysis & Mitigation Plan
Technical (delays, escalation, contractor default) · Commercial (absorption, price drop) · Regulatory (approval delays) · Mitigation (fixed-price contracts, escalation clauses, contingency buffers, escrow/TRA).

## 9. Security & Collateral Proposed
- 9.1 Primary: first mortgage on project land + all structures.
- 9.2 Hypothecation: receivables/sales proceeds/cash flows via Escrow/TRA.
- 9.3 Collateral: additional land/property/liquid security.
- 9.4 Guarantees: personal (promoters) / corporate (parent).

## 10. Annexures & Enclosures
1. Corporate docs (MoA/AoA/COI/Board resolutions) · 2. Financial records (3 yrs audited) · 3. Legal & land docs (title search, sale deeds, DAs, mutation) · 4. Sanction plans & maps · 5. Cost estimates (BOQ by CE/QS) · 6. Detailed cash-flow working files (Excel).

## Branding rules for every DRA doc
Cover: logo (DRA "HOME OF PRIDE"), gold #F7B519 divider rules, "D R A G R O U P" gold kicker, project name charcoal #231F20, "DETAILED PROJECT REPORT", meta lines (status/date/prepared by), charcoal CONFIDENTIAL bar. Headings charcoal with gold bottom rules; table headers charcoal bg white text; red-italic `[ to be completed ]` placeholders for unknowns.
