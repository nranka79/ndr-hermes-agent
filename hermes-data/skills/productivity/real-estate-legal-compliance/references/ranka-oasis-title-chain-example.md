## Recital drafting notes

When these chains were used in a sale deed for Plot 119 (from 158/1C9A):
- VENDOR = DRA Realty (owns 158/1C9A directly → Schedule B → Plot 119 as Schedule C)
- CONFIRMING PARTY = Sevaganapalli LP (owns other surveys, confirms sale)
- No formal JDA between VENDOR and CP — mutual agreement to develop jointly
- CONFIRMING PARTY confirms/ratifies the sale of Plot 119 to the Vendee
