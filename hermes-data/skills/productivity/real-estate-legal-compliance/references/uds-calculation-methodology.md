# UDS (Undivided Share of Land) Calculation Methodology

## What is UDS

UDS (Undivided Share of Land) is the proportionate ownership each apartment owner holds in the total land area of the project. Every unit must have its UDS clearly defined for Agreements of Sale, allotment letters, Kelsa inventory, and sale deeds.

## Calculation Formula

```
UDS per sqft of Super Built-up Area = Total Plot Area ÷ Total Super Built-up Area of all units

Each unit's UDS = Unit's Super Built-up Area × UDS per sqft
```

### Example (Ranka Amber)
- Total plot area as per sanctioned plan: **14,000 sqft**
- Sum of all Super Built-up areas in the sheet: **~31,853 sqft**
- UDS per sqft of Super Built-up area = 14,000 ÷ 31,853 = **0.4395 sqft per sqft of SBUA**

## Land Area Determination — "Lesser of Two" Rule

The total land area to be used for UDS calculation follows a specific hierarchy:

1. **Default:** Use the land area as shown in the **sanctioned plan**
2. **If actual surveyed area > sanctioned plan area:** Still use the **sanctioned plan area** (do not use the larger surveyed area)
3. **If actual surveyed area < sanctioned plan area:** Use the **actual surveyed area** (the lesser of the two)

This rule prevents over-allocating land that doesn't legally exist on paper, while not shortchanging owners by using a smaller-than-sanctioned area.

## UDS Is Fixed Across Versions

Two area statements exist for every project:
1. **Sanctioned plan-based** — used for agreements and legal documents
2. **Execution drawing-based** — actual built area (may be slightly larger due to permissible deviations)

**The UDS per unit MUST remain the same in both versions**, even though the execution built-up areas may be higher. Rationale:
- UDS is already allocated between landowner and developer (50:50 sharing)
- It does not change with permissible construction deviations
- When entering Agreements of Sale, reference the sanctioned plan area and note the permissible deviation — but UDS stays identical

## When UDS Is Needed

- **Agreements of Sale** — per-unit UDS must be stated
- **Allotment Letters** — UDS forms part of the unit particulars
- **Kelsa inventory / cost sheets** — UDS must be tracked per unit
- **Sale deeds** — UDS is a mandatory field
- **SIS Arera details sheet** (RERA registration) — Col J = Undivided Share of Land / site

## Setting Up in the Area Statement Sheet

When adding a UDS column to an area statement spreadsheet:

1. Add column header: **UDS (sqft)**
2. Calculate total plot area per the "lesser of two" rule
3. Sum all Super Built-up areas
4. Compute the UDS multiplier
5. Multiply each unit's Super Built-up Area by the multiplier
6. Cross-verify: sum of all UDS values should approximately equal the total plot area
7. The architect (edit access) should review and confirm the UDS allocation is architecturally consistent
