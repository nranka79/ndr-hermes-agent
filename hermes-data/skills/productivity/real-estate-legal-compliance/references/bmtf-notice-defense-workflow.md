# BMTF Police Notice — Defense & Neutralization Workflow

## Trigger
NDR receives a BMTF (Bengaluru Metropolitan Task Force) police notice for a DRAAS project — typically alleging kharab-land encroachment, buffer zone violations, or byelaw non-compliance. Almost always an extortion attempt.

## Step 1: Immediate Triage
- **Hearing date**: check if it's within 3-5 days (confirms extortion pattern — short notice, no proper documentation)
- **Notice defects** (check for ALL three — if any missing, notice is legally defective per Karnataka HC):
  1. FIR/Crime number must be mentioned
  2. Sections of law must be cited
  3. Copy of complaint/FIR must be attached
- **Property status**: does it have BBMP E-Khatha? Sanctioned plan? RTC showing zero kharab?

## Step 2: Upload to Drive

- Upload: original scan, English translation, proposed reply draft, strategy document

## Step 4: Legal Defenses to Assert
1. **No FIR/crime number** — per Karnataka HC July 2024 judgment on BNSS Section 35: "noticee is not obliged to appear, no coercive action for non-appearance"
2. **No sections of law cited** — BMTF can only act under specific Acts (not IPC)
3. **No complaint/FIR copy attached** — per same HC judgment, mandatory
4. **BBMP limits jurisdiction** — May 2026 Karnataka HC Division Bench: revenue authorities have no jurisdiction within BBMP/GBA limits
5. **Private property with sanctioned plan** — 2013 Karnataka HC: BMTF has no jurisdiction over private property with BBMP-approved plans
6. **RERA NOC demand is ultra vires** — BMTF has zero statutory authority under RERA Act

## Step 5: 4-Phase Strategy
1. **Phase 1 (Days 1-3)**: Do NOT appear. Send formal legal reply through counsel demanding complaint copy, statutory basis, 4-6 week extension.
2. **Phase 2 (Weeks 1-4)**: Administrative fortification — Sakala/RTI for conversion records, buffer-zone evidence, BBMP BECC comfort letter.
3. **Phase 3 (Weeks 5-6)**: Substantive submission — comprehensive written submission dismantling the petition.
4. **Phase 4 (Contingency)**: Writ Petition before Karnataka HC under Article 226 / Sec 482 CrPC for quashing.
