When NDR needs a GPA from a US-resident Indian national landowner (e.g. Kankshita Swaminathan,

## The 2026 rule change (why this exists)

Unregistered GPAs are no longer accepted by the Sub-Registrar for JDA-related registration.
For a principal resident in the USA, the GPA must be executed abroad and then made valid in
India via one of two attestation routes (see below), then adjudicated, before it can be used.

## Required powers (NDR's standing list — put ALL in blue if amending an existing doc)

- Sell / transfer / convey
- Mortgage / charge / encumber / hypothecate
- Gift
- Release / relinquish / surrender / waive, **expressly including in favour of any
  government, statutory or parastatal authority** (BBMP, TPA, etc.)
- Sign/re-sign/amend/vary/correct/clarify/ratify the JDA + any addendum (incl. the signed
  30-11-2024 Addendum 2), present and register it
- Take commercial decisions (sharing, inventory allocation, area statements, refundable)
- Receive consideration in cash or in kind, in favour of EITHER the principal or the
  attorney (into either party's bank account)
- General sweep: "deal with the property in any manner as fully and effectually as the
  principal could"

The Sub-Registrar / lender will NOT infer mortgage/gift/release from a general clause —
name them explicitly.

## US execution + attestation process (verified Sep-2026)

1. Sign before TWO witnesses who are NOT immediate family (full names + addresses on doc)
2. Affix one passport photo on the last page
3. Notarise with a US Notary Public
4. Attestation route — CONFIRM WITH THE SUB-REGISTRAR FIRST which one they accept:
   - Route A (Apostille): notarise locally, apostille from Alabama Secretary of State.
     No travel (Madison AL → Atlanta is 186 mi / ~4 h each way). Bharat reported the SRO
     said US Notary + Apostille is acceptable.
   - Route B (Consular attestation): since 01-08-2025, POA attestation is NOT done at the
     consulate — it moved to VFS Global (Indian Consular Application Centre),
     **1200 Altmore Ave, Suite 175, Sandy Springs, GA 30342** (Atlanta consulate is at
     5549 Glenridge Dr; going there sends her away). In-person only — no postal
     applications. Fees by money order/cashier cheque to "Consulate General of India,
     Atlanta" + ICWF; no cards. Indian nationals do NOT need apostille for this route.
   - Jurisdiction: Atlanta covers Alabama (revised consular jurisdiction 15-07-2025).
5. Courier attested originals to India
6. Adjudicate before the District Registrar (Bangalore) — GPA becomes operative
7. Then register the JDA addendum

Critical: NEVER send her travelling before the route is confirmed — a wrong route costs two
4-hour trips. Tell her the exact route in the WhatsApp message and gate on Bharat's SRO
sign-off.

Signature on the GPA must match the passport signature exactly, or the file is returned.

## Blue-edit convention (NDR)

Changes to a shared Google Doc GPA are made IN PLACE, styled **blue** (rgb 0,0,1) so
reviewers see exactly what changed; never a new doc for an amendment pass. Verify the runs'
rgbColor after batchUpdate — a helper that misreads a missing red key as 1.0 will report
"not blue" wrongly, so inspect raw runs directly.
