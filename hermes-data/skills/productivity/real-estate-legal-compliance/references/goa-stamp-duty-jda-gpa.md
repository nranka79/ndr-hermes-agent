# Goa Stamp Duty — Joint Development Agreement (JDA) + GPA

**When to use this:** Any question about stamp duty on JDAs, GPAs, power of attorney, or conveyance-equivalent duty in Goa — or structuring a landowner–developer JDA + implementing GPA for Goa property (e.g. DRA's Merces/Morambi 33-acre deal adjoining the Prestige JDA block). Research verified Sep 2026.

## Direct answer — the "double charge" is REAL in Goa

Both instruments are charged stamp duty "equivalent to conveyance" in Goa today:
1. A development agreement that authorises a developer to construct/market/sell/transfer units → conveyance-equivalent duty; AND
2. The GPA executed to implement it → conveyance-equivalent duty AGAIN (same parties, same property).

The Goa government itself calls this "duplication of stamp duty on instruments forming part of the transaction" and is legislating to fix it (see Legislative fix below). Stakeholders (incl. CREDAI) have represented against it. So it is not a collector's whim — but the charge is real as of Sep 2026.

## Governing law

Goa does NOT have its own Stamp Act. Applicable statute: **Indian Stamp Act, 1899 as applied to Goa**, amended by Goa state Acts (series: 2012 mining leases, 2014 lease renewal, 2025 mortgages & trusts, 2026 Mhajo Flat bill). The Goa Printing Press (official gazette host, printingpress.goa.gov.in — reachable from VPS) publishes these as Indian Stamp (Goa Amendment) Acts.

## Statutory hooks

- **S. 2(10)**: "conveyance" includes every instrument by which property is transferred inter vivos.
- **Schedule I, Art 5(c)**: agreement relating to sale of property → same duty as conveyance (Art 23).
- **Art 23**: conveyance — in Goa effectively ~5% of market value / consideration (state's own 26-Mar-2026 corporate-restructuring ordinance frames conveyance at "5% of market value or consideration", 80% reduction for small-firm schemes ≤₹5Cr; registration fee additional ~1%). Verify current Schedule-I rates with the Collector before finalising.
- **Art 48 (Power of Attorney)**: nominal duty for general/agency POAs, BUT proviso: a POA executed "for the purpose of conveying" immovable property → chargeable as conveyance. A JDA-implementation GPA that is irrevocable, transfers possession, or recites consideration reads as "for the purpose of conveying".

## Case law (credible, current)

- **Suraj Lamp & Industries (2) v. State of Haryana, (2012) 1 SCC 656 (SC)** — GPA + agreement + possession does NOT convey title; only registered conveyance does. GPA is an agency document.
- **State of Rajasthan v. Basant Nahata, (2005) 12 SCC 77 (SC)** — POA merely creates agency; does not transfer ownership even if irrevocable.
- **Ramesh Chand v. Suresh Chand (SC, 1 Sep 2025)** — reiterates: agreement-to-sell, GPA, even registered will cannot confer title; ownership passes only by registered conveyance. (SCC Online case comment 7-Oct-2025.)
- **Star Developers v. State of Maharashtra (Bombay HC, WP 11127/2018, J. Amit Borkar, 24 Aug 2026)** — landowner's share of future sale proceeds IS consideration for stamp duty on a development agreement; 42% revenue share must be included in value even if unquantified at execution. Directly relevant: JDA value is computed on full economics, not nominal amount. Also: CAG audit objections do not self-determine duty.
- **Bombay HC (2023, redevelopment)** — stamp duty on redevelopment/DA payable only on additional area actually acquired in the instrument (limiting principle on duty base).
- **Bombay HC (28 Aug 2026)** — referred to larger bench whether deficit-duty proceedings must complete within six years (limitation defence if contesting a demand).
- **Chandavarkar Sita Ratna Rao v. Ashalata S. Guram (SC 1986)** — development agreement by itself does not transfer title; title passes only through registered conveyances. Strongest argument the GPA (and even the JDA, absent conveyance) is overtaxed — but practice currently ignores it at the registration counter.

## Legislative fix — Indian Stamp (Goa Amendment) Bill 2026

- **TOI, 24 Aug 2026 (Panaji, Murari Shetye), "Govt to amend stamp law for Mhajo Flat"** — smoking-gun quote from senior Goa govt officer verbatim explaining the double charge and that "once conveyance-equivalent stamp duty is paid on either the development agreement or power of attorney, the subsequent instrument is charged only nominal duty" (several states already do this). Corroborated: Prop News Time 27-Aug-2026, Grihik 24-Aug-2026.
- Bill **introduced 31 Aug 2026** in Goa monsoon session (one of 16 bills — TOI). Registration (Goa Amendment) Bill 2026 passed 1–2 Sep (online registration, digital photos/fingerprints, cancellation of fraudulently registered docs). The stamp bill's passage was NOT confirmed as of 4 Sep 2026 — pending in the sitting session. Sep-3 DD India report of a passed stamp bill was actually an EoDB Court-Fees Act (stamp vendor fines) bill — not the Mhajo bill. Don't confuse them.

## Practical structuring for a Prestige-style JDA

1. If execution can wait for the 2026 Bill: the GPA leg falls to nominal duty; JDA duty may be rationalised. Get Goa counsel to confirm final text covers a landowner–developer JDA+GPA structure (TOI's description of the fixed practice matches it).
2. If executing now, budget double conveyance-level duty. Options on the GPA leg (strong dispute path): (a) Collector determination under s.31/32 before registration arguing agency instrument (nominal Art 48 duty); (b) pay under protest and reclaim/refund; (c) draft GPA as limited, non-irrevocable, no consideration, no possession transfer, no recital of sale — using the Delhi Jul-2026 scrutiny checklist of triggers (consideration / possession / irrevocability / permanent authority to sell-gift-transfer-mortgage) as what to AVOID.
3. JDA base can be contested: duty only on interest/value actually transferred (redevelopment-DA principle) — not the whole parcel value twice; on already-partially-owned land, compute only on the transferred interest.
