## Statutory anchors (Tamil Nadu)

- **Indian Stamp Act, 1899 (Central Act 2 of 1899)**, as amended in its application to TN. Latest
  revision: **Indian Stamp (Tamil Nadu Amendment) Act, 2023 (TN Act 13 of 2024)**, effective
  **03-May-2024** (G.O.Ms.No.475, Law). Raised multiple Schedule-I flat duties (e.g. partnership
  deed ₹300 → **₹1,000**; POA to sell immovable property → 4% of market value).
- **Section 2(10)** — "conveyance" definition (includes every instrument transferring property
  inter vivos).
- **Article 23 (Conveyance)** — TN conveyance duty **7% of market value** (guideline value where
  higher) + registration fee (sources vary 1-4%; confirm the current % with the Sub-Registrar).
- **Article 46 (Partnership)** — TN: flat **₹1,000** (capital > ₹500; ₹50 for ≤ ₹500) + 1%
  registration. Article 46B/47: reconstitution / dissolution; the 2023 amendment expanded
  "family" to include legal heirs of a deceased family member.
- **Registration Act, 1908, s.17** — instruments creating/declaring/assigning right, title or
  interest in immovable property > ₹100 are compulsorily registrable; contribution deed vesting
  title in the Firm needs registration.
- **Amalgamation / reconstruction reduced duty:** Governor of TN (under s.9 Stamp Act / s.78-A
  Registration Act) reduced duty on instruments of transfer relating to company amalgamation or
  **reconstruction** to **2% of market value of the immovable property** OR 0.6% of share value,
  whichever higher — G.O.(Ms) No.29 dated 01.03.2019 + G.O.(Ms) No.47 dated 19.02.2020, per IGR
  Circular 49282/P1/2018 (20.11.2018). **Madras HC, State of TN v. Serene Estate (W.A. 758/2022
  batch, Feb 2024) struck down the 0.6%-of-share-value limb** — 2% on market value of immovable
  property stands. Whether a Firm→company conversion qualifies under this G.O. is exactly what
  needs Sub-Registrar confirmation (it may not, since the G.O. is framed for company
  amalgamations/reconstructions).

## The open question (as posed to the Sub-Registrar)

DRA Realty is ALREADY a ~90% partner in the Firm. If it contributes its own land by way of
capital contribution (title consolidating in the Firm's name), will the instrument be treated:
1. as a mere **Article 46 partnership/supplemental deed** (flat ₹1,000 + 1% reg), or
2. as a **conveyance under Article 23 (7% + registration on market value)**, on the principle
   that title passes from the partner to the Firm?
Does partner-controlled contribution change classification? If Article 23 applies: guideline /
market / composite value base? And for Step 2: does the 2% reconstruction G.O. apply to a firm
conversion, or full 7%?

Framing note: a pure partnership deed is ₹1,000 flat, but TN registration practice has treated
instruments evidencing property transfer as conveyance under Article 23 (IGR Circular + Serene
Estate). The whole point of asking the Sub-Registrar is that partner-contribution of land sits
ambiguously between the two articles — do NOT tell NDR the answer is settled, present both
readings and ask.
