# Partnership Act Forms — Registration Verification

Under the Indian Partnership Act, 1932, the forms governing firm registration vary slightly by state (each state has its own rules framed under the Act).

## Key Forms

| Form | Name | What It Is | Who Issues / Files |
|:-----|:-----|:-----------|:-------------------|
| **Form A** | Application for Registration of Firm | Filed under Section 58 — contains firm name, principal place, names/addresses of all partners, date of joining each partner, firm duration, and any subsequent changes | Filed by the **partners** with the jurisdictional Registrar of Firms |
| **Form C** | Certificate of Registration | Issued under Section 59 when the Registrar accepts the registration application. Proves the firm is a registered entity | Issued by the **Registrar of Firms** |
| **Form D** | Certificate of Registration | In some states (notably Karnataka), the Certificate of Registration is called Form D instead of Form C — same evidentiary value, different state nomenclature | Issued by the **Registrar of Firms** |

## State-Specific Naming

| State | Registration Application | Certificate of Registration |
|:------|:------------------------|:---------------------------|
| **Karnataka** (Bangalore) | Form A | Usually **Form D** (sometimes Form C in older registrations) |
| **Tamil Nadu** (Chennai) | Form A | Usually **Form C** |
| **Maharashtra** / **Delhi** | Form A | Form C |

Always check the form number printed on the actual certificate — the letter designation varies.

## How to Obtain Missing Forms

### Form A — If missing from client records

| Route | Method | Time | Cost |
|:------|:-------|:----|:-----|
| **Registrar of Firms** | File an application for certified copies under the state's rules or RTI Act. Karnataka: Office of the Registrar of Firms, Dept. of Commerce & Industries, Bangalore. Tamil Nadu: Office of the Registrar of Firms, Chennai | 7–30 days | ₹100–500 |
| **Legal counsel** | Law firm (CMS IndusLaw, etc.) can requisition it as part of ongoing litigation due diligence — they know the fastest channel and exact reference number needed | 3–7 days via counsel | Counsel's incidental fees |
| **Online portal** | Some states have searchable firm databases: Karnataka E-Kacheri, Tamil Nadu e-Sevai. May show basic registration details even without the full Form A | Instant | Free or nominal |

### Form C / Form D — If the certificate itself is missing

- The Registrar of Firms can issue a **duplicate Certificate of Registration** on application
- A simple search on the state's firms database may suffice if only the firm's registration status needs to be confirmed

## When Form A May Not Be A Blocker

If the client already has all three of:
- ✅ **Certificate of Registration** (Form C or D) — proves the firm IS registered
- ✅ **Partnership Deed** — proves the terms, partners, and shares
- ✅ **Reconstitution Deed** (if applicable) — proves partner changes (retirement, admission)

Then Form A is the **application for registration**, not the registration itself. It typically contains the same information as the Partnership Deed. Counsel may request it only for completeness or to verify the exact filing date with the Registrar.

**Verdict:** If Form A is missing but the Certificate of Registration + Partnership Deed are available, Form A is rarely a deal-breaker. Ask counsel whether it is strictly necessary for the current proceeding or merely desirable for a complete record.
