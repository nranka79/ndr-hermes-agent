# Property Due Diligence — Registered Documents Checklist

**List of registered documents typically required for Indian property title due diligence, with standard requests for sub-registrar procurement.**

## Standard 15-Item Checklist

Use as the "missing documents" table in email requests to team members (Rahul, Rajan, etc.) asking them to procure certified copies from the sub-registrar office.

| # | Required Document | Period/Date | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Joint Development Agreement (JDA)** | As per transaction | Foundational document between developer & landowner |
| 2 | **Supplementary Agreements** | Post-JDA | Amendments, possession recording, corrections |
| 3 | **Original Sale Deed Chain** | 1950 onward | Complete chain: original owner → intermediate owners → current vendor |
| 4 | **Encumbrance Certificates (ECs)** | Last 30+ years | Verify clear title — no undisclosed mortgages, liens, or claims |
| 5 | **RTC / Record of Rights** | Current & historical (5-10 yrs) | Revenue records per survey number |
| 6 | **Khata Certificate & Khata Extract** | Current | BBMP property tax assessment — confirms A-Khata status |
| 7 | **Tax Paid Receipts** | Annual (last 5+ yrs) | Property tax payment proof |
| 8 | **Sanctioned Plan / Building License** | Original sanction year | BMP/BBMP approved plan |
| 9 | **Commencement Certificate** | Post-sanction | Permission to start construction |
| 10 | **Conversion Order** | Original conversion year | Agri-to-non-agri conversion under KLU Act Sec 95 |
| 11 | **CITB / BDA Layout Approval** | Layout formation year | Approval for layout formation |
| 12 | **Court Orders (Dismissal)** | Date of order | Proves suit conclusively disposed |
| 13 | **Pending Court Records** | Current status | Restoration petitions, latest order/vacancy |
| 14 | **Plaint / Written Statement** | Filing date | Full plaint for each pending/decided suit |
| 15 | **Complete Title Search Report** | 30+ year search | Professional title search from sub-registrar records |

## Email Template for Request

### Subject
`[Project Name] — Registered Documents Required from Sub-Registrar Office`

### Body: Two-Table Format
1. **Table 1: Available Documents** — what's already in the Drive folder (with links)
2. **Table 2: Required Documents** — the 15 items with specific notes per project

### Key phrasing

> "Since most of these are registered documents, could you please obtain certified copies from the Sub-Registrar's Office? The original sale deed chain and encumbrance certificates are critical for establishing clear title.
>
> I will separately coordinate with [Person A] to follow up on this, and also check with [Person B] if some of these documents are already with him. In the interim, kindly help us procure the registered documents so we can move forward with the title verification and plan the next steps with the landowners."

## ⚠️ CRITICAL PITFALL: Don't mix up "referenced as existing" vs "identified as a gap"

**This is the #1 error when compiling missing-documents lists from legal opinions.**

Legal opinions often *reference* documents that exist (as context or evidence) while *identifying others* as unavailable. These are opposites — don't conflate them.

### Examples from real sessions

| Legal opinion text | What it means | Belongs in... |
|---|---|---|
| *"The Querist has also obtained sanctions from the Bangalore Mahanagara Palike vide LP No. 94/1996-97"* | **Document EXISTS** — referenced as evidence of the developer's intent | Table 1 (Available), not Table 2 (Missing) |
| *"We do not have a copy of the plaint"* | **Document IS GAP** — explicitly stated as unavailable | Table 2 (Missing) |
| *"We do not have any information as to the current status of OS 2095/2002"* | **Information is gap** — not a document itself, but indicates missing court record | Table 2 (Missing) if the underlying record exists |

### The rule

Before putting ANY item in the "Missing" table:

1. **Re-read the exact sentence** in the legal opinion that mentions this document
2. Classify as one of:
   - **"Querist obtained/has"** → EXISTS — goes in Available table or is simply omitted from Missing
   - **"We do not have" / "Not provided to us"** → GAP — belongs in Missing table
   - **Merely listed in the property schedule or background** (e.g., "Sale deed dated 06.11.1950") → NOT automatically a gap. The lawyer may have had it. Check if the opinion says anything about not having it.
   - **Standard checklist item with no mention in the opinion** → Do NOT auto-include. If the lawyer didn't flag it, it may exist elsewhere. Mention it separately as "not covered in this opinion" rather than "missing."

3. **The generic checklist trap:** The 15-item standard checklist above is a *reference template* — NOT an automatically correct list for any given project. For every row you include in the actual email, you must be able to point to either (a) an explicit lawyer statement that it's unavailable, or (b) your own knowledge that the folder doesn't contain it AND it's typically registered.

4. **When the user questions a specific item** (like "is that LP for our 15,000 sq ft?"):
   - Go back to the source document immediately
   - Quote the exact sentence and context
   - If the source doesn't specify the detail the user is asking about (sq ft, area, specific plot), say so clearly
   - Don't "fill in" from general knowledge — the user knows the property better than any checklist

### Verification workflow before creating the Missing table

```
For each document you're about to list:
  1. Is it in the legal opinion? 
     → NO: Can you independently verify it's missing from the Drive folder?
        → NO: Don't list it. Stick to what the opinion actually flags.
     → YES: Does the opinion say "we do not have X"?
        → YES: List in Missing table with quote from opinion
        → NO (merely mentioned): Check if it's in Drive. If found → Available table.
          If not found → flag as "mentioned in opinion but not in folder" with caveat
```

## Source References

- **Survey numbers** → verify against ECs and JDA schedule, NOT plan sanction conditions
- **Document dates** → from registered deed dates, not the signature date in the doc body
- **Vendor remarks** → legal opinion "do not have a copy of [X]" statements are direct evidence of gaps
