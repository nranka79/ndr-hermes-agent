# Labour Department (BOCW) Notice — Classification & Response

Verified 2026-09-05 on the Ranka Amber 5-page Labour Dept scan (Adobe Scan,
dated 25-08-2026, from Office of the Labour Officer, Sub-Division-3, Karmika
Bhavan, Dairy Circle, Bangalore). This notice class is FUNDAMENTALLY different
from a BMTF police notice — do not treat it as extortion.

## Critical distinction: BMTF vs Labour Dept

| | BMTF police notice | Labour Dept BOCW notice |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Inquiry summons, often fishing/extortion | Statutory tax assessment + compliance demand |
| Legal basis | 12 revenue/municipal acts, loose jurisdiction | BOCW Welfare Cess Act 1996 S.3(1) — real levy |
| Rate/ask | Usually intimidate → pay off | 1% of construction cost, demanded via assessment |
| Response deadline | Appear on date named (often 4 days) | Furnish documents within 10 days of receipt |
| Non-response | Weak — defective notice → no coercive action | Ex parte assessment on THEIR numbers (real risk) |
| Strategy | Challenge jurisdiction, delay, don't appear | RESPOND within window, register, minimize assessable cost |

## What the notice contains (2 letters, often one scan)

1. **Kannada document-submission notice** (e.g. No. KaaBem-3/Vi.Ka.Ka/Notice/2026-27):
   directs production of registers and records of the last 01 year for
   verification under SIX labour statutes:
   - BOCW Welfare Cess Act 1996 + Rules 1998 (Form-I, Form-IV commencement/
     completion, approved plan, OC, estimated-cost docs, plant & machinery)
   - BOCW (Regulation of Employment & Conditions of Service) Act 1996 /
     Karnataka Rules 2006 (S.7 registration cert, Form-X beneficiaries
     register r.22, Form-XXXV workers register r.258, wage book/muster roll/
     wage slips/service certs r.259, treatment records r.243)
   - Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act 1970 + Rules 1974 (S.7 + r.18
     licence, Form 6A/26 reports, Forms 14/16/17/18/19/24/83 registers, r.71/r.81
     display notices, Form-T combined attendance r.24(9)(b))
   - Minimum Wages Act 1948 (Form-I combined attendance+wages r.29(1), Form-VI
     wage slips S.18(3)/r.29(2))
   - Karnataka Industrial Establishments (National & Festival Holidays) Act
     1963 (2026 holiday list displayed in Kannada + English r.5(1), double wages
     for work on holidays)
   - Inter-State Migrant Workmen Act 1979 (registration, licences, registers)
   Addressed to the Directors (e.g. "1. Nishant Dinesh Ranka and 2. Kishan
   Murjani Nair — Directors"). Ends: principal employer directed to make
   contractors/sub-contractors produce their own records, else legal action.

2. **English cess-assessment notice** (e.g. No. LOB-3/CESS/2026-27): cess under
   S.3(1) BOCW Cess Act 1996 read with Karnataka GO ID-300/LET-2000 dated
   18.01.2007 + Notification LED-62/LET-2007(II) dated 04.04.2007 — 1% of total
   construction cost. Demands (a) S.7 registration certificate, (b) cess paid
   Form-I, (c) Form-IV commencement/completion, (d) approved plan, (e) OC,
   (f) VALUATION REPORT with cost break-up, (g) other records — within 10 days;
   PLUS audited Balance Sheets + Schedules of last 3 FYs under S.7 read with
   Rule 10. Failure → assessment concluded EX PARTE on available materials.

## Response strategy (statutory — respond, don't ignore)

1. Do NOT sit on it. The 10-day clock runs from receipt; ex parte assessment on
   their numbers is a genuine downside (unlike BMTF).
2. Register under BOCW (RECS) Act S.7 (registration certificate) — cheap, fast,
   and satisfies item (a) of the cess notice plus the Kannada notice's first
   requirement.
3. Submit Form-I (cess paid — likely nil to date, disclose truthfully) and
   Form-IV (commencement/completion) — these overlap the CC application pack
   (approved plan, Form-IV), so the same Anbu/Sarthak approvals team can source
   them.
4. The **valuation report is the battleground** — challenge the assessable cost
   of construction; the demand largely derives from it.
5. The audited balance-sheet (3 FY) demand is standard; soften with a reply
   narrowing scope or submitting what exists — do not silently ignore.
6. Respond on paper within the window; prefer the office reply (letter) over
   appearing unprepared — same zero-cost framing as BMTF but with submission,
   not jurisdiction challenge, as the core.

## FIRST check: was the 1% labour cess already paid at plan sanction? (2026-09-05)

**Before drafting any response, verify whether the cess was already collected by
the local authority (GBA/BBMP) at plan sanction.** If so, the LOB-3/CESS notice's
core demand is already answered. This is the single strongest reply to the notice
and it is checked in 2 minutes:

1. Search Drive for the project's `Demand Notes` folder under `Approvals`
   (e.g. `DRA Realty / Ranka Amber / Approvals / Demand Notes`).
2. Look for two matching PDFs on the same date:
   - `... Demand Note CH.... - Labour Cess.pdf` — GBAE/BECC demand note raised at
     plan sanction listing "Labour cess amount" as a line item (e.g. ₹6,25,000)
   - `... Labour Cess Payment Receipt - BBMP EoDB RC....pdf` — the paid receipt
3. Cross-match by File/LP number (e.g. `GBA/BECC/0540/25-26`) and amount.

Worked example (Ranka Amber, 27-Apr-2026): Demand Note **BBMP/EoDB/CH/5255/26-27**
(item "Labour cess amount ₹6,25,000", File GBA/BECC/0540/25-26) matched Receipt
**BBMP/EoDB/RC/5754/26-27** (Transaction ID 114493156453, ₹6,25,000, status
success). The cess had been collected at sanction via the **Section 3(2) BOCW
Cess Act mechanism: "advance collection through a local authority where approval
of such building or other construction work by such local authority is
required."** So the cess notice's 1% demand is answered — attach both proofs.

Also note: the companion Plan-Sanction-Fees demand note (CH5254) contains a line
"1% of Labour cess amount to BBMP ₹6,250" — that is the collection charge on the
cess (a separate line from the cess itself), not evidence of non-payment.

## Applicability matrix — 6 labour statutes at LOW worker strength (≤5-6 workers)

The key legal argument when site labour strength is small: several of the six
statutes have mandatory worker-strength thresholds below which they do NOT apply,
so their registration/licensing/register machinery cannot be demanded. The Cess
Act and Minimum Wages apply regardless; the rest hinge on headcount (and for ISMW,
on the workers' origin state).

| Statute | Threshold (S.1(4) etc.) | At 5-6 workers |
|---|---|---|
| **BOCW (RECS) Act 1996** | 10+ building workers (or on any day in preceding 12 mo) | **NOT APPLICABLE** — no S.7 regn, no Form-I/IV/XXXV, no beneficiary registers |
| **BOCW Welfare Cess Act 1996** | No headcount threshold — 1% of cost of construction (S.3(1)) | **APPLIES** — but see cess-already-paid check above |
| **Contract Labour (R&A) Act 1970** | 20+ contract workmen (or any day in preceding 12 mo) | **NOT APPLICABLE** — no licence / principal-employer regn (Forms 16-24) |
| **Minimum Wages Act 1948** | Construction = scheduled employment in Karnataka, no threshold | **APPLIES** — maintain Form-I (attendance+wages), Form-VI (wage slips) |
| **Karnataka Industrial Establishments (N&F Holidays) Act 1963** | All industrial establishments, every employee | **APPLIES** — display 2026 holiday list Kannada+English, holiday wage |
| **Inter-State Migrant Workmen Act 1979** | 5+ inter-state migrant workmen | **TRIGGER AT 5** — APT only if workers came from other states; confirm each worker's home state with contractor |

**Practical net (5-6 worker site):** of the six statutes, only the Cess Act
(already paid), Minimum Wages (administrative), and the holiday-display rule truly
bite. The BOCW-RECS, CLRA licensing, and ISMW machinery does NOT apply at that
strength. Respond on paper with the cess-paid proof, answer each head, and provide
only the limited records that do apply — the notice is a fishing expedition, but
unlike BMTF it must be ANSWERED (ex parte assessment risk).

**Contractor angle (2026-09-05):** the labour records live with the EXECUTING
CONTRACTOR, not the developer. Ask the contractor to furnish the applicable
records and confirm worker headcount/origin. Resolve the contractor from Kelsa
PO-WO pipeline (e.g. Vardhan Ventures = "Vardhan Ventures", key contact "Mr.
Jayaram/Jeram", turnover PO-739 ₹4.03 Cr for "Amber G+4 apartment"). Full
contractor-document-request email pattern (20-item HTML ☐ checklist under 6
statutes + cess-paid callout box + copy of the Kannada notice) — see
`email-drafter` → `references/html-checklist-emails.md`.
