# Karnataka Stamp Duty on JDA Addenda / Assignments — Research Bank (Sep 2026)

Condensed from the Ranka North Star (Allalsandra) addendum-registration analysis.
Use when a Sub-Registrar challenges stamp duty on a JDA addendum, assignment, or GPA-linked
instrument. This is research, not legal advice — NDR routes through DNR Law Firm / counsel.

## Chargeable-instrument framework — Karnataka Stamp Act 1957

- **Article 5(f)** (substituted 01-03-2014, 2%): "agreement relating to construction or
  development of immovable property between owner and developer" — duty on Market Value of
  UDS conveyed to developer OR constructed share delivered to owner (whichever higher), PLUS
  **"consideration and money advanced"**. Explanation: *money advanced MEANS AND INCLUDES the
  security deposit whether refundable or adjustable* → refundable deposits sit inside the base.
- **₹200 proviso**: if duty paid on a GPA under Article 41(ea) between same parties / same
  property, the corresponding Article 5(f) agreement duty is capped at ₹200. CHECK GPAs first —
  cheapest possible win.
- **Article 5(J)** "agreement (in any other cases)" — fixed ~₹500 residual class.
- **Section 4**: multiple instruments in one transaction → principal bears full duty, others
  nominal (drafted for sale/mortgage/settlement, embodies anti-double-duty policy).
- **Section 31**: adjudication before Deputy Commissioner (₹100 fee) → binding, appealable
  determination + Section 32 certificate; proviso to s.31(1)(b) relieves penalty if full duty
  paid. THE move when SRO pushes back — takes discretion away from the counter.
- **Section 34**: no instrument registered unless duly stamped. If the SRO registered the
  assignment in 2021, s.34 means it WAS treated as duly stamped — State can't impugn its own act.
- **Section 46A(1) LIMITATION**: recovery notice within 5 years of duty becoming payable;
  10 years only on fraud/collusion/wilful mis-statement/suppression/intent to evade. No such
  plea → 5-year bar is hard. Assignment 15.03.2021 → bar expired 15.03.2026.
- **Registration Act s.17(1)(b)**: compulsory registration only for instruments creating/
  assigning/limiting right in immovable property ≥ ₹100. A pure timeline-extension/ack addendum
  may NOT be compulsorily registrable, BUT a clause creating security over landowner inventory
  (Clause 3-type) IS → register it anyway.
- **Registration fee**: 2% from 31-08-2025 (was 1%). Confirm current rate at SRO.

## Case law verified this session (cite in the opinion)

1. **Rajeshwari v. State of Karnataka** (KHC, 25.06.2026) — s.46A limitation is JURISDICTIONAL,
   not procedural; notice beyond max 10-year period "functus officio and void"; subsequent
   bona fide purchaser not liable for vendor's decades-old default. Kills the assignment claim.
2. **Tata Communications** (Madras HC DB, Oct 2025) — amendment agreement to a registered
   instrument is NOT a fresh transaction where no new property/extra rights gained; no fresh
   stamp duty. (News reports: "amendment agreement cannot be treated as fresh lease".)
3. **Adityaraj Builders v. State of Maharashtra** (Bom HC DB, 17.02.2023) — once the development
   agreement is stamped, incidental instrument relating to same premises can only attract
   nominal duty; "stamp duty cannot be levied twice"; duty only on ADDITIONAL area for fresh
   consideration. Struck down contrary departmental circulars.
4. **Wadhwa Construction v. State of Maharashtra** (Bom HC, 07.09.2026) — duty determined from
   SUBSTANCE AND EFFECT of instrument as on execution date; contingent/future rights (e.g. 15%
   on future TDR load) not valued as vested; applicable FSI = FSI actually available on
   execution date, not maximum; strict construction of fiscal statutes — "development potential
   not operative under the instrument on the relevant date cannot be added by implication".
5. **Seetharama Shetty v. Monappa Shetty** (2024 INSC 650) — impounding/penalty sequence under
   ss.33-35 Karnataka Stamp Act.
6. **BPCL v. District Revenue Officer (Stamps)** (2026 INSC 963) — PENDING larger Bench on
   whether undervaluation needs proof of fraud under s.47A parallel. Reserve explicitly.

## The e-stamp certificate evidence trick

The department's own e-stamping (Kaveri/SHCIL) classification is powerful evidence:
- Addendum e-stamped 30-Nov-2024: "Article 5(J) Agreement (in any other cases)", consideration
  Rs. 0, duty ₹500 (cert IN-KA82505335604880W) → department already classified THIS document.
- Assignments e-stamped ₹200 as "Article 12 Bond", consideration Rs. 0, registered 2021 →
  registered as duly stamped (s.34), now time-barred (s.46A).
- Print the certificates and attach — the counter can't argue with its own system's classification
  without contradicting itself.

## Standing analytical conclusions (North Star specific)

- Landowner 33% entitlement on 1.75 FAR: addendum states a RATE on "the Subject Property"
  (53,148 sq ft) but COMPUTES a table on 43,208 sq ft → grey area worth ~5,700 sq ft / ₹4Cr+.
  Treated as illustration of the rate; contra proferentem against drafter (DRA); user has
  told landowners in writing the khata discrepancy has "no impact on entitlements" — that
  statement is now evidence against the table reading. Fix in sharing agreement with express
  basis clause. The sanctioned plan area will resolve it: if sanction loads 53,148 into Kaveri
  2.0, rate reading prevails; if 43,208, table is right.
- The 15% on future TDR/premium FAR is contingent, optional, developer-funded → NOT chargeable
  now (Wadhwa); duty deferred to when developer buys TDR (full duty then). State loses nothing.
- Separate JDA 3033 for Site 10 (12,106 sq ft) vs JDA 3034 for Sites 1-8 (amalgamated 53,148):
  two GPAs, never bundle an exit instrument with the live project GPA.

## Sub-Registrar engagement pattern that works

1. Short legal opinion (DNR Law Firm letterhead): summary table of 4 questions → answer (Not
   due ×3, "not compulsory but advisable") → principal authority per question. 6 pages, not 22.
2. Closing section = polite request: if authority still uncomfortable, REFER under Section 31
   adjudication rather than decide at counter (protects them: penalty waiver + appeal route).
3. Get e-stamp certs + GPAs' Article 41(ea) particulars + Kaveri written estimate BEFORE the
   appointment. Have counsel vet before it goes on the record.
