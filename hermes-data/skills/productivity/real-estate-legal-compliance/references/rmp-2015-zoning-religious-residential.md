# RMP 2015 — Religious & Residential Mixed-Use Zoning (Dharmashala + Temple)

## Scenario

Main use: **Dharmashala** (temporary non-profit accommodation for devotees/gurus)
Ancillary use: **Temple / Place of Worship (U4)**

Land classification options: **Residential (Main) — R1** or **Residential (Mixed) — R2/R3/R4**

Flipped scenario (alternative): Main use = Temple (U4) in **Public & Semi-Public (P&SP) Zone** with ancillary residential ≤20% of site area.

---

## Critical Rule: U4 Ancillary Threshold — 50 sq.m (NOT a Percentage)

RMP 2015 Clause 7.7.3.1: Religious U4 is "ancillary" only when **built-up area < 50 sq.m**.

- **< 50 sq.m** = Ancillary use (no separate treatment needed)
- **≥ 50 sq.m** = Independent use (must meet all standalone U4 conditions: separate parking, access, setbacks, etc.)
- This is a **fixed area cap**, NOT a percentage of total built-up area
- The 20%/30% percentage limits apply to different use categories (e.g., professional offices in mixed zones), NOT to U4

## Zone Comparison: Dharmashala (Main) + Temple (Ancillary < 50 sq.m)

| Factor | Residential (Main) — R1 | Residential (Mixed) — R2 | Best Bet |
|--------|------------------------|------------------------|----------|
| **Dharmashala** | ✅ Permitted (P) | ✅ Permitted (P) | Either |
| **Temple <50 sq.m** | ✅ Ancillary (C) | ✅ Ancillary (C) | Either |
| **Min road width** | 9m (OR 18m if GFA >1,000 sq.m) | **12m** for R2 | R1 (9m) |
| **FAR** | 1.75 – 2.25 | **2.0 – 3.25** | R2 |
| **Ground Coverage** | 45 – 55% | 35 – 50% | R1 (higher GC) |
| **Height potential** | Limited on 9m road | Higher on 12m+ road | R2 |
| **Parking** | 1 ECS/25 sq.m (residential) + 1 ECS/100 sq.m (temple) | Same | Same |

## Residential (Mixed) — U4 as Main Use

If road width **≥ 18m** AND plot **> 240 sq.m**, U4 can be the **main** land use in a Mixed zone (Table 11):

- R2 on 18m road: higher FAR, lower GC
- R3 on 18m road: even higher FAR
- Still best for temple-as-main scenario, but P&SP zone is cleaner

## Residential (Main) — R1 Rules

**From RMP 2015:**

### Permitted Uses (Table 1)
- Plotted Housing, Villas, Apartments, **Dharmashala** (explicitly listed), Group Housing
- U4 (Religious): **Permitted with Conditions (C)**

### Road Width Requirements
- General access: ≥ **9m** (30ft)
- If Gross Floor Area (Dharmashala + Temple) > **1,000 sq.m**: ≥ **18m** (60ft) required per Table 7 for "Religious/Charitable Institutions with area > 1,000 sq.m GFA"

### FAR & Coverage (Table 22 — R1)
| Plot Area × Road Width | FAR | Ground Coverage |
|------------------------|-----|----------------|
| ≤240 sq.m, 9m road | 1.75 | 55% |
| Larger, 12m road | 2.0 | 50% |
| Larger, 18m road | 2.25 | 45% |

### Height Limits (Table 21)
- Directly tied to road width:
  - 9m road: typically capped at **11.5m** (G+2/3)
  - 12m road: up to **15m**
  - 18m road: up to **27-33m**

## P&SP Zone (Alternative: Main = Temple)

If the primary purpose is the **temple** with residential as ancillary:

- Zone: Public & Semi-Public (U4)
- Min road width: > 9m
- Plot > 2,000 sq.m recommended (FAR 2.25)
- Temple = Main use
- Guru accommodation / Dharmashala = **Ancillary ≤ 20% of site area** (NOT the 50 sq.m rule — different zone, different ancillary regime)
- Premium FAR + TDR available
- Single Plot Regulations apply if plot > 4,000 sq.m (10% park + 5% parking to authority)
- Cleaner regulatory path than Residential zone for temple-first projects

## Pitfalls

- **Do not apply the 20%/30% percentage to U4 ancillary in Residential zones** — that rule is for commercial/office ancillary, NOT religious. U4 uses a fixed 50 sq.m threshold.
- **P&SP zone is the cleaner path if temple is the main purpose** — don't force a Residential zone fit when P&SP exists
- **Road width is the binding constraint** — 9m on R1 caps height at 11.5m. A 12m+ road unlocks FAR and height.
- **1000 sq.m GFA threshold** — once combined total exceeds this, road width jumps from 9m to 18m
- **Dharmashala definition is narrow** — "non-profit" and "temporary accommodation." Commercial rates may trigger reclassification as lodging house.
- **Single Plot Regulations** — plots > 4,000 sq.m must relinquish 10% for park + 5% for parking.
- **AAI NOC** — Devanahalli taluk is near airport flight path. AAI clearance likely mandatory.
- **Fire NOC** — temple (public assembly) + residential attracts stringent fire safety requirements.
