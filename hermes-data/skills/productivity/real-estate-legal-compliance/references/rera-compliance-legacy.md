## Relationship to rera-approval-documents

The `rera-compliance` skill was the original, simpler version that covered the same forms (Form-2, Form-3, Allotment Letter, Agreement of Sale, SIS, Common Areas) at a higher level. The `rera-approval-documents` skill is the comprehensive version with:
- Detailed per-document step-by-step filling instructions
- Bangalore mid-market spec benchmarks
- Project team org structure templates
- Complete KRERA form-filling guide with pitfalls and data maps
- Color highlighting workflow for multi-pass corrections
- Data source prioritisation

**Use `rera-approval-documents` instead of this skill.**
