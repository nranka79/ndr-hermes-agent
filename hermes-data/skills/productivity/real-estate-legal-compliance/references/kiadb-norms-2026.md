## Applicability
This order applies to **all plots allotted by KIADB** (Karnataka Industrial Areas Development Board) — including industrial plots, IT/BT zones, commercial, residential, and affordable housing within KIADB-notified areas.

## FAR Based on Road Width (TABLE-3.4.1)

| Row | Min Road Width (m) | Base FAR | Premium FAR (Chargeable) | **Max FAR** |
|-----|-------------------|----------|--------------------------|-------------|
| 1 | < 12 m | 1.75 | 1.05 | **2.80** |
| 2 | 12 m to 18 m | 2.25 | 1.35 | **3.60** |
| 3 | 18 m to 24 m | 2.80 | 1.20 | **4.00** |
| 4 | **24 m to 30 m** | **3.00** | **1.80** | **4.80** |
| 5 | > 30 m | 3.25 | 1.95 | **5.20** |

**Note:** Premium FAR cost = ~50% of allotment value.

## Other Key Relaxations

| Parameter | New Norm (Feb 2026) |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **Ground Coverage** | Up to **75%** (was ~50%) |
| **Setbacks** | Reduced for smaller plots and buildings < 7m height |
| **Stilt Parking** | **Exempted from FAR calculation** |
| **Mechanical Parking** | **Exempted from FAR calculation** |
| **Additional FAR** | Up to **25% extra** where full FAR can't be achieved due to setbacks |

## Key Implications for DRAAS

1. **KIADB plots now have HIGHER FAR than BBMP/BMRDA commercial zones** — FAR 5.2 (KIADB) vs 3.5 (BBMP premium). This makes KIADB-land commercial development more lucrative than it was before.
2. **Stilt parking exempt from FAR** — Adds ~10-12% effective usable area vs other jurisdictions.
3. **75% ground coverage** — Larger floor plates, more efficient design.
4. **Jurisdiction matters** — Verify the plot is a KIADB-allotted plot and NOT a private land conversion. The new norms apply ONLY to KIADB-allotted land.

## How to Verify KIADB Jurisdiction

- Check the **allotment letter / sale deed** — does it reference KIADB?
- Check the **plan sanction** — is it `GBA/BECC/...` (BBMP) or `KIADB/...`?
- Visit **https://kiadb.karnataka.gov.in** → Downloads → Notifications
- If the site address says "Industrial Area" (Devasandra Industrial Area, Peenya Industrial Area, Bommasandra Industrial Area, etc.), it's likely KIADB
