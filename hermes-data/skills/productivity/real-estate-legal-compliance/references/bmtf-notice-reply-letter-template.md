# BMTF Notice — Formal Reply Letter Template

This template is for the interim/proposed reply to a BMTF police notice. It is designed to:
1. Deny the allegations on the record (establishing your defence)
2. Demand procedural compliance (forcing them to reveal their hand)
3. Buy 4-6 weeks of time (archival records excuse)
4. Challenge the scope of the document request (narrowing the inquiry)
5. Provide a non-coercive assurance (showing good faith)

## Structure (5 Points)

The reply follows this exact structure — use this pattern for any BMTF/BBMP police notice.

### 1. DENIAL OF BASELESS ALLEGATIONS

State clearly that the allegations are denied at the threshold. Cite:
- Development undertaken on absolute private converted land
- Full conformity with BBMP Sanctioned Plan
- Valid BBMP e-Khatha
- All applicable municipal laws complied with

### 2. DEMAND FOR COPY OF COMPLAINT AND PRIMA FACIE SURVEY RECORDS

This is the critical procedural demand. The notice indicates a petition/complaint was lodged, but neither the complaint copy, the complainant's identity, nor the supporting survey materials have been furnished. Argue:
- Natural Justice principle — inquiry must rest on specific, verifiable material
- If complainant submitted survey sketch, revenue demarcation report, or village map — we are entitled to copies
- Formally call upon the office to furnish:
  - (a) Complete certified copy of the underlying Complaint/Petition
  - (b) Copies of all survey records, sketches, spot inspection notes, or prima facie materials

### 3. REQUISITION FOR TIME (4 TO 6 WEEKS) TO RETRIEVE ARCHIVAL RECORDS

This is the delay mechanism. State:
- Property has a historic and documented title trail spanning several years
- All root title deeds, archival revenue extracts, and historical project files are in centralized off-site corporate legal archives
- Collation, verification, and retrieval requires 4 to 6 weeks
- We commit to placing all relevant records on file upon collation AND upon receipt of the complaint particulars demanded above

### 4. CLARIFICATION ON RELEVANCE AND STATUTORY BASIS OF EXTENDED DOCUMENT LIST

This challenges the scope creep — BMTF often asks for RERA certificates, NOCs, town planning documents in a boundary/buffer inquiry where they have no relevance. State:
- We cannot comprehend the relevance, necessity, or statutory basis for extraneous documents (RERA Registration Certificates, Town Planning NOCs, general project documentation) in an inquiry solely concerning physical boundary encroachment allegations
- Matters under RERA 2016 fall exclusively within the statutory domain of K-RERA
- Town Planning NOCs fall within the municipal jurisdiction of the Planning Authority
- Request the office to clarify: specific provision of law under which each extraneous document is sought, and the specific purpose in the context of the alleged boundary/buffer inquiry

### 5. INTERIM SUBMISSIONS & NON-COERCIVE ASSURANCE

Show good faith while reserving rights:
- We are ready and willing to cooperate with every inquiry conducted strictly in accordance with due process
- As this is at a preliminary petition inquiry stage, we reserve the right to submit a comprehensive, document-supported response upon receipt of requisite complaint copies and upon retrieval of records from archives

### Requested Orders

i. Furnish certified copies of the Complaint and accompanying survey materials
ii. Grant an extension of 4 to 6 weeks from the date of receipt of complaint particulars to submit substantive reply
iii. Refrain from taking any precipitative, coercive, or adverse action in the interim

## Formatting Notes

- **Address to:** The Police Sub-Inspector, [Authority], [Address]
- **Subject line:** INTERIM REPRESENTATION, DEMAND FOR COMPLAINT PARTICULARS, AND REQUISITION FOR TIME IN RESPECT OF POLICE NOTICE BEARING [REF NO] DATED [DATE]
- **RE line:** Property description with full details (Plot No, Katha No, PID No, Village, Hobli)
- **Under instructions from:** Full entity name (e.g. M/s. DRA REALTY PRIVATE LIMITED)
- **Signature:** Advocate/Law Firm with enrollment number
- **Delivery:** Hand Delivery / Registered Post A.D. (RPAD)
