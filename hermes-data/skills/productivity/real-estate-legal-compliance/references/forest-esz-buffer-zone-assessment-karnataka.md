# Forest / Eco-Sensitive Zone Buffer Zone Assessment — Karnataka

## Bannerghatta National Park ESZ Notification (2016)

- **Notification:** MoEFCC Final Notification F.No. 1-1/2016-ESZ
- **Area:** 268.96 sq km ESZ around 260.51 sq km Bannerghatta National Park
- **Buffer:** 100m to 4.5 km from park boundary (varies by village/survey)

## Critical Legal Distinction: NP/WS vs Reserve Forest

The single most important distinction when assessing land near a forest in Karnataka:

| Category | Definition | ESZ Buffer Rule |
|----------|-----------|----------------|
| **National Park** | Declared under Wildlife Protection Act 1972 | SC 2022 directive: **minimum 1 km ESZ**. May be larger if a specific notification exists (e.g. Bannerghatta: up to 4.5 km) |
| **Wildlife Sanctuary** | Declared under Wildlife Protection Act 1972 | SC 2022 directive: **minimum 1 km ESZ** |
| **Reserve Forest** | Declared under Indian Forest Act 1927, Section 20 | **No automatic ESZ.** The SC 2022 1 km rule does NOT apply. Indian Forest Act restrictions apply only |
| **Protected Forest** | Declared under IFA 1927, Section 29 (or state forest acts) | **No automatic ESZ.** Governed by state-specific forest rules |
| **Unclassed Forest** | Revenue land with tree cover (non-notified) | No specific rules — governed by local land use regulations |

**Practical impact:** The 1 km construction ban triggers ONLY if the adjacent forest is a National Park or Wildlife Sanctuary. If it is a Reserve Forest (most common around Bangalore: Makali Durga, Savandurga, Bannerghatta RF, Dandiganahalli, etc.), the SC 1 km rule does NOT trigger. You must check under the Karnataka Forest Act what restrictions apply (usually activity-specific, not blanket).

## Worked Example: Dodalapur/Gunjur (Sy. 40 & 41) near Makali Durga

### User Question

User's land at **Dodalapur/Gunjur, Sy. 40 & 41** — next to the railway track, abutting **Makali Durga Forest Range ("Makalidur")**. Asked: does the Bannerghatta ESZ notification apply? What buffer zone can they expect?

### Analysis

1. **Makali Durga** is a **Reserve Forest** near Doddaballapur, ~55 km north of Bangalore. It is NOT a National Park or Wildlife Sanctuary.

2. **Bannerghatta NP ESZ notification (uploaded document)** does NOT apply — it covers only Bannerghatta NP (south of Bangalore, different location entirely).

3. **The SC 1 km mandatory ESZ does NOT apply** — that rule covers only National Parks and Wildlife Sanctuaries; Makali Durga is neither.

4. **What DOES apply:**
   - **Indian Forest Act 1927** — Section 20 Reserve Forest; activities within 100m of the RF boundary may be restricted
   - **Karnataka Forest Act 1963** — State-level restrictions on RF-adjacent land
   - **Railway Act** — Separate setbacks from railway tracks (independent of forest rules)

### Action Plan Given to User

1. ✅ **KFD GIS portal** (https://kgis.ksrsac.in/kfd/) — overlay Sy. 40/41 on forest layer to confirm boundary
2. ✅ **DCF Office, Bangalore Urban Division** (Aranya Bhavan, Malleswaram) — get a distance certificate confirming exact distance from Makali Durga RF boundary to the survey numbers
3. ✅ **RTC records** from Doddaballapur Talati — verify Sy. 40/41 has no forest/gomal classification
4. ✅ **Railway Act setback** — separate check independent of forest rules

**Bottom line delivered:** The Bannerghatta ESZ notification (uploaded document) doesn't apply to your land. Makali Durga is a Reserve Forest, so the 1 km construction ban doesn't trigger. But verify RF boundary distance through the DCF office before planning development.

## General Workflow for Any Land/Forest Assessment

### Step 1: Identify the Forest

Ask the user for:
- The name of the forest/range they believe is adjacent
- The survey numbers and village (for GIS overlay)
- The taluk and district

Search Drive for any existing forest notifications, ECs, or documents in the project folder that mention the forest.

### Step 2: Classify the Forest

If you can't determine the classification from available data:

1. **Google search** for `[Forest Name] + "National Park" / "Wildlife Sanctuary" / "Reserve Forest"`
2. Check if a published **ESZ notification** exists for that NP/WS (MoEFCC website or India Code)
3. Check **Karnataka Forest GIS** at https://kgis.ksrsac.in/kfd/ (may require browser tool)
4. Check the **Karnataka Gazette** for forest notifications

### Step 3: Determine Applicable Rules

| Forest Type | Rules to Check |
|------------|---------------|
| National Park / Wildlife Sanctuary | MoEFCC ESZ notification (if published) + SC 2022 1 km default + Wildlife Protection Act 1972 |
| Reserve Forest | Indian Forest Act 1927 + Karnataka Forest Act 1963 + DCF distance certificate |
| Protected Forest | Same as Reserve Forest + check state-specific rules |
| Unclassed Forest | Revenue rules (Tahsildar) + local planning authority (BMRDA/BBMP/DTCP) |

### Step 4: Communicate the Answer

Structure:
1. **What does NOT apply** (if user referenced a wrong document or assumed rules)
2. **What DOES apply** (correct classification and specific restrictions)
3. **Action items** (specific steps with where to go and what to ask for)
4. **Separate restrictions** (Railway Act, BMRDA zonal regulations, etc.)

## Key Delivery Principles

- **Never conflate Reserve Forest rules with NP/WS ESZ rules** — they are completely different legal regimes
- **Never assume one forest notification applies to a different forest** — each NP/WS has its own ESZ notification
- **Lead with what DOESN'T apply** when the user has a mistaken assumption (e.g., "this uploaded document is for Bannerghatta NP, not Makali Durga RF")
- **Always give a concrete action plan** — the user needs to know WHERE to go and WHAT to ask for, not just the legal theory
- **Flag Railway Act separately** — land near railway tracks has independent restrictions that user may not have considered
- **Recommended KFD GIS** as the first step — it's free, quick, and gives definitive boundary overlay
