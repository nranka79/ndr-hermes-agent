# Karnataka Labour Dept notices & BOCW cess — applicability and response

**When to use.** A DRAAS project receives a notice from the Office of the Labour Officer / Cess
Assessment Authority (Karnataka), typically from Karmika Bhavan, Dairy Circle, asking for labour
records and registers and/or assessing building-and-other-construction-workers (BOCW) cess.
Ranka Amber precedent (25.08.2026) arrived as **two letters in one scan**:

1. **Kannada records-production notice** (No. `KaaBem-3/Vi.Ka.Ka/Notice/2026-27`) — produce records
   and registers of the last 01 year for verification under six labour statutes.
2. **English cess assessment notice** (No. `LOB-3/CESS/2026-27`) — cess under **s.3 of the BOCW
   Welfare Cess Act 1996** read with **GO ID-300/LET-2000 dated 18.01.2007**: liable at **1% of
   total construction cost**; furnish documents within **10 days** (a–g list) plus audited Balance
   Sheets and Schedules of the last **3 financial years** under s.7 r/w Rule 10; warns of **ex
   parte assessment** on non-response.

Unlike a BMTF police notice (see `references/bmtf-police-notice-and-bnss35-defence.md`), this is
**genuine statutory machinery** — do NOT ignore it; non-response triggers ex parte assessment on
their numbers. Respond on paper within the window.

## 1. The six statutes and their real thresholds

| Statute | Registration / obligation | Applicability threshold | Notes for a small site |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BOCW (RECS) Act 1996** | s.7 registration certificate; Form-I/IV, Form-X beneficiaries, Form-XXXV workers register, wage books, muster rolls, service certs, medical records | **10+ building workers** employed, or on any day in the **preceding 12 months** — s.1(4) | At 5–6 workers: **not applicable**. No s.7 registration, no Form-I/IV/XXXV, no beneficiary registers |
| **BOCW Welfare Cess Act 1996** | Cess at **1% of cost of construction** (s.3; up to 2% as notified — Karnataka at 1%) | **No worker-strength threshold** — applies to every employer | Collected **through the local authority at approval** (s.3(2) advance collection). See §2 — usually already paid |
| **Contract Labour (R&A) Act 1970** | Principal-employer registration + contractor licence; Form 16/17/18/19/24/83, employment cards, notices | **20+ workmen** as contract labour, or on any day of the preceding 12 months — s.1(4)(a) | At 5–6 workers: **not applicable**. Exemptions also exist for work of an intermittent/casual nature |
| **Minimum Wages Act 1948** | Pay not less than the Karnataka minimum wage schedule; maintain **Form-I** (attendance & wages) and **Form-VI** (wage slips) | Construction ("building operations") is a **scheduled employment in Karnataka** — applies **regardless of headcount** | No threshold exemption, but compliance is cheap and administrative. Have the contractor maintain the registers |
| **Karnataka Industrial Establishments (National & Festival Holidays) Act 1963** | Display the year's holiday list in **Kannada + English** at site; holiday wages | Applies to industrial establishments (incl. shops/commercial establishments/workplaces) — every "employee" | Low-cost to comply; usually only a records/display item in the notice |
| **Inter-State Migrant Workmen Act 1979** | Registration + licences + registers | **5+ inter-state migrant workmen** — s.1(4)(a) | **The one act where 5 is the threshold.** Bites only if those workers came from outside Karnataka. If all are local Bengaluru labour → not applicable. Needs a **factual** answer from the contractor, not just a headcount |

**Net position at a 5–6 worker site:** of the six, only the Cess Act (already paid), Minimum Wages
(administrative) and the holidays display genuinely touch the project. The BOCW-RECS, CLRA and ISMW
registration/licensing machinery does not apply at that strength. The notice is largely a fishing
expedition — answer each head expressly, with the threshold, rather than staying silent.

**Two factual traps** (do not answer these from headcount alone):
- Every threshold reads "or on any day in the preceding 12 months" — a single spike month can pull
  a project in.
- ISMW turns on the **origin** of the workers, not their number relative to 5.

## 2. THE strongest answer: labour cess is collected at plan sanction and is usually already paid

In Karnataka the 1% BOCW cess is **collected by BBMP/GBA at the plan-sanction stage** — precisely
the s.3(2) advance-collection mechanism. Before drafting any reply, look for the proof:

**Drive path: `<Project>` → `Approvals` → `Demand Notes`.**

Ranka Amber worked example (both filed in that folder):
- **Demand note** `BBMP/EoDB/CH/5255/26-27`, dated **27.04.2026**, file `GBA/BECC/0540/25-26`
  (against LP No. `GBA/MDP/DDTP/0007/26-27`) — "Labour cess amount **₹6,25,000**".
- **Payment receipt** `BBMP/EoDB/RC/5754/26-27`, **27.04.2026**, transaction ID `114493156453`,
  ₹6,25,000, status Success.
- The companion sanction-fees demand note (`CH/5254`) also shows "1% of Labour cess amount to BBMP
  ₹6,250" — that is the **collection charge on the cess**, part of the same sanction fees, not a
  second cess.

**So the reply leads with: the cess stands paid in advance to the local authority at plan sanction,
and the demand note + receipt are enclosed.** That single fact answers the LOB-3/CESS assessment.

## 3. What to demand from the civil contractor

The registers demanded are the contractor's to maintain. Pull them via the project's contractor
(identify from the Kelsa PO-WO vendor master for the project — for Ranka Amber the turnkey civil
contractor is Vardhan Ventures, PO-739) and ask for **every item under every head listed in the
notice**, with a per-item commitment date:

- BOCW Cess: Form-I, Form-IV, approved plan, OC, estimated-cost documents
- BOCW (RECS): s.7 registration certificate, Form-X (beneficiaries), Form-XXXV (workers register),
  wage books, muster rolls, service certificates, medical records
- Contract Labour: licence, Form 16/17/18/19/24/83, employment cards, statutory notices
- Minimum Wages: Form-I (attendance & wages), Form-VI (wage slips)
- Holidays Act: the current-year holiday list displayed in Kannada + English
- ISMW: registration, licences, registers — **plus a written confirmation whether any worker is
  from outside Karnataka**
- General: daily headcount record, sub-contractor list

**Standing rule to impose on the contractor:** compliant, current labour records become a **minimum
prerequisite for clearing any invoice** on the project, with records refreshed **fortnightly, and
at least monthly**, for the life of the project. Invoices without them are held pending.

## 4. Response shape

1. Enclose the cess demand note + payment receipt; state the cess is paid at plan sanction.
2. Answer **each** head of the notice, stating the applicable threshold and the site's actual
   headcount (and worker origin, for ISMW).
3. For heads that do not apply, say so expressly with the statutory provision — do not go silent.
4. For heads that do apply (minimum wages registers, holiday display), supply the records.
5. Ask that assessment be kept in abeyance pending the records, so the 10-day clock does not mature
   into an ex parte assessment.
6. The reported construction cost / valuation is the real battleground on quantum — the 3-year
   audited balance-sheet demand usually softens once a records reply is on file.
