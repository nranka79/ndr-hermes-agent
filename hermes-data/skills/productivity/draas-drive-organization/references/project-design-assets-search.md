# Project Design Assets + Architect Deliverables Search

Use when NDR says "pull out all the plans / renders / concepts we made for <project>, and anything <architect> has made" — a recurring class for villa/layout projects (Palya, RoVilla, Oasis, Amber…). Deliver the asset inventory WITH a delivered-vs-promised status check, because the architect usually acknowledges the assignment by email but may not yet have filed anything on Drive.

## Search ladder (validated 2026-08-25, Palya — Sinchana Gowda)

1. **Drive name search** — `name contains '<project>'` across accounts (google-draas first). Look for:
   - A TMP working folder (`TMP/<project>`) holding OUR concept artifacts: site sketches (`<Project>_Site_Sketch_Variant_N.jpg`), satellite boundary overlays (`<project>_satellite_green_vN.jpg`), concept renders (`variantN.png`, `refineN.png`), and R&D reference renders (competitor elevations grouped by style: modern/greco/english skyline+poolside).
   - A land/title folder (e.g. `PALYA ASHOK JEE LAND`) with tippan, surveys, ECs, RTCs, sale deed — these are the boundary/legal inputs the architect needs.
   - Financial models (IRR / land-cost realization sheets) — usually named with project + acreage (`Palya_2A6G_...`).
2. **Owner-email search for the architect** — `'<arch@email>' in owners and trashed=false` finds everything they own on the shared Drive. Do NOT expect their deliverables under the project name — architects file under their own scheme (Serenity/Amber/Oasis files, `RENDERS NEW` folders, `.rvt`/`.dwg` sets). Zero "Palya"-named hits from Sinchana (sgowda@draas.com) was the signal her deliverables weren't filed yet.
3. **Gmail assignment thread** — `q='<project> from:<arch@email>'` (and `q='<project> in:anywhere'`) finds the assignment brief NDR sent and the architect's ack. The brief names the real deliverables + links (site boundary CAD, block layout, SketchUp/Revit 3D) and usually CCs relevant HoDs. This thread is the source of truth for WHAT was promised and BY WHEN.
4. **Delivered-vs-promised check** — after the ack date, re-scan (a) architect-owned Drive files modified since, (b) emails from them since. Report plainly: "assigned 12 Aug, acked 17 Aug 'block plans by tomorrow', but as of <today> nothing filed on Drive / no further email — still pending, worth a nudge." Offer to send the follow-up on the thread.

## Pitfalls

- **The architect's Drive files won't match the project name.** Sinchana's 90+ files are all Serenity/Amber/Oasis — search by her owner email, not by project keyword.
- **The TMP folder mixes projects.** The Palya TMP folder also holds `rovilla_*` reference renders — separate the target project's assets from reference/competitor material in the summary; don't dump whole-folder contents as "ours".
- **"Plans" means different things.** Survey maps (Village Map, SY-1..5, MR views) are title/legal inputs, site sketches are our concepts, RERA building plans are competitor R&D (RoVilla RERA Prelim folder) — categorize by purpose so NDR sees which planning inputs the architect has vs which he still owes.
- **Assignment email left open loop for cost decision** — the Palya brief asked Kantesh/Anbu to opine mass-excavated basement vs driveway-only basement; surface open questions like that in the summary.
