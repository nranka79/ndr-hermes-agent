**File:** `Floor_Plan_Design_Compliance_Checklist_Formatted.pdf` — prepared by Sinchana and sent
to Bhuvanesh 25-Aug-2026 (Gmail thread `1a0376ccf96f4e1e`, subject
`Floor_Plan_Design_Compliance_Checklist_Formatted`, attachment only — it was NOT on Drive).
The header says "Project Type: 1500 sq.ft. Units" and section 3 cites Tamil Nadu bylaws — it was
built for the Oasis/Hosur villas, but NDR treats it as **the** DRA floor-plan checklist and wants
it applied to Ranka North Star too.

Uploaded to Drive **TMP** on 2026-09-17 as
`20260825_Floor_Plan_Design_Compliance_Checklist.pdf`
(id `1fW1wAdrLwoTZ_cBvoGvfix78TTUayyLC`) and shared reader with the DRA review team + AJ Architects.

Its 11 sections: 1 Vastu compliance (no toilet NE/SE, toilets in N don't touch NE, master bedroom
SW, kitchen SE, terrace headroom SW, laundry SW) · 2 Building form & massing (square/rectangular
plan, no NE cut, projection only NE, no SE projection) · 3 Setback compliance · 4 Toilet minimum
dimensions · 5 Parking compliance · 6 **Floor plan–elevation–3D alignment (window positions match
plan & elevation, door sizes match across drawings, balcony projections coordinated, facade
elements aligned)** · 7 Sliding-door standardisation (all 8'-0") · 8 Structural & balcony check ·
9 Levels & drawing clarity · 10 **North point & tagging check (north point correct and consistent,
all door tags corrected and consistent, room tags aligned, no overlapping annotations)** ·
11 Special correction block for the east-facing unit.

**Beyond the checklist, NDR names these explicitly** — always add them to a review request:
1. **Privacy between units** — no two windows of adjoining/facing units looking into each other.
2. **Door positions and swings** correct, not conflicting with each other or with circulation.
3. **Vastu compliance unit by unit** — or at minimum, no Vastu red flags in any unit.
