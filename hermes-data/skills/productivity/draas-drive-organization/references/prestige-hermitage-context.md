## Pitfalls

- The `Hermitage BROCHURE_Low Res.pdf` has **no text layer** — `pdftotext` returns only page separators. The official cloudfront brochure (`1639479206_brochure.pdf`) is Illustrator-generated with text and is the one to OCR; its typical-floor-plan page carries the SBA line.
- `site_and_floor_plans.pdf` floor plans are low-detail marketing drawings — room dims only, no area statement. Don't expect carpet area there.
- Sales deals may have two docs per unit (`DRA Asset Sale - Personal Guarantee - ...` and `DRA Asset Sale SD <name> ...`) — the SD doc is the actual deed; the guarantee is a separate letter.
