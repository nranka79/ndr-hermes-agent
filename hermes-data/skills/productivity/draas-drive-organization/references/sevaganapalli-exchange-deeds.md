## Gotchas

- The deed filename's doc number/date can be WRONG (see #1). Trust the deed body, not the name.
- `167/1I` is sold-deed, never exchanged (see
  `references/ranka-oasis-survey-extent-extraction.md`).
- Boundary descriptions in the A/B schedules are the cheapest way to confirm you have the
  right instrument before OCRing all 34 pages.
- These are pure image scans (no text layer). `pdftoppm` + `tesseract --psm 3`; the schedule
  in a Sevaganapalli deed typically lands in the first third of the page count.
