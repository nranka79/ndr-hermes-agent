# Detailed Project Report (DPR) — Master Template

Standard 10-section structure for DRA Group project DPRs. Fill from the investor-portfolio project tab + firm dossiers + project folders. Red-italic placeholders for anything not on file (never invent).

## Executive Summary
- Project Overview: Title, location, type (residential, commercial, industrial, infrastructure), and developer profile.
- Key Financial Highlights: Total Project Cost, Debt-Equity Ratio, IRR, DSCR, NPV.
- Funding Requirement: Loan amount requested, proposed tenure, moratorium period, security offered. (If not finalised: italic note "to be finalised with lender; indicative structure in Section 9.")

## 1. Developer & Promoter Profile
- 1.1 Background & Track Record: company history, past projects, sq.ft delivered (group deck + dossiers).
- 1.2 Legal Entity & Management Structure: constitution, directors/promoters, key management, org structure.
- 1.3 Financial Soundness: promoter net worth, last 3 years audited financials, credit ratings, banking relationships.

## 2. Project Description & Scope
- 2.1 Land & Location Details: location/address, plot area, connectivity; ownership status (freehold/leasehold/dev-right + title certificate details); zoning, master-plan alignment, conversion status.
- 2.2 Project Specifications: BUA, saleable/carpet area; towers/blocks/floors, unit breakdown; amenities, parking, green-building standards.

## 3. Regulatory Approvals & Statutory Clearances
- Table: Approval | Status (Obtained / Filed / In process / Not available) | Reference / No. | Drive link.
- Rows: Title Clearance Report; Sanctioned Plan / Master Layout; Environment Clearance; RERA; Fire NOC; Airport Authority NOC; PCB NOC; Utility commitments (Water, Electricity, Sewage); project-specific (BESCOM, e-Khata, EC, Thasildar, Adangal).

## 4. Technical & Engineering Aspects
- 4.1 Architectural & Engineering Design (structural standards, MEP planning).
- 4.2 Project Execution Strategy (EPC / item-rate / in-house).
- 4.3 Contractor & Consultant Details (architect, structural, MEP, main contractor — only what's on file).
- 4.4 Project Timeline (Gantt/bar chart; construction start / expected completion from portfolio tab).

## 5. Market Analysis & Commercial Viability
- 5.1 Industry & Micro-Market Overview (demand-supply, absorption).
- 5.2 Competitor Analysis (benchmark pricing/velocity — placeholder unless researched).
- 5.3 Marketing & Sales Strategy (segment, pricing, pre-launch, channel partners, sales schedule).

## 7. Financial Estimates & Projections
- 7.1 Revenue Assumptions: price per sq.ft (achieved / assumed), revenue schedule by milestone.
- 7.2 Cash Flow Statements (quarterly/annual — placeholder unless Excel model exists).
- 7.3 Profitability Statement (projected income statement).
- 7.4 Balance Sheet Projections (placeholder).
- 7.5 Key Financial Viability Metrics: DSCR (gross/average), Project IRR & Equity IRR, Break-even % (placeholders unless computed).

## 8. Risk Analysis & Mitigation Plan
- Technical: construction delays, material escalation, contractor default.
- Commercial/Market: slower absorption, price drop.
- Regulatory: approval/NOC delays.
- Mitigations: fixed-price contracts, escalation clauses, contingency buffers, escrow/TRA mechanism.

## 9. Security & Collateral Proposed
- 9.1 Primary Security: first mortgage on project land + present/future structures.
- 9.2 Hypothecation: receivables, sales proceeds, cash flows via Escrow / Trust & Retention Account.
- 9.3 Collateral Security: additional land/property/liquid security (negotiable).
- 9.4 Guarantees: personal guarantees of promoters / corporate guarantees of group entities.

## 10. Annexures & Enclosures
1. Corporate Documents: MoA, AoA, COI, Board Resolutions for borrowing.
2. Financial Records: last 3 years audited financials of borrower + promotor companies.
3. Legal & Land Documents: title search report (13–30 yrs), sale deeds, development agreements, mutation.
4. Sanction Plans & Maps: approved site/layout/floor plans, elevations.
5. Cost Estimates: BOQ certified by Chartered Engineer / QS.
6. Detailed Cash Flow Sheet: month-by-month Excel model.

Footer: "Prepared by [group] internal team using [sources, date]. Draft — figures are management estimates, subject to lender appraisal."
