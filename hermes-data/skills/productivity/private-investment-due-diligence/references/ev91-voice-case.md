## Model red flags (from their own Excel)

- FY26-27 projection: rev ₹144.63 Cr, EBITDA ₹8.82 Cr (6.1%), PAT ₹0.88 Cr (0.6%) — contradicted by MIS (see above).
- Equity utilisation (₹20 Cr): ₹5 Cr WC, ₹10 Cr capex (1,538 EVs @ ₹65K — aggressive for B2B e-scooter), **₹1 Cr Sun Mobility dues, ₹3 Cr statutory dues, ₹1 Cr adhoc** → 25% of raise clears arrears; the ₹2 Cr ask ≈ GST+TDS outstanding → bridge money, not growth.
- Model quality: #REF!/#ERROR! cells; sheets showing different vehicle counts (4,123 vs 2,660–2,727); some months rider payout + margin > rider revenue by ~2.5%.
- Capex payback: ₹10 Cr capex → ₹19.6 L/mo gross margin → ~51 months to recoup; dep alone ~₹2 Cr/yr.

## Verdict pattern (reusable)

"Decline in current form; conditional yes ONLY if: secured NCD + vehicle hypothecation (specific VINs, RTO endorsement) + PG from all directors + 15–20% collections escrow + CHG-1 registration within 30 days + 20–24% coupon + CPs (equity round closed at an evidenced valuation, statutory dues current, audited FY25/FY26 AFS, charge registry clean, hand loans being refinanced by equity). Kill criteria: round doesn't close by firm date, statutory dues unpaid at disbursement, existing charges unregistered, collateral already hypothecated, audited numbers contradict MIS/deck, founders refuse escrow/PG/charge, ₹200 Cr valuation not evidenced by signed term sheet."
