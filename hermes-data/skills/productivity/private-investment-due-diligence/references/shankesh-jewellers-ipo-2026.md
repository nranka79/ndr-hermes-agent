## Verdict reached
Reasonable long-term story at a fair price: genuine growth/margin trajectory, asset-light B2B model with blue-chip retailer clients, debt-repayment catalyst (₹158 Cr of ~₹145-167 Cr borrowings), ~12.8x FY26 with 50%+ RoNW. Risks: customer/supplier/geographic concentration, 100% outsourced manufacturing, ~7% returns, negative FY25 OCF, working-capital intensity, promoter OFS at massive gain, aggressive ₹5,000 Cr FY29 target, modest GMP = no listing-pop edge. "Selective apply for a 2-3 yr hold; not a pop trade."
