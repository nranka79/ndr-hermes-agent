## Notes

- P/E multiples for real estate companies vary widely because of POCM (Percentage of Completion Method) accounting — reported earnings often diverge from cash flows
- EV/EBITDA is more reliable than P/E for real estate, as it removes the effect of different debt levels and depreciation
- High-growth companies (Signature Global, smaller developers) often trade at lower P/E initially as earnings catch up to market cap
- Geographic concentration (single-city developers) typically trades at a discount to diversified pan-India developers
- For private company valuation → IPO, expect a 2-4x liquidity premium over private DCF valuation (which includes DLOC + DLOM discounts)
