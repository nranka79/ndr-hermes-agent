## Takeaways for future briefs

- Row-villa FAR precedent in Karnataka: 0.66–2.25. Dense row-houses (2.0–2.25)
  happen on small parcels (0.37–1.93 ac); large-acreage villa communities run
  low FAR (0.66–0.74). A 2.15 ac parcel at 1.8 is aggressive but within
  demonstrated precedent (Ashish 2.25 at 0.86 ac; SunCrest 2.0 at 1.93 ac).
- The basement-parking row-villa concept (cars->basement, villas accessed from
  basement, entertainment/store/office room in basement, G+1 above — the
  MIMS Espacio pattern) is the design anchor for Palya; brief sent to Arvind
  Jain, A.J. Architects 2026-08-25 (draft; CC Sinchana Gowda sgowda@draas.com).
  The Palya IRR model (20260225 ... 2A6G RowHouses IRR, spreadsheet
  `1nc2jdtG1qWydjLh0Bln7VQE-d1vlXEk0riJ0WZzdAU0`) still assumes FAR 1.05 @
  ₹12,000/sf, 83 units x 2,200 sf — needs refresh if the 1.8 @ ₹15,000/sf
  brief becomes the plan.
