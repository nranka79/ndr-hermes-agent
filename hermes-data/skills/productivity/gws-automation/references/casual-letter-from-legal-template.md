# Casual / Friendly Letter Drafting from Legal Templates

When a user asks to convert a formal legal agreement into a casual, friendly letter among familiar parties (e.g., shareholders in mediation, family members, long-term partners), use this transformation guide.

## When to Use

The user requests language that is "casual", "friendly", "not legal", "just something we all agree to", "shouldn't look like a legal contract" — while preserving the exact same intent and operative provisions.

## Transformation Table

| Legal Element | Casual Equivalent |
|---|---|
| Title: "INTERIM COOPERATION AGREEMENT" | "A Quick Note — Just to Have This on Record" |
| "THIS AGREEMENT is entered into among…" | "To all of us — [names]" |
| "The Shareholders / the Company / the Confirming Party" | "all of us", "we", first names |
| "WHEREAS:" / recitals | Background paragraph starting "As you know…" |
| Numbered clauses (1., 2., 3.) | Flowing paragraphs with natural breaks |
| "Each Shareholder agrees as follows:" | "We all agree to the following:" |
| Defined terms: "the Company", "the Property" | "our company", "the property" |
| "Applicable Law", "fiduciary duties under Applicable Law" | "as required by law", "as per the rules" |
| Bullet points / sub-clauses (a), b), c)) | Comma-separated list in a sentence |
| "For the avoidance of doubt" | "just to be clear" |
| "Irrespective of the completion of…" | "Even after…" |
| "Witnesseth" / formal recital verbs | "This note is simply to put down in writing" |
| Signature blocks with defined roles | Same signature blocks (keep for execution) |

## Structural Changes

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Numbered clauses + sub-clauses | 3-5 flowing paragraphs |
| Multiple "whereas" recitals | One paragraph of background context |
| Defined terms table | Plain English descriptions inline |
| Specific cross-references ("Section 5") | Direct description ("the representations above") |
| Formal closing ("IN WITNESS WHEREOF") | Simple closing ("Signed by each of us") |

## Key Principles

1. **Same intent, different voice**: Every operative provision must survive — just said differently. Don't drop coverage.
2. **Use first-person plural**: "we", "us", "our" replaces "the Shareholders", "the Parties"
3. **Address parties by name or role**: "Ranjeeth, Roshni, Nishant, Dharmesh, and Manish" not "the Shareholders"
4. **Keep signature blocks unchanged**: They exist for execution — leave the signatory lines, witness lines, role descriptions as-is
5. **Title signals the tone**: Call it a "Note", "Record of Understanding", "Letter of Intent" — never "Agreement" or "Contract" if casual
6. **Background context**: Start with "As you know…" rather than "WHEREAS"

## Example — Opening Paragraphs

**Legal original:**
> "This Interim Cooperation Agreement (the 'Agreement') is entered into among [Parties]… The Company is presently engaged in preparation for an initial public offering… During the period leading up to either the execution of such Shareholders' Agreement or the completion of the buy-out… the Shareholders wish to formalise certain cooperation commitments so that the Company may continue its ordinary course operations…"

**Casual rewrite:**
> "As you all know, the Company is preparing for an IPO, and at the same time we are working through discussions on a Shareholders' Agreement and Manish's exit. This note is simply to put down in writing what we've all discussed and agreed orally — so there's no confusion on the basics while we work through the bigger picture."

**Legal operative clause:**
> "1. Cooperation. Until the earlier of (a) execution of the Shareholders' Agreement… or (b) completion of the buy-out… each Shareholder will extend reasonable and timely cooperation on all statutory, procedural and ordinary course matters of the Company — including KYC, statutory declarations, dematerialisation of equity shares, consents to short notice of meetings, attendance for quorum, IPO process documents and signing of routine corporate documents…"

**Casual rewrite:**
> "Until the SHA is finalised or Manish's shareholding is bought out — whichever happens first — we all agree to the following: We will all cooperate fully and promptly on everyday procedural matters that the Company needs to keep things moving. Things like KYC, statutory declarations, dematerialisation of shares, consents for short-notice meetings, showing up for quorum, signing routine corporate documents, and anything else the Company reasonably needs from any of us."

## Formatting for Casual Documents

- **Single column, flowing paragraphs** — no numbered lists or bullet columns
- **Font**: Arial 11pt or 12pt (friendlier than Times New Roman or Bookman Old Style)
- **Title**: Bold, centered, 14pt max
- **Section breaks**: Blank lines between paragraphs, not numbered headings
- **No all-caps text** (avoid "PARTIES", "WHEREAS", "NOW THEREFORE")
- **Signature blocks**: Keep as-is for execution purposes
