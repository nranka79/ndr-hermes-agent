## What I had to learn the hard way

1. **The three "named" opinions are not all JDA opinions.** The 2024-10-19 J Sudha Reddy opinion covers SLP-owned land (158/1C3, 158/1C4, 158/1C6, 158/1C9A, 167/2C). The 2021-01-19 D M Shiva Shankar "kencha reddy" opinion covers DRA-owned land (176/1B, 176/1B2, 176/2B, 177/1A). The 2024-09-26 CMS IndusLaw 7.22-acre opinion covers SLP-owned land. None of the three is a JDA-specific legal opinion.

2. **"Industrial" in the voice note meant "Indus Law"** — the law firm CMS IndusLaw. ndr corrected this. Voice transcription of proper nouns is unreliable; always check the file names on Drive for the canonical spelling.

3. **The IndusLaw Preliminary Title Report is the strongest single document for the Ramesh Reddy JDA parcels** because it walks the title chain through 168/1B explicitly and names the Pattadhar. The JDA agreements add the schedule of properties and title warranty. The SLP/owned-land opinions add nothing to the JDA title story.

4. **The K Harish JDA parcels are an exposure** — no advocate opinion exists. This is a real gap for the Jiraaf pitch. Action: ask Nishant Prakash to commission a JDA-specific legal opinion from a Hosur-based advocate (e.g. Adv. J Sudha Reddy or Adv. N Manjunath) before sending the deck.

5. **OCR pipeline took 3-5 min per big opinion.** The 63-page IndusLaw report was the bottleneck. Background it with `notify_on_complete=True` if the rest of the analysis can proceed on the smaller opinions first.
