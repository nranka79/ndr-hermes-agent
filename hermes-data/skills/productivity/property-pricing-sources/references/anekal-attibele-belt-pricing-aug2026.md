## Land / Per-Acre Benchmarks

- Guidance: Anekal periphery ₹2,500–6,000/sqft; agri ₹500–2,500/sqft → ₹2.2–10.9 Cr/acre
- Market farmland Anekal–Agalakotta ₹1–2 Cr/acre; Hosur outskirts ₹1.5–2.5 Cr/acre
- Bestamanahalli 120-acre ask ₹7 Cr/acre (Instagram outlier, unverified)
- Converted/approved plot land: Anekal ₹13–19.6 Cr/acre; Attibele ₹17.4–26.6 Cr/acre
- Context: the Sanchaya Lands deal (27A front @ ₹4.25 nominal, blended ~₹4 Cr/acre) sits well
  below converted-land market, above agri — consistent with an aggregation play.
