## 1. Probationary Period
- 6 months from date of joining
- Extendable by another 6 months if performance is not satisfactory
- Management may terminate without notice during probation if performance is unsatisfactory
- On confirmation, a letter of confirmation will be issued

## 2. Confirmation
- Permanent appointment after probation period expires
- Letter of confirmation issued by authorised signatory

## 3. Business Hours and Holidays
- 8 hours/day Monday–Friday
- 6 hours on Saturday
- **Office timing: 10:00 am – 6:30 pm** (with 30-minute break)
- Minimum 8 hours Mon–Fri, 6 hours on Sat
- Extensions from time to time per management requirements

## 4. KELSA Sign In/Out and Attendance
- All employees sign in / sign out via KELSA from approved locations only
- KELSA is directly linked to payroll — salary is auto-computed from KELSA data
- Leave application must be produced 5 days in advance and approved by authorised signatory

## 5. Salaries and Incentives
- Salaries paid on the **7th of every month**
- Computed as per KELSA data
- Revised from time to time per management decision

## 6. Leave

**Types of leave:**
- Casual / Earned Leave: 12 days/year
- Medical / Sick Leave: 10 days/year
- Public Holidays as per company calendar

**General provisions:**
- Leave reckoned on calendar-year basis (Jan–Dec) after consultation with team heads
- Apply in writing to Sanctioning Authority
- Leave cannot be claimed as a right; management may refuse/defer/revoke
- Apply well in advance except in urgent cases / illness
- Extension requests via written/email (SMS/WhatsApp not entertained)
- Overstay of sanctioned leave → no salary for that period + disciplinary action
- Medical certificate may be required for medical leave
- **No long-weekend extensions on ad hoc basis** — apply 7 days in advance, else 2X deduction
- Ad hoc sick leaves after long weekends not acceptable except in special circumstances
- Handover charge to designated person before proceeding on leave

**Casual Leave:**
- 1 day/month worked, post-probation only
- Max 3 days at a time; >3 days requires 3 weeks prior application
- Leave without permission = 2X deduction

**Medical Leave:**
- 10 days/year
- Full emoluments during medical leave
- >2 days requires proof (doctor's prescription/note)
- Unused medical leave cannot be carried forward or encashed

**Maternity Leave (per Indian Maternity Benefit Act 1961):**
- 26 weeks paid leave for first two children (8 weeks pre-delivery + 18 weeks post)
- 12 weeks paid for each subsequent child

## 7. Termination and Resignation
- **<₹20,000/month:** 5-day notice period
- **≥₹20,000/month:** 30-day notice period
- Management can terminate with 5/15 or 30-day notice (or pay in lieu)
- Termination without notice/salary possible in cases of misconduct/misbehaviour

**Confidentiality clause (mandatory in every offer):**
- Treat company's business interests with utmost confidentiality, during and after employment
- Treat remuneration/salary confidentially — do not disclose to other employees except Directors
- Personal work of directors must be handled with confidentiality
- Breach → penalties / termination

## 8. Email Disclosures
- All company email is official record, not private property
- May be disclosed under legal proceedings, disciplinary, FOI
- Users responsible for actions on their account

## 9. Salary Advance
- Available after 5 years of service
- One-time, equivalent to 2x monthly salary
- Repayment period decided at disbursal

## 10. Code of Conduct (20 misconduct items)

Every employee is expected to demonstrate good conduct. The following are **misconduct**:

a. Engaging in outside trade/business that compromises quality of work
b. Unauthorised disclosure of company affairs
c. Unauthorised disclosure of customer / connected-person information
d. Drunkenness / riotous / disorderly / indecent behaviour on premises
e. Willful damage to company / customer property
f. Willful insubordination / disobedience of lawful order
g. Gambling or betting on premises
h. Abetment or instigation of any of the above
i. Giving or taking bribe or illegal gratification
j. Absence without leave / overstay of sanctioned leave
k. Irregular attendance
l. Neglect of work / negligence in duties
m. Breach of any rule / instruction for running the Department
n. Committing nuisance on premises
o. Entering / leaving premises except by designated entrance
p. Failing to show consideration / courtesy / attention / unseemly behaviour on duty
r. Marked disregard of ordinary requirements of decency and cleanliness in person or dress
s. Incurring debts to an extent considered excessive by Management
t. **Strict vegetarianism shall be followed in the premises**

## 11. Office Timings (strict, with late-coming penalty)

- Adhere to **10:00 am – 6:30 pm** unless explicitly exempted by Management in writing
- **Late Coming Penalty & Salary Deduction Policy** introduced for discipline
- Standard deductions for late comings (>=3 days/month late past 10:00 am = half-day absent)
- Same penalty structure applies to early logout / unauthorised early departure
- Every second instance of Uninformed Leave triggers further deduction
- Repeated late comings → disciplinary action
