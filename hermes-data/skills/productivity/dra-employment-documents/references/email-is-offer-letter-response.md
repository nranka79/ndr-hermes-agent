# "Email is the Offer Letter" — Candidate Response Pattern

## When to use

A candidate has received their offer via email, accepted it, but is asking for a **revised offer letter** (a separate printed/hard-copy document incorporating clarifications discussed). DRA Realty's policy is that the email itself constitutes the offer letter — no separate document is issued.

## Pattern

1. **Thank & acknowledge** — confirm acceptance and joining date
2. **Explain policy** — the email communication itself is the offer letter; no separate hard-copy is issued
3. **Summarise agreed terms** — position, CTC breakdown, joining date
4. **Open for further clarification**

## Verbatim template

```
Dear [Candidate],

Thank you for your kind acceptance and for sharing your joining preference. We are delighted to welcome you to the team and look forward to having you onboard on [joining date].

Regarding your request for a revised offer letter — please note that at DRA Realty Private Limited, the email communication itself serves as the offer letter. We do not have a policy of issuing separate printed or hard-copy offer letters. All the terms and conditions of your employment, including the clarifications shared in our previous emails, form part of this electronic record and are equally binding.

To summarise your terms of engagement:

• Position: As discussed
• CTC: ₹X,XXX per month (₹X,XXX fixed + ₹X,XXX attendance allowance)
• Joining Date: [day], [date]

Please feel free to reach out if you need any further clarification.

Warm regards,
Roshini Ranka
```
