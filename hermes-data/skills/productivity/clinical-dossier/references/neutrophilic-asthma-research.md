# Neutrophilic Asthma Research — Ruhaan Ranka (Jun 2026)

## Key Finding

Neutrophilic asthma (a Type 2-low / T2-low asthma phenotype) was the correct diagnosis for Ruhaan's persistent cough — missed in the initial dossier because of binary framing (habit cough vs small airways disease).

## Phenotype Features

- **Low FeNO** (<25 ppb) — no eosinophilic inflammation
- **Low/normal IgE** (<295 IU/mL) — no atopic marker elevation despite confirmed allergic sensitization
- **Steroid-resistant** — prednisolone 16mg × 5d did NOT settle cough
- **Less bronchodilator reversibility** (+2.2% BD)
- **Normal spirometry** (FEV1 93%) with isolated low FEF25-75
- **Viral triggers** — classic precipitant is viral URI
- **Responds to azithromycin** — anti-inflammatory, not antibiotic effect

## Why It Was Missed

See `clinical-dossier/references/neutrophilic-asthma-missed-differential.md` for analysis. Key reasons: binary framing, normal FeNO misinterpretation, steroid-resistance misattributed to habit cough.

## Key PubMed Research

### T2-Low Asthma Management (PMID 34688426)
*Ann Allergy Asthma Immunol.* 2021. DOI: 10.1016/j.anai.2021.05.027
- T2-low accounts for ~1/3 to 1/2 of all asthma
- Features: steroid resistance, less BD reversibility, neutrophilic inflammation
- Interventions: standard inhalers, **azithromycin**, lifestyle

### Azithromycin Mechanism

| PMID | Year | Finding |
|------|------|---------|
| 31759853 | 2019 | Suppresses Th1/Th2 chemokines IP-10/MDC |
| 27664570 | 2016 | Modulates dendritic cell + CD4+ T cell response |
| 25458910 | 2015 | Reduces IL-8, attenuates neutrophilic airway inflammation post-viral |

### Airway Microbiome (PMID 40535324)
*J Asthma Allergy.* 2025. DOI: 10.2147/JAA.S521800
- Distinct microbiome/metabolite profiles in EA vs NEA

### Azithromycin in Paediatrics (PMID 31629643)
*Paediatr Respir Rev.* 2020. DOI: 10.1016/j.prrv.2019.07.002
- Risk of antimicrobial resistance — use judiciously

### AMAZES Trial
- Azithromycin 500mg 3x/week reduced exacerbations by 40% in non-eosinophilic asthma
- Prophylactic: 250mg Mon/Wed/Fri for frequent breakthrough exacerbations

### Severe Therapy-Resistant Asthma (PMID 39596984)
*Medicina.* 2024. DOI: 10.3390/medicina60111799

### Vitamin D + Azithromycin Protocol (PMID 35418427)
- NCT05028153, NCT05043116
- Consider Vitamin D 1000-2000 IU/day adjunctive
