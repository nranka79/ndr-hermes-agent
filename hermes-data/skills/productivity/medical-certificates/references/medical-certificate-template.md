## Indian Insurance Certificate — Special Notes

For statin/medication declarations in Indian insurance contexts:

- State clearly it's **precautionary** based on **family history**, not active disease
- Mention **all blood parameters are normal** — lipid profile, sugar, liver function
- Clarify **no active cardiovascular condition** — this is the key concern for insurers
- Doctor should note **no hospitalization history** if true
- Keep it factual — don't over-explain or include extraneous medical detail
