### Required Documents

1. Original Claim Form (Form C) — filled & signed by policyholder
2. Original Discharge Summary
3. Original Itemized Hospital Bill
4. Original Payment Receipts (all payments made)

## B. Pre-operative / Pre-hospitalization Expenses

### Coverage
- Up to **30 days before** hospitalization
- Expenses directly related to the condition being treated
- Includes: consultant visits, diagnostic tests, medicines prescribed in that window

### How to Claim
- Submit together with the main hospitalization claim (reimbursement or cashless)
- **For cashless hospitalisation:** pay pre-op expenses upfront, then submit for reimbursement *after discharge* once the cashless claim is settled
- Keep originals of: consultation receipts, diagnostic bills & reports, pharmacy bills with prescriptions

### Required Documents
- Original consultation fee receipts
- Original diagnostic reports & payment receipts
- Original pharmacy bills + corresponding prescriptions
- Covering letter linking these to the main hospitalization event
- Copy of claim settlement statement (if cashless was used for admission)

## C. Cashless Claim (no upfront payment for hospitalisation)

### Planned Hospitalisation
1. Inform Medi Assist **3–7 days before** admission (portal / app / call 1800 345 3322)
2. Choose a network hospital (check on Medi Assist website/app)
3. Hospital TPA desk fills Pre-Authorization Form + medical records
4. Medi Assist reviews and sends **Authorization Letter** (usually within 24h)
5. You pay 20% co-payment at discharge; insurer pays 80% directly

### Emergency Hospitalisation
1. Get admitted to nearest network hospital immediately
2. Inform Medi Assist **within 24 hours** (ask hospital TPA desk to do it)
3. Hospital initiates pre-authorisation request retrospectively
4. Authorization letter issued upon approval

### Documents to Carry
- Royal Sundaram e-Health Card (policy number sufficient at most network hospitals)
- Photo ID (Aadhaar / PAN)
- Policy number: LLA0016946000107

## D. Disputing Claim Deductions / Underpayments

### Escalation Matrix

| Step | Contact | Expected Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Medi Assist** | 1800 345 3322 / claims@mediassistindia.com | Understand reason for deduction |
| **2. Royal Sundaram Grievance Officer** | grievance@royalsundaram.in | 15 days (mandatory acknowledgment within 3 working days) |
| **3. Chief Grievance Officer** | chiefgrievanceofficer@royalsundaram.in | Escalate if Step 2 unresolved in 15 days |
| **4. Insurance Ombudsman** | www.cio.org.in | After 30 days total / dissatisfied with Step 3 resolution |

### What to Include in Your Dispute Letter
- Policy number: **LLA0016946000107**
- Claim number (from settlement letter)
- Specific amount disputed vs. amount paid
- Supporting documents (hospital bills, TPA explanation letter)
- Confirm the **20% co-payment** was correctly applied — that is the *only* mandatory deduction

### When Co-Payment is the Issue
The policy has a **20% co-payment applied individually to Kanta D Ranka** (not a family deductible). This means:
- For every ₹10,000 of admissible claim, insurer pays ₹8,000, she pays ₹2,000
- If a deduction seems larger than 20%, request a detailed break-up
- The 20% is on the **admissible amount** after policy exclusions (e.g., consumables if not covered), so the total deduction may be: co-pay + excluded items
