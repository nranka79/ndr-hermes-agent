## Wall Finishes

| Method | Cost/sq ft | Pros | Cons | Who Does It |
|--------|-----------|------|------|-------------|
| Paint (standard emulsion) | Rs 25-60 | Cheapest, recolor anytime, any color | Plain look, chips over time | Painter |
| Paint (texture/roller) | Rs 60-120 | Adds depth, hides imperfections | Harder to repair/repaint | Skilled painter |
| Wallpaper | Rs 80-300+ | Patterns, textures, instant character | Peels in humidity (use vinyl in bathrooms) | Specialist or DIY pre-pasted |
| Wall cladding (wood/MDF/PVC) | Rs 150-500 | Premium look, durable, hides uneven walls | Reduces room size, trend-dependent | Carpenter |
| Stone/brick cladding | Rs 200-600 | Textural feature wall statement | Heavy (check structure), collects dust | Mason |
| Lime wash / Venetian plaster | Rs 150-400 | Natural matte breathable, upscale | Skilled applicator needed, costly | Specialist |
| 3D wall panels (gypsum/PVC) | Rs 150-350 | Instant architectural feature | Trend-dependent, dust in grooves | Carpenter/DIY |
| Tile on wall | Rs 80-250 | Durable, waterproof, easy clean | Cold, hard to change | Tile mason |

## Ceiling Treatments

| Type | Cost/sq ft | Pros | Cons |
|------|-----------|------|------|
| POP (Plaster of Paris) | Rs 60-120 | Cheap, any shape | Messy install, cracks, hard to repair |
| Gypsum board | Rs 80-180 | Clean install, fire-resistant, crack-resistant | Costs more than POP, needs 4" min drop |
| PVC / Stretch ceiling | Rs 100-250 | Waterproof (leak protection), gloss finish | Plastic look, limited colors |
| Exposed concrete (painted) | Rs 50-100 | Taller room feel, zero headroom loss | Needs even concrete pour, acoustic issues |
| Wooden ceiling | Rs 200-500 | Warm, premium acoustics | Expensive, termite risk, height loss |

**Recommendation for Bangalore:** Gypsum board ceiling for living/bedrooms (clean, crack-resistant). PVC/stretch only in bathrooms. Always pre-plan cove lighting channel, AC duct space, and curtain track recess before ceiling layout.

## Bathroom Waterproofing (Mandatory)

- Cementitious polymer-modified waterproofing compound
- Apply on floors + walls up to 6ft in wet zone (higher if adjacent wardrobe)
- Seal pipe penetrations with expanding sealant
- Floor slope: minimum 1:100 (1cm/m) toward drain
- Anti-skid tiles in wet zone (textured/matte finish)
- **2 coats**, 24hrs dry between coats, then 48-hour ponding test
- Bangalore brands: Dr. Fixit, Bostik, Fosroc, Weber

## Key Electrical Planning Notes

- Switch standard height: 4ft (120cm) from floor
- Bedside switches: 2.5ft (75cm) from floor
- Kitchen counter switches: 3.5ft (105cm) from floor
- Place sockets every 6ft along each wall
- Minimum 6-8 sockets in kitchen counter area
- One 15A point for microwave/oven
- USB charging sockets bedside (both sides)
- Vacuum cleaner charging dock point in living room
- Ethernet point in home office (more reliable than WiFi for VC)
- Separate lighting circuit + appliance circuit per room
