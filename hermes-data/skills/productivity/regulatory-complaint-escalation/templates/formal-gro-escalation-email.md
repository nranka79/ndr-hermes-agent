## Tone calibration

- **Polite but firm**: every sentence is true, every sentence is non-inflammatory
- **No exclamation marks** (except in quoted regulator language)
- **No sarcasm** (regulators read these and tone-deaf sarcasm loses them)
- **No "I have been a customer for N years"** — irrelevant to the regulator, only the law matters
- **No threats of legal action** beyond the specific regulatory venues — vague threats reduce credibility
- **Every claim has a citation** — either the counterparty's own PDF or the regulation section
- **Every demand is specific and dated** — not "please resolve this soon" but "by 18 July 2026"
- **The strongest complaint is the one a regulator reads and nods**
