# The 8 Teaser Hooks — Reusable Framework for WhatsApp Sales

Replace dead-end qualifying questions with curiosity-triggering teasers. Every teaser must be a **bite-sized compelling fact** — not a question — that makes the prospect's brain ask "why?" or "tell me more."

## Hook #1: 🏌️ Golf Course / Landmark Proximity

**Strength:** ★★★★★ — Best opener for most prospects
**Replaces:** "Are you investing or building a home?"
**Trigger condition:** Any expression of interest

**Primary message:**
> "Ranka Udaya sits right opposite Clover Greens — Bangalore's most revived golf course. They just 10x'd their membership under new management. Plots with a golf course view are rare around here."

**Follow-up if engaged:**
> "The course was underperforming for years — new management took over and turned it completely around. Membership went from 200 to 2,000 in under a year. Best course around Bangalore right now."

**Psychology:** Golf course = lifestyle + exclusivity + scarcity. The "revival" story adds momentum. No question asked, but the prospect instinctively wants to know more.

## Hook #2: 💰 Price Arbitrage

**Strength:** ★★★★★ — Best for price-sensitive or comparing prospects
**Replaces:** "What's your budget range?"
**Trigger condition:** User asks about price or budget

**Primary message:**
> "Quick fact that surprises most buyers: ₹4,000/sq ft here vs ₹10,000/sq ft 3km into Karnataka. Same Sarjapur growth corridor, same distance to ORR. The state border is the only difference."

**Follow-up angle:**
> "And TN registration is ~7%. Karnataka is 12-15%. On a 1,200 sq ft plot that's nearly ₹2L saved in stamp duty alone."

**Psychology:** Concrete numbers create cognitive dissonance. The brain wants to resolve "why is it cheaper?" The answer (border) becomes a feature, not a bug.

## Hook #3: 🏭 Major Infrastructure / Jobs

**Strength:** ★★★★☆ — Best for investment-focused prospects
**Replaces:** "Are you looking for investment or end-use?"
**Trigger condition:** User mentions investment, growth, returns

**Primary message:**
> "SWIFT City is coming up 1km across the border — a 1,000+ acre industrial park for high-tech industries. 20,000+ high-income workers who will all need housing within 5km."

**Follow-up angle:**
> "Battery tech, electric cars, biotech — these aren't low-wage jobs. These are people who can afford ₹15-20K rent. And there's almost no rental supply here yet."

**Psychology:** A massive economic driver creates FOMO. "Jobs coming = demand coming = prices going up" is intuitive even for non-investors.

## Hook #4: 🏨 Institutional Confidence (Hotel/Hospital)

**Strength:** ★★★★☆ — Best for skeptical or analytical prospects
**Replaces:** "Let me explain why this is a good investment..."
**Trigger condition:** User raises concerns about future value

**Primary message:**
> "A 200-key 5-star hotel is under construction right next door. Hotels don't get built without serious demand studies. Someone big is betting this area will fill up fast."

**Follow-up angle:**
> "A 200-key hotel employs roughly 1 staff per room. That's 200 permanent jobs plus construction workers needing housing right now. Hotel before residents means institutional confidence."

**Psychology:** External validation is powerful. The prospect doesn't have to trust the salesperson — the hotel developer's ₹200 Cr investment speaks for itself.

## Hook #5: 📊 Demand-Supply Economics

**Strength:** ★★★★☆ — Best for analytical/business-minded prospects
**Replaces:** "Let me share the appreciation potential..."
**Trigger condition:** User seems educated about real estate

**Primary message:**
> "38 plots. 10,000+ new residents expected in this corridor from SWIFT City alone. Add the hotel staff and support services — you're looking at 20,000+ people. Basic economics: 38 homes vs 20,000 people = massive appreciation pressure."

**Psychology:** Appeals to logic. The math is inescapable. Even a skeptical prospect can't argue with supply-demand mechanics.

## Hook #6: ⏳ Scarcity / Countdown

**Strength:** ★★★☆☆ — Use sparingly, only after other hooks
**Replaces:** "This offer won't last forever"
**Trigger condition:** Prospect is stalling, comparing, or "thinking about it"

**Primary message:**
> "We started with 38 plots. 10 booked in the first month. At this rate, we'll have single digits left in 3-4 weeks."

**Specific plot scarcity:**
> "The 2,400 sq ft southwest-facing plot you liked — it's on soft hold for another buyer until Thursday. I can reserve it if you decide."

**Psychology:** Real scarcity works. Fake urgency backfires. Only use this if the numbers are real.

## Hook #7: 💡 Social Proof

**Strength:** ★★★☆☆ — Best for prospects on the fence
**Replaces:** "What would it take to convince you?"
**Trigger condition:** Prospect has seen info but isn't moving

**Primary message:**
> "3 families booked plots this weekend after visiting. They all said the same thing — seeing the golf course from the land itself made the decision easy."

**Psychology:** Social proof is the most powerful persuasion mechanism. Specific, recent, relatable examples work better than "many buyers are choosing..."

## Hook #8: 📋 Legal / Approvals Clarity

**Strength:** ★★★☆☆ — Best for analytical/risk-averse prospects
**Replaces:** "Don't worry, everything is legal"
**Trigger condition:** Prospect asks about RERA, title, legal safety

**Primary message:**
> "RERA approved (TNRERA/30/LO/0642/2026), HNTDA approved, 100% freehold. Two independent legal opinions confirmed clear title. Happy to share the docs for your lawyer to review."

**Psychology:** Transparency builds trust. Offering documents for THEIR lawyer's review signals confidence. This reverses the typical "trust me" dynamic.

## Selection Matrix

| Prospect Behavior | Best First Hook | Backup |
|---|---|---|
| Said "interested" (no specifics) | Golf Course | Price Arbitrage |
| Asked about price | Price Arbitrage | Golf Course |
| Mentioned investment/returns | SWIFT City | Demand-Supply |
| Skeptical about location | Price Arbitrage | Institutional Confidence |
| "Thinking about it" | Scarcity | Social Proof |
| Asked about legality | Legal/Approvals | Price Arbitrage |
| Concerned about value | Demand-Supply | Hotel Confidence |
| Comparing to other projects | Price Arbitrage | Scarcity |

## Critical Rule

**Never use the same hook twice with the same prospect.** If a teaser doesn't get a response within 4-6 hours, switch to a DIFFERENT hook category. The brain habituates quickly — novel information cuts through, repetition trains ignore.
