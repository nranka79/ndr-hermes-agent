# Building NOC Requirements Summary (Bangalore — BBMP/BDA)

## RMP 2015 Zonal Regulation — Section 3.12 "No Objection Certificates" (Page 25)

The only NOCs explicitly listed in the Zonal Regulation:

| Clause | NOC Required | Condition |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| I | **KSPCB (Pollution Control Board)** | For Development Plans, Apartment buildings & Residential layouts under KSPCB-stipulated category |
| II | **Fire Force DC + KSPCB NOC** | For buildings with height ≥15m *(amended to **≥21m** via Karnataka Gazette No.437 dated 04-06-2026)* |
| IV | **Airport Authority of India** | Wherever applicable (height/zone restrictions near airports) |

**No mention** of BESCOM, BWSSB, or any built-up area threshold in Zonal Regulation Section 3.12.

### 2026 Amendment (Gazette No.437, 04-06-2026)
- Changed the height threshold in Section 3.12(ii) from 15.0m → **21.0m** for Fire Force + KSPCB NOC requirement
- Also redefined "High rise building" from ≥24m → **≥21m** height
- This means fewer buildings now trigger the mandatory Fire NOC requirement

## Bangalore Building Bye-Laws 2003 — Section 3.2(10) (Page 11)

Lists certificates required from authorities:

| Agency | Certificate Required For |
|--------|------------------------|
| **BWSSB** | N.O.C. in case of **high rise building** or **group housing** |
| **BESCOM** | N.O.C. in case of **high rise building** or **group housing** |
| Fire Services | N.O.C. in case of **high rise building** |
| Airport Authority | As applicable |
| BDA | Commencement certificate for change of land use |

**Key implication**: BESCOM and BWSSB NOCs are tied to the definition of "high rise building" — if the building is below the high-rise threshold and not group housing, these NOCs are not required under the bye-laws.

## Practical Note

The 30,000 sq.ft built-up area threshold the user mentioned is **not present** in any of the standard BBMP/BDA regulatory documents (RMP 2015 Zonal Regulation, Building Bye-Laws 2003, Model Building Bye-Law 2017, or BBMP Draft Building Bye-Laws 2026). This threshold may come from:
- A specific BBMP circular or notification (check Drive for circulars)
- An internal practice note from a liaisoning agent
- The KMC Act or rules thereunder

If asked again about "which NOCs are NOT required under 30,000 sqft", the correct answer is: the Zonal Regulation doesn't address this — reference the Building Bye-Laws 2003 which tie NOC requirements to "high rise building" and "group housing" status, not to sq.ft.
