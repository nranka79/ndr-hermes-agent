## NDR's standing positions (from the Apr 2026 CredWest risk matrix — treat as defaults)

1. **No setup/retainership fees** on zero-risk RERA-approved inventory (rejected ₹15L
   setup to CredWest; CBRE's ₹40L retainership also resisted).
2. **Staggered brokerage payout**: payout at sales-agreement execution capped at
   RERA-permissible forfeiture; balance on registration / 20% realization;
   clawback/adjustment on cancellation.
3. **Qualified lead definition**: unique family unit, educated on product type, price
   range and delivery timelines PRIOR to site visit; repeat/family/advisor visits = 1.
4. **Exclusivity linked to performance**: minimum qualified visits per target sale
   (~8–10/month), rolling quarterly review; on miss, DRA can alter scope (take over
   lead gen, limit partner to site closures) or terminate.
5. **All leads to DRA CRM within 48h**; no offline warehousing; transparency tooling
   (AI meeting transcription) at site for QA.
6. **Collateral pre-approval**: ads, digital, SMS/WhatsApp blasts, influencer content
   approved in writing by DRA marketing head before deployment.
7. **Lead lock-in / anti-poaching**: leads are DRA property; partner (and its other
   clients) prohibited from cross-selling other projects ≥ 6 months.
8. **Audit rights + direct interviews** with leads at any funnel stage; feedback must
   be implemented and demonstrable.
9. **Three-strikes auto-termination** (documented mis-selling, unreported leads,
   cross-selling). Leads already in CRM that convert post-termination still earn a
   pre-agreed fee — but capped.
10. **Price floor + escalation matrix**: no sales below agreed cutoff; escalate every
    N units (20 for large projects, ~2 for boutique).

## Structural options for a trial (recommendation order)

- **A. Milestone-linked "preferred partner"** (trial default): exclusivity over
  partner-tagged CRM leads only, NOT project-wide; full sole converts only on hitting
  gates. Kills the "DRA pays commission on its own demand" problem.
- **B. Lead-gen only**: partner feeds qualified leads; DRA site team presents /
  negotiates / closes → pay a reduced rate (2/3 of full).
- **C. Gated sole**: keep "sole" as bait, tied to marketing budget + hard milestones;
  exclusivity auto-lapses on a missed gate.

## Milestone gates NDR uses

- **60-day first-booking gate** (signed allotment + booking amount) — miss →
  exclusivity lapses / terminate with immediate effect (no notice period for
  performance failure)
- **N-month cumulative floor** (e.g. 3 bookings in 6 months) — renewal only on hit
- **Monthly funnel gate**: 8–10 qualified site visits per target sale, CRM-tracked
- 30-day notice retained ONLY for convenience exit

## Clause-level red flags in partner drafts (the poison clauses)

1. **"Credit for all sales regardless of source" + "all walk-ins routed to partner"**
   = DRA pays commission on its own demand (brand, website, RERA, signages create
   footfall). Carve out DRA-origin sales (website, own agents, referrals, walk-ins)
   during any trial.
2. **Exclusivity with zero commitments** — no min leads/visits/marketing spend.
   Demand minimums or re-structure non-exclusive.
3. **No lead definition / no CRM / no audit trail** → post-exit tail clauses
   ("initiated / introduced / facilitated") become a dispute factory. Credit only
   CRM-tagged leads; cap tail at ~90 days post-exit.
4. **Front-loaded fee** (100% at agreement execution) → stagger + clawback (position #2).
5. **Rate vs market**: 7% + GST is top-of-market for lead-gen only (benchmarks:
   CBRE 5% + ₹40L retainership full-stack; Bangalore channel partners 1–3% referral,
   4–6% full-stack). Convert to per-unit ₹ to make it visceral for NDR.
6. **Sloppy scaffolding**: non-legal-entity party name ("DRA Ranka Group" → must be
   DRA Realty Pvt Ltd / actual JDA SPV), blank unit numbers (scope ambiguity — fill
   them), blank notice emails, no GSTN exchange.
7. **No RERA agent registration clause** — verify the partner's registration status
   (Karnataka RERA requires agents to register).
8. **Price-discipline risk**: partner negotiates at site without a floor → discount
   creep below the established launch price. Require written DRA approval for any
   deviation; escalation matrix.

## Final negotiated term sheet — Ranka Amber × Proplion (Aug 2026, NDR-dictated)

The definitive structure NDR approved for the Proplion trial (6-month SOLE mandate, 1 Sep
2026 – 28 Feb 2027, 6 dev units). Reuse as the default template for boutique-project trials:

- **Rate ladder (all-inclusive, per sq ft)**: Unit 1 = ₹12,000; Units 2–3 = ₹12,500;
  Units 4–5 = ₹13,000; Unit 6 = ₹13,500. Lower first-unit floor is DELIBERATE (momentum
  incentive), not a typo vs the ₹13,000 already achieved.
- **Upside share**: 20% of anything above the applicable base rate (₹1,000 above base =
  +₹200/sq ft to partner). Mutual agreement to push higher if market allows.
- **Gates (exclusivity auto-terminates on miss)**: ≥1 booking within 60 days (31 Oct 2026);
  ≥2 bookings within 90 days (30 Nov 2026). Miss → sole mandate terminates automatically,
  exclusivity lapses, no compensation due beyond sales completed per fee schedule.
- **Per-unsold-unit penalty**: if not all 6 units are sold by expiry, brokerage on any sale
  after expiry reduces 1% per unsold unit (3 unsold → 4%). This is the "liquidate or you
  earn less" lever for a 6-month basis.
- **Fee**: 7% + GST on agreement value — NDR accepted top-of-market given limited inventory.
- **Payment 80/20**: 80% payable at execution of sales agreement AND receipt of up-to-date
  slab payment (the CURRENT slab, not a fixed %); 20% withheld until registration / end of
  cancellation-risk window. Cancellation clawback: proportionate refund of fee; if the
  cancellation fee becomes refundable (incl. per RERA judgment) → proportionate clawback
  mandatory; if cancellation stems from partner misrepresentation / false commitment /
  inducement (not developer non-performance) → ENTIRE fee clawed back.
- **White-label ops**: partner presents as DRA sales team; all branding/logos/uniforms = DRA;
  partner never disclosed to customers. Sales rep present at site ALL working hours; no
  walk-in/lead ever unattended.
- **Recording**: ALL site sales-agent ↔ customer engagements recorded via DRA-introduced
  devices — mandatory, non-negotiable.
- **Content control**: every piece of marketing content pre-approved in writing by DRA —
  ads, collaterals, social, digital, AND one-to-one WhatsApp pitches.
- **Marketing budget commitment (formula)**: expected fee pool (7% of ladder GSV ≈ ₹82L)
  − 25% partner profit − 6-month sales-closer salary − ~30% office/corporate overheads
  = minimum marketing budget for the mandate (~₹6–7L/month on Amber), PROVEN monthly via
  invoices/receipts/campaign reports. The point is provable effort, not the exact number.
- **Party identity**: developer = DRA Realty Pvt Ltd — Regd office No. 201A/202BA, Queens
  Corner, 3 Queens Road, Bengaluru – 560001; PAN AAPCS9730H; GSTIN 29AAPCS9730H1ZO.
  NOT "DRA Ranka Group" (no such entity). CIN is NOT in NDR's files — do not guess; leave
  blank or ask. Verify from MCA if needed.
- **Structural outcome**: NDR chose to KEEP "sole" for the trial (gated sole, not
  non-exclusive) — the 60/90-day gates ARE the protection; structure option A's strict
  lead-tagging-only model is for partners without a clear ops plan.

## Draft reply anatomy (mandate feedback email to partner)

1. Thanks + context numbers (units sold so far, last price achieved, what's offered)
2. Trial term (6 months) + performance gates (60-day, cumulative floor, aspiration)
3. Exclusivity model during trial (tagged leads vs project-wide; conversion path)
4. Pricing floor + escalation + written-approval rule
5. Brokerage: rate, lead-origin tiering, staggered payment, clawback
6. Compliance: CRM, collateral pre-approval, no cross-selling, fortnightly funnel
   reviews, audit rights, RERA agent registration
7. Post-exit tail (CRM-tagged only, ≤90 days)
8. Close: call this week, then legal drafts final agreement
