# Tamil Nadu Stamp Duty — Contribution to Partnership, Firm→Pvt Ltd Conversion (TN Act 13 of 2024)

Verified 2026-09-14 while researching the Ranka Oasis × Jiraaf Capital (Sevaganapalli Land Partners) structure. Use when NDR/team asks about stamp duty / registration impact of:
- a partner contributing immovable property into a partnership it already controls,
- converting a partnership firm into a private limited company,
- any TN-registered land transaction where the Sub-Registrar's classification must be confirmed.

## Governing law (as in force in Tamil Nadu)

- **Indian Stamp Act, 1899 (Central Act 2 of 1899)**, as amended in its application to TN.
- Latest revision: **Indian Stamp (Tamil Nadu Amendment) Act, 2023 (Tamil Nadu Act 13 of 2024)**, effective **3 May 2024** — revised Schedule-I duty rates. L.A. Bill No. 10 of 2023 → TN Act 13 of 2024; gazetted via G.O.Ms.No.475, Law, dated 3 May 2024.
- Registration Act 1908 governs registration of conveyances/compulsorily registrable instruments.

## Key sections / articles and TN rates

| Provision | What it covers | TN rate (post Act 13 of 2024) |
|---|---|---|
| s.2(10) | "conveyance" — every instrument transferring property inter vivos (movable OR immovable) | — |
| **Art 23** | Conveyance / sale of immovable property | **7% of market value** (guideline value if higher) + registration fee (~1–4%, confirm current % with SRO) |
| **Art 46** | Partnership deed — instrument of partnership | **Flat ₹1,000** (capital > ₹500; ₹50 if capital ≤ ₹500) + 1% registration. Was ₹300 before Act 13/2024. |
| Art 46B / 47 | Dissolution / reconstitution of partnership | "family" definition expanded to include legal heirs of deceased member (TN 2023 amendment) |
| Registration Act s.17 | Compulsory registration of instruments creating/declaring/assigning/limiting rights in immovable property > ₹100 | Contribution deed that vests title in the Firm is compulsorily registrable |

## The contribution question (partner → firm, partner already ~90%)

A contribution deed by which a partner puts its own land into the partnership is the area of genuine uncertainty that the advisor (Vishwas Rao / Vantage Point Advisors) flags as "check with registrar":

- **Two candidate classifications:**
  1. **Article 46 partnership deed / supplemental deed** — flat ₹1,000 + 1% registration (cheap); or
  2. **Article 23 conveyance** — 7% (+ registration) on market/guideline value of the contributed land (expensive), per the principle that title transfers from the partner to the firm.
- The Registration Department has consistently treated instruments evidencing transfer of immovable property as chargeable under **Article 23** (IGR Circular No. 49282/P1/2018 dated 20.11.2018) and, for company amalgamations, orders sanctioning schemes of arrangement are classifiable as conveyance under Art 23 — see *State of Tamil Nadu v. Serene Estate (P) Ltd* batch, Madras HC, WP/WA 758/2022 etc., **Feb 2024**.
- Whether a partner's capital contribution to a firm it already controls (not arm's-length) is a "conveyance" or a mere partnership instrument is **not settled by statute alone** — role of the Sub-Registrar's classification. This is exactly the question NDR wants Bharat (BHARAT H, sales1.blr@draas.com) to take to the jurisdictional SRO (Hosur / Denkanikottai for Sevaganapalli village), with Prakash Singh (psingh@draas.com) supporting.

## Firm → Pvt Ltd conversion (Step 2 of the Jiraaf structure)

- Converted under **Companies Act 2013, ss.366–374**; intended **tax-neutral** under Income-tax Act 2025 (assets/liabilities continue with the company; partners get shares; 51%-in-value continuity ~5 years; ~45 days).
- **Stamp side:** TN had reduced amalgamation/reconstruction-of-companies duty to **2% of market value of the immovable property** (G.O.(Ms) No. 29 dated 01.03.2019 and G.O.(Ms) No. 47 dated 19.02.2020).
- **Madras HC (Serene Estate batch, Feb 2024)** struck down the alternate **0.6%-of-share-value** limb of the reduction circular as a wholly new levy not relatable to Art 23; the property-based 2% limb survived. (IGR Circular No. 40405/P2/2021 dated 9 Sep 2024 directs registering officers on the surviving basis: 2% of market value of immovable property, subject to ₹25 Cr cap per the original G.O.s.)
- Whether the **firm→company conversion** qualifies for the 2% reconstruction treatment (vs full 7%) is precisely the Sub-Registrar clarification required — do NOT assume it does.

## RERA angle

Vishwas's note also flags RERA compliance: the land/project (Ranka Oasis at Sevaganapalli, already RERA-registered) — contribution and/or conversion may trigger intimation/amendment requirements. Surface this alongside stamp duty when writing to the team.

## Standard clarification question set (Sub-Registrar)

1. Contribution by a ~90% partner → Art 46 (₹1,000) vs Art 23 conveyance (7%)?
2. Does partner-controlled (non-arm's-length) contribution change classification/rate?
3. If Art 23 — value base (guideline / market / composite) and current registration fee %?
4. Firm→company conversion — instrument + rate (2% reconstruction vs 7%)? Title registration to the company?
5. RERA interface for both steps?
