## ⭐ Clause 5.6 — Sharing and Costs for Additional SBUA (The Key Premium FAR Clause)

Found in the Principal JDA Registration. Exact wording (OCR'd):

> **5.6 Sharing and Costs for Additional SBUA:**
> 
> In the event that any additional Floor Area Ratio (FAR) or development rights—such as Transferable Development Rights (TDR), rights under Premium FSI schemes, or any other similar entitlements such as but not limited to Premium FAR scheme or the like—become available for the Project, **the Landowner shall have the sole right and responsibility to procure such rights at their own cost.**
> 
> **Any additional built-up area (SBUA) that becomes possible as a result of these rights shall be compulsorily utilised in the Project. The Landowner and the Developer shall share the resultant additional SBUA in equal proportion, i.e., 50:50.**
> 
> **While the cost of procuring such rights shall be entirely borne by the Landowner, the cost of approvals, sanctioned & constructing the additional area shall be borne by the Developer.**

### Clause Breakdown

| Aspect | Provision |
|---|---|
| Who procures Premium FAR? | **Landowner** (sole right and responsibility) |
| Who bears cost of procuring Premium FAR rights? | **Landowner entirely** |
| How is additional SBUA shared? | **50:50** (Landowner : Developer) |
| Who bears cost of approvals & sanction? | **Developer** |
| Who bears cost of construction of additional area? | **Developer** |
| Is use of additional FAR mandatory? | **Yes** — "compulsorily utilised in the Project" |
| What rights are covered? | Premium FAR, Premium FSI, TDR, or any similar additional development rights |

### Commercial Logic

The Landowner bears the upfront cost of purchasing Premium FAR/TDR rights (paid to government). In exchange, the Landowner gets 50% of the additional built-up area created. The Developer bears construction cost for the additional area for both shares — balanced because the Developer gets 50% of additional saleable area without paying for the rights.

## What the Addendum Says
