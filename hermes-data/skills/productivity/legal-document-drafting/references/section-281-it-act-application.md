# Section 281 Income Tax Act — Application Template

## When to use

When a partner is contributing assets (land, shares, property) to a partnership firm and needs prior approval from the Income Tax Department under **Section 281 of the Income Tax Act, 1961**.

Section 281 voids certain transfers of assets during pendency of IT proceedings. The application seeks the AO's prior approval / NOC so the transfer is not challenged as void.

## Document Structure

### Cover Letter (1-2 pages)
- From: Applicant (partner) with PAN and address
- To: The Assessing Officer, Circle [XX], [City]
- Subject: Application under Section 281 for prior approval
- Body: Brief description of proposed transaction
- Reference to tax compliance history
- List of enclosures

### Form ITNS 281 (prescribed format)
- "Application under section 281 of the Income-tax Act, 1961"
- Applicant's name, PAN, address
- Description of assets being transferred/contributed
- Full property schedules with survey numbers, extents, villages
- Tax return filing history (last 3 AYs)
- Declaration of no outstanding tax demands
- Prayer for approval

## Key Schedules to Include

| Schedule | Content |
|----------|---------|
| Schedule A | Registered Sale Deed properties (survey no, extent, village, taluk, district) |
| Schedule B Part A | Properties under registered sale deeds via partition/settlement |
| Schedule B Part B | Properties held under Agreements of Sale + GPA (PTCL or pending clearance) |

## Annexures

1. ITR V / Acknowledgement (last 3 AYs)
2. Screenshot of outstanding tax demand status from IT portal
3. Copy of Partnership Deed / Transaction Agreement
4. Property schedules from the deed
5. Supporting title documents

## Placeholder Pattern

Use `[____]` or `[Description]` for unknown fields:
- PAN, address, phone, email of applicant
- AO Circle details
- ITR filing dates and returned income figures
- Specific deed/document numbers
