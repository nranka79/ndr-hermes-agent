# NRI Power of Attorney — Execution & Attestation Checklist

Source: official VFS **Power of Attorney/Affidavit Checklist** (Aug-26 revision, most
current). Latest versions live under
`https://services.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/india/united-states-of-america/miscellaneous-services/pdf/`
— fetch the newest dated file, do not hardcode an edition.

## Apostille — the answer most guides get wrong

For an **Indian national**, apostille is **NOT required**. Consular attestation is enough.

Apostille is prescribed only for nationals of *other* countries (and per the checklist, OCI
card holders are exempt even then). The Consulate's own note: *"No Commercial Documents are
attested by the Consulate... The apostille is done by the Office of Secretary of State of the
state concerned."*

So for a US-resident Indian citizen: **notarise in the US, then consular attestation.** Do
not send them to the Alabama Secretary of State.

## Sequence

1. Executant signs in the presence of **two witnesses who are not immediate family**, whose
   full names and addresses are recorded on the instrument
2. One passport-size photograph affixed to the **last page** of the PoA
3. **Notarise** with a US notary public
4. Submit **in person** at VFS (cannot be posted)
5. Courier to India
6. Stamp / adjudicate before the **District Registrar**
7. Then produce before the **Sub-Registrar** for registration

## Documents to carry

- Miscellaneous Application Form — one per executant, signed, photo affixed
- 2 identical photos, **51mm x 51mm**, white background, taken within 6 months
- Original **and** photocopy of the notarised PoA
- Notarised copy of passport first & last pages
- Notarised copy of US legal status (visa)
- Notarised proof of US address — driving licence, utility bill, lease, State ID, or mortgage deed

## Traps that cost a second trip

- **Name must appear exactly as in the passport** in the first two paragraphs of the PoA,
  together with the US residential address.
- **Signature on the PoA must match the passport signature.** If it differs the file is
  returned for re-signing — call this out to the executant explicitly.
- An alias in the PoA needs supporting documentation.
- Photos must be 51mm x 51mm (±), white background, no glasses, no flash reflection.

## Fees

- Consular attestation is **$20 per attestation** (+$10 per additional document *photograph*
  attested), plus a separate **$2 ICWF** — figures verified from the Consulate's own
  comparable "Attestation of Will" page. Confirm the exact PoA total with VFS.
- Payable by **money order or cashier's cheque** drawn in favour of "Consulate General of
  India, Atlanta". **No credit/debit cards, no personal cheques.** Cash only if in person.
- A pre-paid traceable return envelope (USPS Express / FedEx) is needed if return by mail.
  Mail-back handling alone is ~$20.

## Drafting pattern: add an Annexure A

When producing the PoA itself, append an **ANNEXURE A — Execution and Attestation** clause
set covering: jurisdiction (cite the 15 July 2025 revision), the service centre and its
address, travel/access notes, mode of execution (witnesses, photo, notarisation), the
apostille position for that executant's nationality, the no-post rule, fees, and what
happens after attestation.

Worked example: `20260919_Kankshita_Swaminathan_Power_of_Attorney_to_V_Swaminathan`
(Google Doc, owner ndr@draas.com) — 8 clauses, ~4,200 chars added on top of the ~17,200-char
instrument body.

## Blank, never invent

Leave bracketed placeholders for anything not sourced from a document: Aadhaar, passport
number with place/date of issue/expiry, PAN, email, DOB, and the place of execution. A
wrong Aadhaar in a notarised instrument is a defect that surfaces at the registration
counter. See `productivity/gws-automation/references/whatsapp-phone-number-pitfall.md` for
the same rule applied to phone and identity numbers generally.
