# RERA Model Form of Allotment Letter (Karnataka Annexure-1) — Clause Reference

Source: "Ranka Amber Allotment Letter.pdf" (RERA Karnataka Annexure-1 model form, 10 pages).
Trigger: user shares an allotment letter PDF or asks for cancellation/booking terms for a DRAAS project (Ranka Amber, Ranka Udaya, etc.). NOTE: the model form is a BLANK template — the "Ranka Amber Allotment Letter" file shared by DRAAS contains no filled project/plot details; only the standard clause skeleton. Extract the clause structure, not the field values.

## What the document IS
- ANNEXURE-1 — Model Form of Allotment Letter under RERA Act 2016, clause (g) of sub-section (2) of Section 4
- Mandatory format whenever booking/advance collected (≤ 10% of unit cost)
- Template fields are all blank (name, unit no., carpet area, price, parking, etc.)

## Key clauses (verbatim structure, useful for drafting DRAAS-specific terms)

### Clause 8 — Interest on delayed payment
- Interest = SBI highest Marginal Cost of Lending Rate (MCLR) + 2%

### Clause 9 — Cancellation of allotment (buyer-initiated)
| Within | Deduction |
|---|---|
| 15 days from allotment letter | Nil |
| 16–30 days | 1% of unit cost |
| 31–60 days | 1.5% of unit cost |
| After 61 days | 2% of unit cost |
- Balance refunded WITHOUT interest within **45 days** of cancellation request
- If refund delayed beyond 45 days → balance + interest at SBI MCLR + 2%

### Clause 12 — Execution & registration of agreement for sale (promoter-side cancellation)
- Allottee must execute + register agreement within **2 months** of allotment letter (extendable by mutual understanding)
- If missed → promoter serves **15-day notice**
- If still not complied → promoter cancels allotment, forfeits **up to 2%** of unit cost; balance refunded within 45 days without interest
- Stage-payment variant: if allottee misses a stage installment, promoter serves 15-day notice; on non-compliance cancels + forfeits amount paid, capped by the Clause 9 table

### Clause 13 — Validity
- Allotment letter doesn't limit rights after agreement registration; cancellation thereafter governed by the registered document

### Clause 7 — Possession (Ranka Amber example)
- Possession on or before **31.07.2029** (project at Plot No. 1-B, D'Silva Layout, Pattandur Agrahara, K.R. Puram, Bangalore East)

## DRAAS booking-terms nuance (Bharat, Aug 2026)
For a Ranka Udaya Block 5 booking with a client, Bharat dictated tighter terms than the model form:
- 15 days for the client to sign the agreement + complete legal verification
- If the client doesn't return/complete within 15 days → **entire booking amount forfeited**
- ⚠️ This is stricter than the model form's 2% cap — flag the divergence when drafting; the model form's Clause 9 table is the RERA-compliant fallback.

## Drafting pattern
When asked to draft an allotment-letter-based cancellation email/letter:
1. Confirm whether the user wants the RERA model-form terms (2% cap, 45-day refund) or their stricter dictated terms (full forfeiture after 15 days)
2. Present the clause structure in plain language, one point per line
3. All-new content for Bharat gets yellow-highlighted (see legal-document-drafting highlight conventions) when it lands in a doc
