# MOU Standard Drafting Format

When the user asks to draft or restructure a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) for a real estate land aggregation / joint monetisation transaction, use this standard 6-Part format.

## Structure

```
DRAFT

MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

Date: ___ day of ________ 20__

BY AND BETWEEN
[Party A details] — "FIRST PARTY"
AND
[Party B details] — "SECOND PARTY"

WHEREAS:
A. [Recital: Party A's ownership/interest]
B. [Recital: Party B's ownership/interest]
C. [Recital: Purpose/scope of the MOU]
D. [Recital: Intent to collaborate — optional]
E. [Recital: Mutual agreement — optional]

NOW, THEREFORE, IN CONSIDERATION OF THE MUTUAL PROMISES
AND COVENANTS CONTAINED HEREIN, THE PARTIES HEREBY AGREE
AS FOLLOWS:

PART I — DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

PART II — CONSIDERATION AND ADVANCE

PART III — LAND PROCUREMENT, REGISTRATION, AND COSTS

PART IV — JOINT MONETIZATION

PART V — TIMELINE AND EXIT

PART VI — GENERAL COVENANTS

SCHEDULE A — [Party A's lands]
SCHEDULE B — [Party B's lands]
SCHEDULE C — [Lands to be procured]

IN WITNESS WHEREOF...

WITNESSES:
1. _________________
2. _________________

For [FIRST PARTY]
For [SECOND PARTY]
```

## Key Clauses by Part

### Part I — Definitions
- Define key terms used throughout: Consideration, Project Lands, Clear and Marketable Title, etc.

### Part II — Consideration and Advance
- Per-acre consideration for procurement
- Advance payment amount, cheque details, acknowledgment
- Keep Rs. 5 Cr/acre as indicative with inclusions/exclusions

### Part III — Land Procurement, Registration, and Costs
- **Procurement obligation** — which party procures which lands
- **Registration** — each party registers in its own name; specify 50:50 ratio for newly procured lands
- **Payment** — consideration paid against concurrent registration
- **Costs** — each party pays stamp duty, registration, legal due diligence for their own lands; First Party bears all other incidental/title costs
- **Title & documentation** — First Party conveys good marketable title

### Part IV — Joint Monetization
- Both parties bound to monetise jointly
- Land aggregation priority (contiguous block formation)
- Development options (DM for Plotted Development, Grade A developers)
- Proceeds shared proportionately to land holding

### Part V — Timeline and Exit
- **Time-bound procurement** — 6-month deadline from signing
- **Monthly progress reports** from procuring party
- **Exit for delay** — if procuring party fails to deliver, advance refunded with 18% interest
- **Bilateral exit** — both parties have termination rights
- **Force Majeure** — timeline extension on external events

### Part VI — General Covenants
- Good faith obligation
- Non-alienation (no independent dealing)
- Dispute resolution (binding mediation, Mediation Act 2023)
- Governing law and jurisdiction (Bengaluru)
- Entire agreement clause
- Amendments clause
- Matters not provided for

## Conditions Precedent

When the user specifies that certain conditions must be satisfied before monetisation, add a **condition precedent** clause linking the procurement/registration phase to the monetisation phase:

> *"The [50:50 purchase and registration ratio], [strategic contiguous-block procurement], and [completion of aggregation] as set out in this MOU shall be conditions precedent to the joint monetization of the Project Lands under Part IV. Until such conditions precedent are satisfied, neither Party shall have the right to require monetization."*

## Schedule Tables Format

Use this 9-column format for land schedules:

| Sl.No. | Sy.No. | Owner Name | Extent (A) | Extent (G) | Kharab (A) | Kharab (G) | Total (A) | Total (G) |

Where:
- A = Acres
- G = Guntas (1 Acre = 40 Guntas)
- Kharab = Deduction for roads/amenities
- Total = Net extent after kharab deduction

Always include a **TOTAL row** at the bottom of each schedule with summed values.

## Clause-by-Clause Gap Analysis

Before restructuring, do a gap analysis against these standard MOU clauses:

| Clause | Check |
|---|---|
| Parties & Recitals | ✅ Who, what properties, purpose |
| Consideration | ✅ Per-acre rate, what's included/excluded |
| Advance payment | ✅ Amount, cheque details, acknowledgment |
| Procurement obligation | ✅ Which party, which lands |
| Registration | ✅ Who registers in whose name |
| Costs split | ✅ Each party pays own share |
| Timeline | ❌ Add 6-month clause |
| Exit / Termination | ❌ Add bilateral exit + consequences |
| Interest on advance | ❌ Add 18% on default |
| Joint monetisation | ✅ Both parties bound |
| Profit sharing | ✅ Proportional to holding |
| Dispute resolution | ✅ Mediation / jurisdiction |
| Force Majeure | ❌ Add |
| Indemnification | ❌ Add (if needed) |
| Entire agreement | ❌ Add |
| Schedules populated | ❌ Fill survey numbers, extents, totals |

## Style Rules

- **Numbered clauses** (1, 2, 3...) with sub-clauses (a), (b), (c)
- **Bold clause titles** in ALL CAPS followed by colon
- **Part headers** bold, centered, larger font (13pt)
- Document title (MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING) centered, 16pt bold
- Standard 1.15 line spacing, 6pt space between paragraphs
- SCHEDULE headers bold, centered

## When to create D2

If the restructuring is substantial (changing ownership ratios, adding new Parts, reworking the entire agreement structure), create a **D2 version** of the document rather than editing in-place. Name it `[Original Name]_D2`. See `gws-automation` → references for the D2 naming convention (Nishant preference: use `_D2` suffix, not v2/final/updated/new).
