# NRI GPA Drafting + US Attestation Process (Karnataka registration)

**Class:** drafting a Power of Attorney from a US-resident Indian national landowner in favour
of a relative/dad so the family can register an addendum/JDA in India; and getting that GPA
accepted by the Karnataka Sub-Registrar. Verified Sep-2026 on Ranka NorthStar (Kankshita
Swaminathan → father V. Swaminathan). This is a LIVE matter — the attestation-route
contradiction below was unresolved at last update.

## When this applies
- Landowner is an Indian citizen but US resident; the office needs a FRESH GPA because
  unregistered GPAs are no longer accepted for registration (Sub-Registrar's new position).
- The registered JDA needs an addendum registered and one co-owner cannot be present.

## Structure decision — ONE amalgamated-property GPA + a SEPARATE exit-site GPA
Two instruments, never one combined:
1. **GPA One — the live JDA property.** For an amalgamated property, describe her holding as
   an UNDIVIDED SHARE in the whole (khata 591/1077/14/1/1, 53,148 sqft), NOT as her old site
   numbers (sites 6/7 were merged into the amalgamation in 2014). Recitals: joint owner of the
   whole, Deed of Amalgamation 04.02.2014, her share. Schedule = Parcel One (whole property +
   boundaries) + Parcel Two (her undivided 10,422 sqft / 19.61%). Powers: execute/present/
   register the Addendum to the JDA (the already-signed one), and authority to sign, re-sign,
   amend, vary, correct, clarify, ratify, re-execute the Addendum and ANY further addendum,
   plus all commercial decisions (sharing of inventory, unit allocation, area statements,
   refundable adjustments).
2. **GPA Two — the exit site** (site 10 in NorthStar's case, own JDA 3033, own khata
   12,106 sqft). Scope is wider: cancellation of that JDA, sale, disposal, release and
   relinquishment of rights, settlement including court settlement, receipt of consideration.
   Keep it separate so a contentious exit never blocks the main registration.

## US execution & attestation — THE ROUTE IS CONTESTED, CONFIRM BEFORE SENDING HER
Two routes exist and the Bangalore Sub-Registrar's acceptance differs by office:
- **Route A — Apostille:** sign → 2 witnesses (NOT immediate family, full names+addresses on
  doc) → US notary public → apostille from the Secretary of State of Alabama. No travel.
- **Route B — Consular attestation:** sign+notarise locally, then attend IN PERSON at the
  Indian Consulate's service provider. Since **01-Aug-2025**, POA attestation is handled by
  **VFS Global** (for Atlanta jurisdiction: 1200 Altmore Avenue, Suite 175, Sandy Springs,
  Georgia 30342 — NOT the consulate on Glenridge Drive). Applications CANNOT be posted —
  in-person only. Atlanta covers Alabama per the revised consular jurisdiction (15-Jul-2025).
  Travel from Madison AL is ~186 miles / 4 hrs one way.
- **The contradiction:** the consulate's own rules say Indian nationals do NOT need an
  apostille (consular attestation suffices), but Bharat's Sep-2026 report from three SROs was
  that **US Notary + Apostille IS acceptable**. These are opposite processes. **Gate the
  instruction to her on an SRO-confirmed route** — she must not travel 4 hours on a guess.
- Send her the WhatsApp message that lays out BOTH routes and explicitly says: do not attend
  for attestation until Bharat confirms which route the Sub-Registrar accepts. One day of
  waiting beats two four-hour trips.
- Signature must EXACTLY match the passport signature or the file gets returned.
