# NRI Power of Attorney — US Consular Route (verified 2026-09-19)

Context: Kankshita Swaminathan, Indian national resident in Madison, Alabama, needed to
execute a fresh GPA in favour of her father V. Swaminathan so the Allalsandra JDA addendum
could be registered. The Sub-Registrar rejected her earlier unregistered GPA.

## Jurisdiction — check the OFFICIAL revision, not a general guide

Alabama falls under the **Consulate General of India, Atlanta**. Confirmed against the
*Revised Consular Jurisdiction* notified by the Embassy of India, Washington DC, effective
**15 July 2025**:

- Washington DC: Bermuda, District of Columbia, Kentucky, Maryland, North Carolina, Virginia, West Virginia
- **Atlanta: Alabama, Florida, Georgia, Mississippi, Puerto Rico, South Carolina, Tennessee, US Virgin Islands**
- Houston: Arkansas, Kansas, Louisiana, Oklahoma, Texas
- Others: Boston, Chicago, Los Angeles, New York, San Francisco, Seattle

Best citable source is the MEA mission's own PDF, e.g.
`https://www.cgichicago.gov.in/content/Consular-Jurisdiction-Sep-2025.docx.pdf`
(MEA's own `mea.gov.in` returns 403 via CloudFront; Jina works on the mission PDFs.)

## Two domain traps

- The Atlanta mission's site is **`indiainatlanta.gov.in`** — NOT `cgiatlanta.gov.in`,
  which does not resolve at all.
- Google Maps / contact pages list the Consulate at **5549 Glenridge Drive NE, Atlanta, GA
  30342**. That is the Consulate, but it is *not* where POA attestation happens (see below).

## The VFS transition — the single biggest gotcha

Per the Consulate's own notice: *"With effect from 1st August 2025, all services including
Miscellaneous Consular Services will be handled through the Consulate's authorized service
provider i.e., M/s VFS Global, at 1200 Altmore Ave Suite 175, Sandy Springs, GA 30342."*

So the address to send an executant to is:

```
VFS Global (Indian Consular Application Centre)
1200 Altmore Avenue, Suite 175, Sandy Springs, Georgia 30342
```

Excluded from the VFS move (still at the Consulate): registration of death, NOC for
transport of mortal remains/ashes, and issue of Life Certificate.

## Travel from north Alabama

- 230 Mainsail Way, Madison AL 35758 → VFS Sandy Springs: **~186 miles, ~4 hours by road**
- Huntsville International (HSV) is ~20 min from Madison with direct flights to Atlanta
- Next-nearest Indian mission is Houston (~1,290 km), then Chicago (~1,000 km) — Atlanta is
  the only practical option

## Do not send by post

The Consulate's notice states plainly: *"all applicants are advised NOT to send their
'Miscellaneous Consular Applications – Attestation Service Applications' to the Consulate by
postal means."* In-person submission only. This matters a lot when the executant was
expecting a courier-only process.
