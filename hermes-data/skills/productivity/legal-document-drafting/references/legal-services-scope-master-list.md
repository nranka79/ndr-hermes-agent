## 1. Preliminary Due Diligence
- Examine title-related documents and revenue-related documents
- Verify up-to-date Encumbrance Certificates (ECs) and Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops (RTCs)
- Check RTC entries alignment with title flow
- Perform extent check (RTC, Aakar Bal/FMB, title, original grant/purchase)
- Examine village map and revenue records for land classification (lake, grazing land, Kharab lands)
- Verify property contiguity to public road through non-Kharab portions
- Conduct litigation search for property and title holders (from current ownership year onwards)
- Perform online search for adverse reports or case mentions

## 2. Detailed Legal Due Diligence
- Thoroughly study and verify all critical documents for authenticity
- Obtain certified copies from relevant departments
- Check corroborated evidence from neighboring lands
- Verify physical location and boundaries as per documents
- Apply for NOCs from government departments to check for potential land acquisition
- **Analyze developability under current town planning norms**
- **Identify applicable buffer zones and development restrictions**
- **Assess potential for land use change and any impediments**
- **Identify relevant town planning authority and current zoning**

## 3. Physical Verification
- Conduct site visit to assess soil condition
- Verify physical access to property from the road
- Check for signs of illegal development or unregistered sales
- Identify potential issues: high tension wires, village roads, nalas, water bodies, quarries, wells, burial grounds, temples

## 4. Physical Boundary Establishment
- Reestablish boundaries as per revenue records
- Ensure physical site conforms with or is within documented boundaries
- Prepare documents for actual physical extent for purchase consideration

## 5. Documentation Services
- Advise on transaction structure to minimize costs and taxes
- Prepare Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)
- Draft partnership, LLP, or company documents
- Create joint development agreements
- Prepare sale deeds, sale agreements, and deferred payment arrangements
- Draft applications for change of land use
- Provide support for licensing processes
- Draft letters to statutory departments for missing records

## 6. Litigation Support Services (5 years post-acquisition)
- Provide litigation support for 5 years post-acquisition
- Create and respond to legal notices
- File objections, petitions, and interim applications
- Argue cases in court
- File for stay orders and injunctions
- Handle stamp duty and income tax disputes
- Assist with market rate vs. guideline rate discrepancies

## 7. Customer Support Documentary Services
- Respond to and resolve customer legal queries
- Explain document omissions or provide relevant documentation
- Address concerns raised by customers' legal advisors

## 8. RERA-related Documentary Support Services
- Draft and finalize RERA-compliant documents
- Prepare sale agreements, sale deeds, and allotment letters
- Create payment receipts and handover documentation
- Draft deed of declaration for apartment association / society formation
- Prepare society membership documents
- Create handover documentation for common areas and amenities

## 9. Document Management and Accessibility
- Maintain online repository (Google Drive) of all title flow documents
- Create and update master index of all documents
- Categorize documents by type, survey numbers, and individual names
- Provide hyperlinked documents in title opinions
- Continuously update documents based on comments and feedback
- Track and categorize customer queries
- Maintain database of responses for future reference

## Usage Notes

**When engaging a new law firm:**
1. Send this master list verbatim
2. Ask the firm to explicitly identify which services they WOULD NOT offer
3. Ask for per-service pricing / payment break-up
4. Prefer firms comfortable with Google Docs/Drive workflow (online document management)

**Sadananda & Co. response pattern:**
- Comfortable with online portals (Google Docs/Drive) for document review
- Excluded: out-of-pocket travel expenses, obtaining government approvals/permits, drafting (separate fee)
- Fee structure: Rs 40,000/acre or per survey number (whichever higher); drafting from Rs 30,000/doc
- Payment: 30% on first docs, 30% on additional docs, 40% on DDR delivery
- Title review period: minimum 60-70 years or from origin of title

**Key exclusions a new firm may cite:**
- Out-of-pocket expenses (travel, SRO visits, department visits)
- Obtaining approvals/permits from government authorities
- Drafting documents (typically separate engagement/fee)
- Customer support and RERA document services
