# Fresh Registered GPA for an Overseas Landowner (Karnataka registration)

Use when an addendum / supplementary agreement to a JDA must be registered but a
landowner is resident abroad and can no longer be represented by the old,
**unregistered** GPA.

## Why a fresh GPA is suddenly needed

The Sub-Registrar will not accept representation by an **unregistered** GPA.
The GPAs signed in the early life of these projects (2003-era) were acceptable
then; under the current rule they are not. When the primary deed was executed by
the landowner **in her own individual capacity**, she must be represented by a
**registered** GPA for the registration to go through. Do not argue this at the
counter — it costs weeks of lost time.

## The process chain (state it plainly to the family)

1. Fresh GPA is drafted (by us) and shared with the overseas signatory **and** the
   proposed attorney.
2. The signatory executes it abroad.
3. It is **attested / notarised at the Indian Consulate** nearest to them
   (alternative: notary public + **apostille**).
4. Courier to India.
5. We take it to the **District Registrar** here for **adjudication**.
6. Only then does it become valid and operative — and only then can the addendum
   be registered.

**Confirm the accepted route with the local District Registrar / Sub-Registrar
BEFORE the signatory acts.** Whether they will take consular attestation, or a
notary-plus-apostille, decides whether the person has to make the trip twice.
This is a one-question check for the office contact — do it first, not last.

## Powers to include (derived from what NDR dictates for this class)

Group them by function so the document reads cleanly:

- **Execution** — sale deeds, agreements for sale, JDAs, **addenda / supplementary
  and rectification deeds to the JDA**, assignment deeds, confirmation deeds,
  declarations, affidavits, undertakings, receipts, no-dues.
- **Presentment, adjudication and registration** — present/admit/register before
  the jurisdictional Sub-Registrar; get instruments stamped (incl. e-stamping);
  pay stamp duty and registration charges; obtain endorsements and certified
  copies; file for adjudication.
- **Revenue and municipal** — khata, PID, e-Aasthi, property tax, betterment
  charges, RTC/Pahani, mutation, survey records; BBMP / BDA / BMRDA / planning
  authority; no-objection certificates and sanctions.
- **Appearances** — before the Sub-Registrar, District Registrar, Deputy
  Commissioner, Tahsildar, K-RERA, tribunals and courts; engage advocates.
- **Ancillary / general** — receive monies, refundable and non-refundable
  deposits, sale proceeds; ratify prior acts; substitute a sub-attorney.
- **No revocation** while the project obligations subsist, and an express
  **ratification** clause covering acts already done.

Make it a **SPECIAL (limited) power tied to the specific project property**, not a
general GPA. That is what the registering authority wants to see. If the family
wants the wider 2003-style authority, draft that as a separate instrument.

## Particulars: extract from the executed instrument, never from memory

This is where an assistant gets burned. Every particular in the GPA must be traced
to a document, and the document cited.

**Worked errors caught in one session (2026-09-19):**

- An Aadhaar and an age were asserted for the attorney from memory and were
  **wrong**. The executed addendum gave a different Aadhaar and a different age.
  Grep the OCR'd instrument and use what the instrument says.
- A **phone number quoted from memory was wrong** and produced a dead WhatsApp
  link. Numbers come from the contacts resolver, never recollection.

**Sharp trap — the Aadhaar printed beside a represented party's name may not be
theirs.** In the recital the Aadhaar shown against a landowner who is "Represented
by [GPA holder] ... vide GPA dated ..." was in fact the **attorney's** Aadhaar. Do
not copy it into the GPA as the landowner's. Where her own Aadhaar is genuinely
absent from the record, leave a blank rather than invent one.

**Sharp trap — check whether the owner's site sits under a DIFFERENT JDA.** The
main agreement for a project may cover Sites 1–8 while a particular landowner's
site is governed by its **own separate JDA** (a different document number). If so,
the fresh GPA must cover both agreements. Verify per-owner before the signatory
signs, or the document comes back.

**Leave unverified items blank with a visible rule** (e.g. `[Aadhaar No. ______]`)
and list the gaps in the covering message. Do not fill a blank with a plausible
value.

## Delivery

- Produce it as a **Google Doc** (NDR asks for "a Google Word document") and give
  him the **link, exact document name, file ID, owning account and parent folder**.
- Then a **WhatsApp message** to the overseas signatory requesting the missing
  particulars (Aadhaar, passport with place/date of issue/expiry, PAN, email, DOB,
  address confirmation) plus the consulate process, and naming the office contact
  who will coordinate.
- Tell NDR which items are still open; he will chase them.
