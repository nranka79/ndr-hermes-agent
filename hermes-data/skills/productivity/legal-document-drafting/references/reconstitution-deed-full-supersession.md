# Reconstitution Deed — Full Term Supersession Pattern

## When to Use This Pattern

When two partners already have an existing partnership deed (e.g. DRA Muthanallur Land Partners) and want to **completely replace all terms** — change the name, profit ratio, capital structure, and every operating clause — **without creating a new entity**.

This avoids having two separate deeds between the same parties and instead rolls everything into a single reconstitution deed that supersedes the original.

## Key Difference from Standard Reconstitution

| Aspect | Standard Reconstitution | Full Supersession Reconstitution |
|--------|----------------------|---------------------------------|
| Purpose | Change partners (retire/admit) | Change ALL terms of the partnership |
| Scope | Minimal changes + continue old terms | Complete replacement of old terms |
| Clauses carried over | Most old clauses continue | All new clauses replace old ones |
| Ratio change | Possible as one change | Possible as one change |
| Name change | Rare | Common |

## Structure of the Deed

### Part I — Reconstitution Covenants (short, 4–5 clauses)

These are the active changes being made to the original deed:

1. **Recital of Original Deed** — State the original deed date, parties, and registration details
2. **Name Change** — From old name to new name  
3. **Ratio Change** — From old ratio to new ratio
4. **Supersession** — ALL terms of the original deed are replaced by the attached new terms
5. **Conditions Precedent** — e.g. Section 281 approval required before certain assets vest

### Part II — Imported New Terms (full deed articles)

Copy all articles from the new/target deed verbatim. This becomes the governing terms of the reconstituted entity:

- Article 1: Definitions
- Article 2: Interpretation
- Article 3: Name, Registered Office, Registration
- Article 4: Commencement, Duration, Continuity
- Article 5: Objects and Business Purpose
- Article 6: Contribution of Land Assets
- Article 7: Capital Contribution and Valuation
- Article 8: Revenue Waterfall and Profit Sharing (with new ratio)
- Article 9: Management
- Article 10: Duties and Representations
- Article 11: Title Indemnity and Capital Adjustment
- Article 12: Prohibited Acts
- Article 13: Lock-In Period and Transfer
- Article 14: Continuity — Death, Incapacity, Succession
- Article 15: Project Finance and RERA
- Article 16: Valuation Upon Exit
- Article 17: Dissolution
- Article 18: Dispute Resolution
- Article 19: Indemnification
- Article 20: Conversion to LLP
- Article 21: Miscellaneous
- Additional articles as needed (e.g., Article 22: Section 281 Compliance)

### Part III — Schedules

- Schedule I: Palya Land
- Schedule II: Byadarahalli Lands (Part A: Registered + Part B: PTCL/GPA)
- Schedule III: DRA Land (if applicable)
- Schedule IV: Original Documents Checklist

### Part IV — Execution Block

Signatures of all parties + witnesses.

### Step 2: Identify what changes

From the original deed:
- **Preserve**: Original deed date, registration details, parties → these go in Recital A
- **Replace**: Name, ratio, capital terms, all operative clauses
- **Remove**: Any clauses specific to the old structure that don't apply

### Step 3: Draft Part I — Reconstitution Clauses

```python
# Clause 1: Recital
A. The Original Partnership Deed dated [date] between [parties] under the name [old name]...

# Clause 2: Name Change
The firm name is changed from [OLD NAME] to [NEW NAME] with effect from [date].

# Clause 3: Ratio Change
The profit sharing ratio is changed from [old ratio] to [new ratio].

# Clause 4: Supersession
All terms of the Original Deed are superseded and replaced by the terms in Part II.

# Clause 5: Conditions Precedent (if any)
This reconstitution is subject to [condition], e.g., Section 281 approval.
```

### Step 4: Import Part II — Full Articles from target deed

Simply copy all articles, schedules, and execution blocks from the new DRA KAAJ deed. The language "Save and except for the amendments..." from standard reconstitutions does NOT apply here — the supersession clause makes Part II the sole governing document.

## Pitfalls

- **Supersession vs amendment language**: Use explicit language "superseded and replaced in their entirety" — NOT the standard reconstitution language "save and except for the amendments contained herein, the original deed continues" — otherwise old terms survive.
- **Don't carry over prejudicial recitals**: If the original deed says "DRA Realty failed to contribute suitable land" and DRA Realty is still a partner, that recital is harmful. Write fresh recitals.
- **Date the reconstitution correctly**: The reconstitution date is the effective date of ALL changes, not the original deed date.
- **Registration**: A reconstitution deed that changes the firm name and ratio must be registered with the Registrar of Firms (Form V / Form A filing within 90 days).
