# GPA for an Overseas Landowner — Drafting + Indian Consulate Registration (Karnataka)

Verified 2026-09-18, Ranka North Star / Allalasandra (Sy No 14/1). Trigger: NDR says
"we need a fresh GPA from <overseas landowner> so we can register the addendum to the JDA",
or the Sub-Registrar has refused to register against an unregistered GPA.

## 1. Why a fresh GPA is needed at all

The Karnataka Sub-Registrar will **not accept representation by an UNREGISTERED GPA**.
The GPAs executed at the start of these projects (~2003–2014) were fine then and are not
now. Until a **fresh GPA is executed, registered/franked at the Indian Consulate, and then
adjudicated at the District Registrar in India**, the addendum to the JDA cannot be
registered with that landowner represented by their attorney.

Consequence to state plainly to NDR: this is a hard registration blocker, it shifts the
registration timeline by roughly 7–10 days minimum (courier + consulate + adjudication), and
nothing downstream (bank pre-approval → project finance → RERA) moves until it clears.

## 2. The process chain (put this in the message to the family)

1. Draft the GPA; share with the landowner **and** their attorney (e.g. Kankshita + Swaminathan sir).
2. Overseas principal **signs and executes** it.
3. **Notarised / attested at the closest Indian Consulate** (or executed before a US notary
   followed by an apostille — see the route trap below).
4. Couriered to India.
5. **Adjudicated at the District Registrar** here.
6. The GPA becomes valid and operative → registration of the addendum can proceed.

## 3. THE ROUTE TRAP — confirm before the principal travels

Consulate-attestation and US-notary-plus-apostille are **different routes with different
acceptance**. Before the principal books an appointment, have the local team (Bharat / Rupa)
**confirm with the specific jurisdictional Sub-Registrar / District Registrar which route is
accepted at that office**. Getting this wrong sends the principal in twice and costs weeks.

## 4. Drafting the GPA — mirror the original, update to the current instruments

The fresh GPA must be "very much in line with the original GPA" (NDR's standing instruction)
— same powers, refreshed for everything that has happened since. Build the recitals from the
**executed documents**, not from memory:

- original JDA (date, Doc No, book, Sub-Registrar)
- the assignment deed to the current developer entity
- every addendum executed since
- the khata / title particulars and survey number
- revenue records, and the RERA position

Power groups that worked (18 recitals, ~8 pages):

- **A. Execution powers** — sale deeds, sale agreements, JDAs and addenda to JDAs, assignment
  deeds, rectification and confirmation deeds, declarations, affidavits, undertakings.
- **B. Presentation, registration, adjudication** — filing before the jurisdictional
  Sub-Registrar, presenting/admitting/registering instruments, paying stamp duty and
  registration fees, securing endorsements and certified copies.
- **C. Revenue and municipal** — khata, PID, property tax, Betterment Charges, e-Aasthi.
- **D. Appearance and representation** — BBMP, BDA, BMRDA, Urban Development Authority, Town
  Planning Authority, Deputy Commissioner, Tahsildar, District Registrar, Sub-Registrar,
  RERA, courts and tribunals.
- **E. Ancillary and general** — engage advocates and agents, sign applications and returns;
  general clause covering all connected acts.
- **F. Ratification and no revocation.**
- **G. Specific approval of acts already done.**
- Closing covenant: acts done under it are as valid as if done personally by the principal.

**Scope decision — special vs general.** Tie the GPA to the specific property/project unless
the family asks for wider authority. Say which you drafted and offer the wider document
separately rather than assuming.

## 5. WHO MUST SIGN — the trap that blocks registration at the counter

Verify from the executed instrument **in what capacity each party signed**. A GPA holder
cannot substitute for a principal who signed a document **in their individual capacity**.

Worked example: the addendum had been signed by the wife **in her individual capacity**, not
by her husband as her GPA holder (he had signed only as GPA holder for their daughter). So
she had to be present at registration even though a GPA existed. The correct line to the
family is: *"the addendum was executed by her directly, so the Sub-Registrar will not allow
registration without her — this is procedural, not a comment on the GPA."* Pair it with a
logistics offer (transport, accommodation, drop her home after registration) to make it easy.

Also verify **who is a named party in their own right**. Sreeraman Vaidyanathan is a named
party (Landowner 6) in both the JDA and the addendum in his own right — so a statement that
he "is not a party to it" is wrong on the face of the instrument, even though the practical
registration point (his signature already on the executed addendum) may still hold. Check the
signature page before asserting anything at the counter.

## 6. Placeholders only the principal can fill

Leave as bracketed placeholders and say so explicitly: **passport number, overseas residential
address, phone, email, place of execution.** Everything else should be populated from the
documents. Do not invent an Aadhaar, address or parentage — pull it from the executed papers
or leave the placeholder.

## 8. The amalgamation deed is the title backbone — read it before drafting

For the Allalasandra / Ranka North Star Sites 1–8 parcel, the governing instrument is the
**Amalgamation Deed dated 04/02/2014**, Doc No. `BNG(U)BYP/…/2013-14`, registered
**Sub-Registrar Byatarayanapura** (CD No. BYPD193 dt. 23/10/13), stamp ₹1,600 + ₹700 fees,

khata/dimension data are in `references/allalasandra-amalgamation-deed-2014.md` — pull from
there rather than re-OCR'ing.

Three things about this deed that change how you draft:

**a. Schedule letters are consumed by the individual sites.** Schedule A = Site 1, B = Site 2,
C = Site 3, D = Site 4, E = Site 5, **F = Site 6 AND Site 7 together** (as Item No.1 and
Item No.2), G = Site 8. There is then an unlettered closing "SCHEDULE PROPERTY (Description
of amalgamated property)" covering Sites 1–8 jointly, **53,148 sqft**, commonly bounded East
by property in Sy. No. 14/2, West by Chikkabommasandra/Allalasandra village limit, North by
tank/park, South by Allalasandra Road. Do not conflate the deed's internal Schedule letters
with the schedules of the GPA you are drafting — the user's instruction ("Plot 1 to 8 =
Schedule A, her UDS = Schedule B") re-letters them.

**b. THE DEED STATES NO UDS PERCENTAGES — do not invent one.** Each party is described as
"sole and absolute owner" of their own site plus "share of undivided right, title and
interest in the passage/private access road lying to its west" (a per-site sqft figure,
1,100–1,400 sqft). Clause N says only that the owners will decide their built-up-area shares
"based on their ownership or as they deem fit" under a **separate sharing agreement**. So a
Schedule B expressed as a precise UDS *fraction* cannot be derived from this deed. Offer NDR
the choice explicitly:
  - **(a) descriptive** — site-wise extent + road share + reference to the amalgamation deed
    (safe, no invented figure), or
  - **(b) numeric** — only if he supplies the percentage, the sharing agreement, or the
    area statement.
He chose Plot Nos. 1–8 only and **excluded Site 10** (Sep 2026).

**c. All seven parties executed through one GPA holder — V. Swaminathan himself.** The
amalgamation deed records every party as "duly represented by their GPA Holder Sri. V.
Swaminathan". Useful corroboration that a Swaminathan GPA is the family's standing execution
route — but it is a *separate, earlier* GPA and does not substitute for the fresh instrument
in Kankshita's favour.

## 9. Scanned registration PDFs — the OCR path (these are image-only, always)

Every Allalasandra title PDF in Drive (amalgamation deed, JDA, sale deeds, POA) is a **scan
with no embedded text layer** — `pdftotext` returns `\f\f` and nothing else. Producer strings
tell you what you have: `PDFium` and `iLovePDF` = scanned. Run OCR first, every time:

```bash
ocrmypdf --force-ocr --output-type pdf -l eng -j 4 in.pdf ocr/in_ocr.pdf
pdftotext ocr/in_ocr.pdf ocr/in.txt
```

- The **JDA is 37 pages** — OCR it in the background (`background=true,
  notify_on_complete=true`); the 16-page amalgamation deed finishes in foreground.
- `jbig2` / `pngquant` "optional dependency not found" warnings are cosmetic — ignore them.
- OCR quality on these scans is **good for English body text, unreliable for Kannada** blocks
  (the e-Khata PDF especially) and for rotated pages.
- **BBMP e-Khata PDFs sometimes carry a real text layer with a broken CMap.** `pdftotext`
  emits garbage codepoints but the **numbers, PAN and address extract cleanly** — grep for the
  ASCII you need rather than declaring the file unreadable. This is how Kankshita's PAN
  `EUKPS3543E` was recovered.
- Drive `KANKSHITHA SWAMINATHAN.pdf` is the **BBMP e-Khata / PID document**, not a KYC scan.
- `vision_analyze` accepts image files only — a `.pdf` path is rejected. Either OCR it or
  rasterise to PNG/JPG first.

## 11. Scope expansion — cancellation powers are a distinct head

The Sep 2026 GPA brief went wider than the §4 power groups: NDR wants **cancellation** powers
— cancellation of the JDA, cancellation/revocation of any GPA, plus "re-renting to JDA
application". Add a dedicated head rather than burying these in the general clause:

- **Cancellation & revocation powers** — to cancel, revoke, terminate or withdraw any JDA,
  addendum, agreement, GPA or instrument, and to execute and register the corresponding
  cancellation/revocation deeds and supporting affidavits.
- **Re-development / re-entry** — applications for fresh JDA, re-entry and allied
  re-development approvals. NDR's phrase "re-renting to JDA application" is **ambiguous —
  confirm your reading with him before drafting** rather than guessing; render it and state
  the interpretation you used.
- Also flag to NDR: a GPA that authorises its own cancellation sits oddly against the
  ratification / no-revocation clause in §4(F). Surface the tension; let him decide.

## 12A. SEARCH THE EMAIL THREAD BEFORE DRAFTING — the draft usually already exists

**Verified Sep 2026, and it changed the whole task.** NDR had *already drafted this GPA*
and published both instruments on the live email thread. The agent had spent the session
reconstructing the deed from Drive scans and was about to draft from scratch; the user
pointed at the email almost as an afterthought ("you can take some inputs from there").

**Rule: for any fresh-GPA-on-a-live-project request, search NDR's Gmail thread FIRST.**
Before OCR'ing anything, before building any clause plan:

```
gmail.users().messages().list(userId="me",
  q="<project name> OR <principal surname> OR amalgamation", maxResults=30)
```
Then pull the newest thread with `users().threads().get(id=..., format="full")`.

NDR's working style on live projects is to **put the drafting artefact in the email**: the
Google Doc links, the extents, the percentages, the schedule structure, the open decisions,
and the paragraph-by-paragraph actions he wants from the team. He writes long structured
emails that *are* the instruction set. Ignoring them means re-deriving work he has already
done — and producing a rival draft that contradicts his.

**Read-only rule.** When the user says "this is just for reference" / "do not reply", the
thread is input, not a channel. Read it, do not draft a reply, do not send, do not create a
draft in the mailbox. Say explicitly in your reply that you did not touch the thread — the
user is relying on that.

⚠️ **The thread will contradict your own analysis, and NDR is usually right.** In this
session the agent had concluded "the amalgamation deed states no UDS percentage, so Schedule
B cannot be numeric — offer descriptive or ask for the figure." NDR's already-drafted GPA
had gone numeric anyway: **10,422 sqft ÷ 53,148 sqft ≈ 19.61%**, derived by adding Site 6
and Site 7 *with their access-road shares* (5,115 + 5,307). That is a legitimate derivation
from §8(b) — the road share is an undivided interest appurtenant to the site, so it counts
toward her share. **Do not re-litigate a figure NDR has already chosen; verify the
arithmetic and flag genuine source ambiguities instead.**

## 12B. REVIEWING A DRAFT NDR WROTE — the conflation traps

NDR drafts fast and carries names across documents. When asked to review his GPA (or any of
his drafts), check these four specifically — all four fired in Sep 2026:

1. **Dimensions that lost a number.** NDR's Site 7 read *"measuring East to West 70 feet"*.
   The amalgamation deed says Site 7 is *"East and West 55 feet… Western Side 70 feet"* — he
   dropped the 55 and promoted the Western dimension. **Verify every dimension against the
   source; dropped cardinal figures are the most common error.**
2. **A person's parentage taken from the wrong party.** NDR wrote Swaminathan as *"son of
   Late Sri R. Venkataraman"*. But "Late Sri R. Venkataraman" in this deed is the **husband
   of V. Rangammal (Owner 7)** — an unrelated person. Swaminathan's own father, per his PAN,
   is **Venkatraman Ramaswamy**. Classic conflation between two people in the same document.
   **Cross-check every parentage/spouse recital against the PAN, not against the deed.**
3. **An age that does not track the source.** NDR wrote Kankshita as *"aged about 49"*. The
   2014 amalgamation deed records *"aged about 40"*. Recompute from the source age to the
   execution date and flag any gap over a year.
4. **A recital citing an instrument nobody has seen.** NDR's recital 5 cited a *"Power of
   Attorney dated 18th August 2003"*. Nothing in the amalgamation deed, the JDA, or the Drive
   index supports it, and her actual 2013-14 POA runs to DRA Projects, **not** her father.
   **Ask for the instrument or ask him to drop the recital — never let an unsupported
   document citation into a registered instrument.**

**Also flag source-internal inconsistencies rather than silently smoothing them.** The
amalgamation deed states Site 6 as **4,015 sqft** in the operative clause but **4,035 sqft**
in Schedule F of the same deed — a 20 sqft discrepancy *in the registered instrument itself*.
NDR used 4,015 (the operative clause — the right call). Surface it; do not quietly pick one.

**Reporting shape for a draft review** (NDR reads these on a phone): a short ✓-verified list
first, then a numbered ⚠️ error list with *what the draft says / what the source says /
what to do*. Close with the specific decisions you need from him — not an offer to proceed.
