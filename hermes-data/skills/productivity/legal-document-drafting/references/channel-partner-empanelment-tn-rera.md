## Commission slab-by-effort structure (the "slab based on steps of closure")

Rate determined by how much of the closure effort the partner performs (on Realised Value only):

- S1 Referral (introduces + registers, hands over) — 0.50%
- S2 Assisted close (sources, qualifies, arranges visit, supports negotiation) — 1.00%
- S3 Co-close (full selling cycle to booking; Company documents) — 1.50%
- S4 Full-cycle (end-to-end through registration; Company only admin/legal) — 2.00%
- Volume accelerator (rolling 12 months): +0.25pt at 3–5 units, +0.50pt at 6–9, +0.75pt at 10+
- Payout: 50% at execution of agreements + ≥50% realisation; balance at registration/full
  realisation; TDS 194H/GST apply; clawback on cancellation (full clawback if partner's fault).

**Design tension to handle in drafting**: S4 2.00% + 0.75% accelerator exceeds any notified
ceiling around 2%. Draft a "notified ceiling prevails" clause so the cap is never breached —
and have Legal confirm whether the state's notified brokerage cap applies to developer-paid
channel commission at all (most caps limit what agents may charge customers).

## TN RERA statutory anchors (verified wording-safe, 2026-09-25)

- Project must be registered; no ad/booking/sale otherwise (s.3 RERA Act).
- 70% of amounts realised from allottees into a separate project account (s.4(2)(l)(D)).
- No money beyond 10% of consideration without a REGISTERED agreement for sale in the standard
  format filed with the Authority (s.13).
- Ads must be truthful and consistent with registered particulars (s.12); ad must carry project
  RERA number + promoter name + (when the partner advertises) the agent's own registration no.
- Agent registration MANDATORY to facilitate sale/purchase in a registered project (s.9);
  registration format **TN/Agent/____/____**, valid **5 years** (verified against TNRERA's own
  expired-agents register: TN/Agent/0299/2021 dated 09.07.2021 expired 09.07.2026).
- Agent duties (s.10): written consent of promoter, maintain books/records, no unfair practice,
  supply project details to the buyer, furnish info to the Authority on notice.
- Penalties: s.61 — imprisonment up to 1 year or fine up to 10% of the cost of the
  plot/apartment/building or both (contravention of ss.9/10); s.62/s.64 — continuing daily
  penalties for non-compliance with Authority orders / failure to furnish information (up to
  ₹10,000/day, cumulative up to 5% of the cost of the plot/apartment for which the sale was
  facilitated — per the official RERA MOFA FAQ).
- Buyer rights preserved explicitly: s.18 refund with interest, s.15 title compensation,
  s.14(3) defect liability, s.31 complaint to Authority/Adjudicating Officer.
- Interest on defaults/refunds: TN rate commonly reported around SBI MCLR + 2% (~10.5% p.a.)
  — mark for Legal to confirm the current notification; do NOT print an unverifiable %.

## Drafting principles & pitfalls

- **Never fabricate statutory numbers you cannot verify.** The TN RERA Rules 2017 PDF
  (cmdachennai.gov.in) was unreachable from the VPS (curl 000 even via the residential tunnel).
  Draft the commission ceiling as "the maximum brokerage/commission ceiling notified by the
  Government of Tamil Nadu / TN RERA under the Act from time to time" and add a
  Legal/Finance confirmation box in the compliance annexure (Schedule E.3 pattern). Verified
  facts (TN/Agent format, 5-year validity) came from TNRERA's own public registers.
- **Statute-prevails clause**: any inconsistency between the form and the Act/Rules/registration
  record/Authority directions → the latter prevail (severability double-guard with clause 15.4).
- **Auto-suspension**: empanelment suspends automatically while the partner's agent registration
  is not in force; 7-day notification duty; survival clause must carry the RERA clauses.
- **"Proposed for Management approval" framing** — the cover and Schedule B note say final
  rates/schedule are issued in writing before the partner starts activity. Never let a partner
  begin work off an unapproved Schedule B.
- **Version discipline**: compliance upgrade = version bump on the cover + the confidentiality
  line ("Prepared in conformity with RERA Act 2016 + TN RERA Rules 2017 (Part E)").
- **Deliverables**: branded PDF + editable DOCX; update the SAME Drive file IDs in place
  (files().update with media) so shared `anyone-reader` links survive revision rounds; verify
  perms after update (anyone/reader + owner). Verify the PDF (page count, key strings via
  pymupdf get_text, low-ink page scan) and the DOCX (table count + heading count) before
  delivering links.
