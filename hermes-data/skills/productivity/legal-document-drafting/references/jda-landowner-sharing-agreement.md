# JDA Landowner Share — Sharing / Supplementary Sharing Agreement

Class of work: a JDA (or its addenda) obliges the landowner and developer to execute a
**Sharing Agreement** at the end of development to fix and vest the landowner's share.
Two structural variants exist in DRAAS and they are NOT interchangeable:

| Variant | Project type | Share is expressed as | Precedent |
|---|---|---|---|
| KA apartment | Apartment/flat project (BBMP / K-RERA) | Saleable **super built-up area**, units, UDS per unit, car park slots | `20260624_RankaAmber_SupplementarySharingAgreement_EDITABLE` |
| TN plotted villa | Plotted layout with villas (DTCP / TN-RERA) | **Developed Site Area** (plots) **+ Built-up Area (BUA)** per villa | Ranka Oasis — Ramesh Reddy (this reference) |

---

## 1. Read every source document BEFORE drafting

Never draft from the JDA alone. The entitlement usually gets amended. Pull and read, in order:

1. **Principal JDA** (registered) — grant of development rights, share %, handover timeline,
   arbitration/jurisdiction, schedule of property + boundaries.
2. **Every addendum in date order** — these are where the real entitlement lives. Check
   whether an addendum *supersedes and replaces* specific clauses of the JDA.
3. **The binding term sheet / ToR** preceding the JDA — shows the original commercial intent
   and is the tie-breaker when addenda conflict.
4. **GPA in favour of the developer** — confirm the clause that says the GPA does **not**
   authorise the developer to alienate the landowner's share (that clause goes into the
   sharing agreement verbatim).
5. **RERA registration certificate** — project name, registration number, scope (how many
   house sites / units, which survey numbers), promoter, designated accounts.
6. **DTCP / planning authority layout sanction + local body approval** — sanction numbers,
   ROC numbers, dates.
7. **Project sheet / area statement / unit nomenclature** — the as-built commercial position.

Registered documents in TN/KA are almost always **image-only scans** (zero text layer).
OCR them — see §6.

## 2. Entitlement reconciliation checklist — DO THIS, DO NOT SKIP

The single highest-value step. Reconcile the landowner's entitlement across *every* source
and flag conflicts rather than silently choosing one:

- [ ] Share percentage as originally stated in the JDA (e.g. "26% of Developed Site area")
- [ ] What the addendum *replaced* it with (e.g. 26% developed site area **plus** BUA at
      1.5× the allocated plot area)
- [ ] **Configurations offered** (e.g. Option A 4 plots × 1,500 = 6,000; Option B 5 plots ×
      1,200 = 6,000) — which one is elected, and by whom (usually developer's sole discretion)
- [ ] **Unit count** as shown in the internal project sheet — this frequently disagrees with
      the addendum's plot count (real case: addendum = 4 or 5 plots, project sheet = **3
      units**). Put the discrepancy to the user in the covering message; leave Schedule B
      blank rather than inventing plot/villa numbers.
- [ ] Goodwill / non-refundable consideration already paid (cheque no., date, bank)
- [ ] Any loan advanced by the developer to the landowner + security mechanism (lien, sale of
      a unit at a fixed ₹/sq.ft.) — must appear in the encumbrance schedule
- [ ] Internal errors in the source documents themselves (real case: the Second Addendum
      described the First Addendum as "dated 23.12.2025" when it is dated 31.10.2025).
      Use the true date and flag it — a rectification note may be needed at the SRO.
- [ ] Cross-check the areas arithmetically: `BUA = multiplier × plot area`; the developer's
      BUA should equal `total residential area × FAR − landowner BUA`.

**Never fabricate plot/villa numbers.** Leave them as clearly-marked blanks in Schedule B
and ask. Inventing a plot number in a registrable instrument is the worst possible failure.

## 3. Clause skeleton (24 clauses — works for both variants)

Each clause should expressly carry forward its parent JDA clause by number, so the sharing
agreement is self-evidently supplemental.

1. Definitions and interpretation (define Developed Site Area, BUA, Common Areas and
   Facilities, Owner's Allocation, Owner's Plots, Owner's Villas, Owner's BUA)
2. Schedule Property (description + all boundaries, incorporated by reference)
3. Project, approvals and RERA registration (sanction nos., RERA no., designated accounts,
   promoter/allottee status, Rule 9 / Annexure A agreement for sale)
4. **Owner's Allocation — final and confirmed** (site area + BUA + configuration table +
   identification + allocation-anywhere-in-layout + limitation of entitlement + developer's
   balance share + finality)
5. **Allotment and vesting of title** (vesting on registration; proportionate undivided share
   in common areas; further assurances; no irrevocable PoA for the owner's share)
6. Construction of the owner's villas / units and specifications (5-year structural defect
   liability under RERA s.14)
7. BUA entitlement — surplus (compensable at prevailing selling price) and shortfall (to be
   made good by the developer); no reduction of the owner's BUA
8. Measurement and area adjustment (deficit only; two methods at developer's discretion)
9. Common areas, amenities and maintenance
10. Possession and handover (mirror the JDA timeline + conditions precedent + deemed delivery)
11. Representations and warranties of the owner
12. Representations and warranties of the developer
13. **Lien and right of recovery of the developer** (first and paramount charge over the
    owner's allocation for all dues, incl. any loan; release within 15 days of full payment)
14. Confirmation, consent and relinquishment by confirming parties
15. Indemnity (both ways)
16. Taxes, statutory dues, **stamp duty and registration**
17. NOC, sale by the owner and nomination
18. Force majeure and extension of time (incl. landowner-attributable delay)
19. Co-operation and further assurances
20. Notices
21. Dispute resolution and jurisdiction
22. Relationship with the principal agreements (supplemental; this agreement prevails only to
    the extent of conflict; addenda continue)
23. Entire agreement, severability, amendment
24. Execution in counterparts and governing law

Schedules: **A** property description with boundaries · **B** allotment (plots/villas, areas,
configurations, boundaries, confirmation) · **C** specifications + BUA statement ·
**D** project and approval particulars · **E** encumbrances, lien and outstanding dues ·
**F** principal agreements and documents register.

## 4. Statutory references to cite (TN plotted-villa variant)

- Registration Act, 1908 — s.17 (compulsory registration), s.23 (time for presentation), s.49
- Indian Stamp Act, 1899 as in force in Tamil Nadu + **Tamil Nadu Stamp (Prevention of
  Undervaluation of Instruments) Rules, 1968** (guideline value; duty on the higher of
  consideration or guideline value)
- Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 — ss.4, 11, 13, 14, 17, 18, 19
- Tamil Nadu Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Rules, 2017 — Rule 9 + Annexure A
- Tamil Nadu Town and Country Planning Act, 1971; Tamil Nadu Combined Development and
  Building Rules, 2019
- Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996; Powers of Attorney Act, 1882

For the KA apartment variant substitute: Karnataka Apartment Ownership Act, 1972; BBMP
building plan sanction (LP No.); K-RERA.

**Stamp duty on the instrument itself:** the sharing agreement is a registrable instrument,
NOT a first-sale villa sale. Do not apply the TN composite first-sale slabs (4%/5%/7% + 2%
registration) to it — those apply to villa *sales*. State the duty basis (consideration vs
guideline value) and recommend confirming at the jurisdictional Sub-Registrar before
execution. Default to **stamp duty and registration borne by the Developer**, matching the
Ranka Amber precedent — but ask, since it is a commercial choice.

## 6. Reading the source scans and producing the deliverables (this environment)

Registered deeds are image-only. Extract text with the OCR venv + the tesseract CLI
(`pytesseract` is not installed — call the binary):

```bash
# /opt/data/.venv-ocr/bin/python
import pymupdf, subprocess
d = pymupdf.open(path)
for i, pg in enumerate(d):
    pg.get_pixmap(dpi=300, colorspace=pymupdf.csGRAY).save(f'/tmp/ocr/{i:02d}.png')
    r = subprocess.run(['tesseract', f'/tmp/ocr/{i:02d}.png', 'stdout',
                        '-l', 'eng', '--psm', '6'], capture_output=True, text=True)
    print(r.stdout)
```

300 dpi grayscale + `--psm 6` reads KAVERI/TN registration endorsements, stamp certificates
and Aadhaar slips reliably. Page numbers and the "Document No. … of 2025 of Book …" stamps
come out interleaved with body text — ignore the noise, but read the endorsements, they carry
the registration number, date, deficit stamp duty and registration fee.

Produce .docx and .pdf from one content list:

- **DOCX**: `python-docx` in `/opt/data/.venv-docx`
- **PDF**: `weasyprint` (HTML → PDF) in `/opt/data/.venv-render`
- No LibreOffice / pandoc on this host, so do not plan on a `soffice --convert-to` step.
- Starter builder: `templates/legal_doc_docx_pdf_builder.py` — copy it, replace the content
  blocks, and it emits matching .docx + .pdf + .txt from a single source list.

## 7. Filing the deliverable

Verify write access to the destination **before** attempting the upload. Query the parent:

```
files().get(fileId=<parent>, fields='capabilities(canAddChildren),owners(emailAddress)')
```

Shared project folders under `Sevaganapalli Land Partners / 01_Legal_and_Title_Docs /
JDA_GPA_SPA / JDA and GPA` are owned by other accounts (sales1.blr, ndr, presales.blr) and
psingh@draas.com holds only read-only access — `files().create` returns
`403 insufficientParentPermissions`. In that case save the .docx / .pdf / native Google Doc
to the user's **own My Drive** and say so in the message, rather than silently failing or
filing somewhere arbitrary.

To create a native Google Doc from the .docx in one call, upload with
`body={'mimeType': 'application/vnd.google-apps.document'}` and the docx mime type on the
media body — Drive converts on upload.
