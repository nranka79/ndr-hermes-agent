# UDS (Undivided Share of Land) Calculation for Property Sale Agreements

**When to use this:** Drafting sale deeds, allotment letters, Agreements of Sale, or any property transaction document that needs UDS values per unit. Also when adding UDS columns to area statement sheets or inventory tracking.

---

## What is UDS?

UDS (Undivided Share of Land) is the proportionate ownership each apartment owner holds in the total land area of the project. It is a mandatory field in sale deeds and agreements of sale for apartment projects under RERA.

---

## How to Calculate UDS (Formula)

```
UDS per sqft of SBUA = Total Plot Area ÷ Total Super Built-up Area of all units

Per-Unit UDS = Unit's Super Built-up Area × UDS per sqft of SBUA
```

### Ranka Amber worked example (June 2026):

| Variable | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Total plot area (sanctioned plan) | 14,000 sqft |
| Sum of all Super Built-up Areas | ~31,853 sqft |
| UDS factor | 14,000 ÷ 31,853 = **0.4395** |
| Unit 101 (Super Built-up 1,687 sqft) | 1,687 × 0.4395 = **741.5 sqft UDS** |

---

## Critical Rules — Which Land Area to Use

The total land area for UDS calculation follows this hierarchy:

1. **Use the sanctioned plan area** — the area shown in the BBMP / planning authority sanctioned plan
2. **If actual surveyed area is LARGER** than sanctioned area → Use sanctioned area (cannot exceed sanctioned)
3. **If actual surveyed area is SMALLER** than sanctioned area → Use the actual surveyed area (lesser of the two)

**Rule:** Always use the **lesser of sanctioned plan area vs actual surveyed area**.

---

## UDS Across Multiple Area Statements

Projects typically maintain two area statements:

| Version | Purpose | Area |
|---------|---------|------|
| **Sanctioned plan-based** | Used in agreements, RERA, sharing docs | Base area (as per approval) |
| **Execution drawing-based** | Actual built area (may be slightly larger) | Permissible deviation |

### Critical rule — UDS stays fixed across both versions

Even though the execution built-up area may be larger due to permissible deviations, the **UDS per unit must remain the same** in both versions. Rationale:

- UDS is already allocated between landowner and developer (e.g. 50:50 sharing)
- Once allocated, it does not change
- When entering Agreements of Sale, reference the sanctioned plan SBUA but note permissible deviation
- Deliver the execution area — but UDS stays identical

---

## Where UDS Is Needed

- **Agreements of Sale** — every unit's sale agreement must state UDS
- **Sale deeds** — final registered sale deed
- **Allotment letters** — initial allotment to buyer
- **Kelsa inventory / cost sheets** — internal tracking
- **Sharing agreements** — between landowner and developer

---

## Format for Area Statement Sheet

The sanctioned plan area statement sheet should contain per-unit columns:

| Column | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Unit # | 101 |
| Share | LO (Landowner) / DEV (Developer) |
| Configuration | 3 BHK Double Balcony |
| Floor | First |
| Unit Built-up Area (sqft) | 1,362 |
| RERA Carpet Area (sqft) | 1,248 |
| Super Built-up Area (sqft) | 1,687 |
| **UDS (sqft)** | **741.5** |
