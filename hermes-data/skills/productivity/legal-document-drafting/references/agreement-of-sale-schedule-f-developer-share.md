# Schedule F — Developer's Share from JDA (Agreement of Sale)

## When to Use

When the user asks to regenerate **Schedule F** (Description of Property covered as per Sanctioned Plan) in the Agreement of Sale Proforma, and the **vendor is the Developer** who acquired rights via a **Joint Development Agreement (JDA)** — not via a direct registered sale deed from the original landowner.

## The Pattern

### Original (Landowner as Vendor)

> The Vendor is the absolute Landowners and in peaceful possession... acquired through Registered Sale Deed... bounded on the...
>
> East by : Property of Mr. Saldhana and Plot No. 1A
> West by : Plot No. 7 and No. 8
> North by : Layout Road and Plot No. 1A
> South by : Plot No. 2

### Updated (Developer as Vendor via JDA)

> The Vendor is the Developer and has acquired the development rights and ownership entitlement in respect of the Schedule Property by virtue of a Registered Joint Development Agreement dated [Date], registered as **Doc No. [Number]** at the office of the [Sub-Registrar Office], and the Vendor is in absolute and peaceful possession and enjoyment of the same. The details of the Schedule Property as per the sanctioned plan are as follows:
>
> **Total Sital Area:** [Area] Sq.Mtrs
> situated at [Village], [Hobli], [Taluk], [District] — [City].
>
> And bounded on the:
>
> East by : [Boundary 1]
> West by : [Boundary 2]
> North by : [Boundary 3]
> South by : [Boundary 4]

## What Changes

| Original Element | Updated To |
|---|---|
| "absolute Landowners" (landowner name) | **"Developer"** |
| "acquired through Registered Sale Deed" | **"acquired development rights and ownership entitlement by virtue of Registered JDA dated [Date]"** |
| Sale Deed registration details | **JDA registration details** (Doc No., Sub-Registrar Office) |
| "converted from Agricultural..." (may be omitted if already converted) | JDA reference + **GPA details** if applicable |
| Landowner name as entity | **Developer company name + CIN** |
| Boundaries | **Unchanged** (taken from the JDA/EC, not from plan sanction conditions) |

## Key Rules

1. **Keep it brief.** No explanatory paragraphs about how the developer got the land, who the earlier owners were, or dissolution of earlier entities. Just the JDA doc reference and the boundary schedule.
   - ✅ "acquired development rights by virtue of Registered JDA dated 16.08.2025 (Doc No. SHV-1-02227-2025-26)"
   - ❌ "These lands were originally part of M/s. Satvik Developers, which was dissolved and its assets partitioned amongst its erstwhile partners..."
   
2. **Boundaries come from the EC/JDA, not the plan sanction conditions.** The sanctioned plan gives the total area; the EC/JDA gives the correct N/E/S/W boundaries.

3. **Include GPA details** when the vendor's authority flows through a General Power of Attorney registered alongside the JDA.

4. **Area in square meters** — use the sanctioned plan's sital area figure.

5. **If the sital area is in irregular shape**, note: "Total area calculated as per total station sketch and engineering area calculation."

## Pitfall

Do not revert to the original "absolute Landowners" framing when the user asks to update Schedule F for a JDA-based project. The Developer is not the landowner — they hold development rights. The wording should clearly state "development rights and ownership entitlement by virtue of Registered JDA."
