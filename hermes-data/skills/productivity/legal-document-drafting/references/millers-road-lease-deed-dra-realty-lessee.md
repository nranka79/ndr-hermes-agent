## Tenant's Risk Register (12 Risks)
1. **Premature Vacation / Eviction** — compensation formula at pre-agreed depreciated rates (not market / not actual bills)
2. **Yield / Sub-lease Risk** — sub-lease rights must be absolute; no interference from lessor
3. **Title / Encumbrance Risk** — lessor warrants clean title; indemnity for any claims
4. **Ground Floor Handover Delay** — specific handover date; penalty or rent reduction if delayed; termination right on extended delay
5. **Lock-in Mismatch** — 3-year tenant lock-in confirmed; beyond 3 years, exit with 3 months' notice; no automatic renewal without consent
6. **Structural Alteration Clarity** — broad permission for all alterations except RCC; no separate approvals needed
7. **Maintenance Escalation** — maintenance escalation capped or tied to formula; tenant voting rights in Association
8. **Sub-lease / Assignment** — absolute right to sub-lease on same or stricter terms; assignment with intimation only
9. **Branding & Naming Rights** — exclusive branding across entire building; no competing signage
10. **Utility Interruptions** — lessor warrants uninterrupted essential services; tenant may install independent backup
11. **Security Deposit Recovery** — deposit in separate account; interest accrues to tenant; no deduction without mutual audit
12. **Force Majeure** — rent suspension during FM; min 6 months suspension right; termination if FM exceeds 12 months
