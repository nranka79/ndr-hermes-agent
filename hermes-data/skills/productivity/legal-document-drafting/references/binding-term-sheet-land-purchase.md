# Binding Term Sheet — Land Purchase (Indian Real Estate)

A binding term sheet for outright land purchase differs from an MOU or LOI — it creates enforceable obligations while the formal Sale Deed / Agreement to Sell is being prepared.

## Structure

1. **Parties** — Buyer (Company), Seller (Individual/Firm)
2. **Property Details** — Village, Taluk, District, State, Survey Numbers, Extent (Acres), Property Type, Intended Use
3. **Financial Terms** — Rate per Acre, Total Consideration, Token/Advance Amount, Balance payable on registration
4. **Payment Schedule** — Stage-wise table (Token on signing, Balance at registration)
5. **Conditions Precedent to Close** — Title clearance, EC, Patta, encumbrances, dues, NA conversion, **survey measurement**, **fencing/compounding**
6. **Legal Due Diligence** — 60-day outer limit, **document delivery within 5 days** of legal counsel's written request, **clarifications within 5 days**, auto-extension for seller delay, **access for due diligence** (prior notice + seller's representative present)
7. **General Terms & Conditions** — **Binding language** (seller acknowledges Buyer's costs, no withdrawal/negotiation with 3rd parties), double refund on seller default, forfeiture on buyer default, registration charges, TDS, governing law, **disclosure warranty** (3-year disclosure, ongoing during DD)
8. **Confidentiality**
9. **Signatures**
10. **Binding closing paragraph** — Right to specific performance

## Key Clauses for Adjoining Land Purchase

### Survey and Measurement
```text
Actual survey by Government Surveyor and/or Private Surveyor.
Superimpose FMB and Patta records.
Payment on WHICHEVER IS LESS: actual physical extent OR Patta extent.
Patta extent must conform to FMB records.
```

### Fencing / Compounding
```text
Seller arranges fencing/compounding of entire boundary.
Buyer bears cost.
Seller guarantees no disputes with neighbouring landowners.
```

### Document Delivery
```text
Seller provides all documents within 5 days of written request
by Buyer's appointed legal counsel.
Timelines extend automatically by period of seller's delay.
```

### Disclosure Warranty
```text
(i) Property free from all encumbrances, mortgages, charges, litigations.
(ii) Seller clears any before registration.
(iii) All notices/orders/proceedings in last 3 years disclosed (even if resolved).
(iv) Ongoing disclosure during DD period.
```

### Access for Due Diligence
```text
Buyer + authorised reps (surveyors, architects, engineers, legal)
may access property with reasonable prior written notice.
Access in presence of Seller's representative (deputed promptly).
```

## Formatting (Google Docs)

When updating an existing term sheet via Google Docs API:

1. **All text pure black** (RGB 0,0,0) — no navy, blue, or gray fonts
2. **Section headings** — Arial 12pt Bold, light gray background (RGB 0.9, 0.9, 0.9) for print compatibility
3. **Body content** — Arial 10pt Normal, white/transparent background
4. **Title** — Arial 22pt Bold
5. **Subtitle** — Arial 13pt Bold
6. **Sub-headings** — Arial 11pt Bold
7. **Bullet content** — 3pt spacing above/below, consistent
8. **Table left column** — Arial 10pt Bold (headers)
9. **Table right column** — Arial 10pt Normal
10. **Paragraph spacing** — section headings 10pt above/4pt below, sub-headings 8pt/4pt, bullets 3pt/3pt

## Rename "Term Sheet" → "BINDING TERM SHEET"

Change the heading and all references throughout the document:
- Title: "BINDING TERM SHEET FOR PURCHASE OF LAND"
- Remove any "non-binding" language in General Terms
- Final paragraph should state binding nature + right to specific performance
