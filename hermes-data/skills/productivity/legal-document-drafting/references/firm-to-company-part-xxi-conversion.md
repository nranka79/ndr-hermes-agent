# Firm → Private Limited Company under Part XXI (Companies Act 2013) — Continuity + TN Stamp/Patta

Verified 2026-09-22 for Sevaganapalli Land Partners (SPL) / Ranka Oasis conversion question posed by NDR.

## The conversion route (NOT fresh incorporation)

- **Part I of Chapter XXI of Companies Act 2013, Ss.366–374** read with **Companies (Authorised to Register) Rules, 2014** (Form URC-1 + URC-2 ad).
- S.366(1) (amended w.e.f. 15.08.2018 by Companies (Amendment) Act 2017): "company" includes a **partnership firm** applying to register.
- S.366(2) proviso (vii) + Rule 3(1) (as amended 15.08.2018): **2+ members** suffice; **<7 members MUST register as a private company** (the old 7-member bar was removed in 2018 to let small property-owning firms corporatise — Vinod Kothari note).
- S.367: Registrar certifies the firm "is incorporated as a company under this Act". S.368: all property (movable AND immovable, incl. actionable claims) **passes to and vests in the company** — no conveyance. S.369: rights/liabilities/**contracts survive** registration.
- Rule 3(2) attachments: partner list (within 6 clear days), proposed directors (DIN + S.164 affidavits), verified partnership deed copy, CA statement of assets/liabilities (**within 15 days of filing**), statement of nominal capital, **NOC of ALL secured creditors** (or declaration of none), consent of majority of partners.
- Rule 3(3): notarised affidavit of all partners → dissolution intimation to Registrar of Firms after registration. Rule 4: **21 clear days** public notice via URC-2 in English + vernacular paper in the firm's district (Bangalore here).
- File URC-1 linked with SPICe (INC-32); ROC Bangalore; non-STP manual review — expect queries.
- Correct label: **statutory succession / change of constitution of the firm into a company** (a firm is not a juristic person under Partnership Act s.4 — never say "same legal entity" to a judge; say "vesting by operation of law under s.368 + savings under s.369").

## Continuity case law (verified)

- **Vali Pattabhirama Rao v. Sri Ramanuja Ginning & Rice Factory (P) Ltd., AIR 1984 AP 176 (DB)** — firm→company under Part IX: property vests in company by statute; **no separate conveyance needed** (also cites Ramasundari Ray v. Syamendra Lal Ray, ILR 2 Cal 1).
- **UoI v. Mahalaxmi Saw Mills (P) Ltd., Delhi HC LPA 2514-15/2005** — no conveyance needed ⇒ no transfer ⇒ no levy (unearned increase).
- **CIT v. Chetak Enterprises (P) Ltd., (2020) 423 ITR 267 (SC) / AIR 2020 SC 4305** — firm's statutory benefit (s.80-IA) continues to converted company; vesting ≠ distribution (no s.45(4)).
- **Sozin Flora Pharma LLP v. State of HP (HP HC, CWP 4019/2020)** — firm→LLP (s.58 LLP Act): vesting by statute ⇒ no stamp duty/registration for record correction.
- **M/s Security Products (P) Ltd. v. State of HP (HP HC, 19.07.2024)** — firm→company conversion: revenue directed to mutate **without duty**; cites Vali Pattabhirama + Sozin Flora.
- HP HC public→private conversion (reported 05.03.2020): entity subsists without discontinuity; no duty.
- Distinguished: **Hindustan Lever v. State of Maharashtra (2004) 9 SCC 438 (SC)** (court ORDER under s.394 = instrument/conveyance — Bombay Act expressly covered it; N/A to Part XXI) and **State of TN v. Serene Estate batch (Madras HC, WA 758/2022 etc., 19.02.2024)** (NCLT amalgamation orders = Art 23 conveyances; 2%-of-property limb of G.O.Ms.No.29/2019 + G.O.Ms.No.47/2020 survives; 0.6%-of-share limb struck down).

## TN stamp duty + patta (SPL facts)

- TN law = Indian Stamp Act 1899 as amended in TN (latest TN Act 13 of 2024 w.e.f. 03.05.2024). S.3 charges **instruments**; no instrument ⇒ no duty. Art 23 conveyance = 7%; Art 46 partnership deed flat ₹1,000.
- Part XXI conversion: NO instrument of transfer executed (vesting by statute) → no TN duty, no Registration Act s.17 registration. Never execute a contribution/conveyance/rectification deed.
- No TN precedent squarely on Part XXI firm→company conversion — honest gap. De-risk via: written SRO classification → District Registrar/CCRA adjudication (s.31-32/56) → writ (AP/Delhi/HP precedent supports). Worst case benchmark = 2% reconstruction limb, not 7%.
- Patta: TN Patta Pass Book Act 1983 s.5 — Tahsildar mutates on report; mutation is administrative (fee nominal), **no stamp duty on mutation**. Document pack: S.367 certificate, deed copies, CA certificate, affidavits, NOCs, existing patta/EC/FMB.
- Flags: TN Land Reforms (Fixation of Ceiling on Land) Act 1961 — company holding agri land (verify succession-vs-acquisition); RERA promoter-name update; JDA no-assignment clauses → confirmatory recordal only.

## Tax/GST notes

- IT Act 1961 **s.47(xiii)**: conversion not a "transfer" if all assets+liabilities pass, shareholders = partners only, share ratio = capital-account ratio, no consideration other than shares, ≥50% voting power held 5 years. Breach → retroactive capital gains. New Income-tax Act 2025 has successor continuity conditions — verify with tax counsel.
- GST: succession — transfer ITC via s.18(3) CGST / Rule 41 (GST ITC-02); new company PAN/GSTIN.

## Pitfalls

- Don't file stale CA statement (>15 days) — top ROC query cause.
- Don't settle any partner in cash at conversion.
- If a secured loan (e.g. Jiraaf interim) exists at filing — NOC mandatory (Rule 3(2)(g)); sequence financing around conversion.
- Always verify the CURRENTLY REGISTERED partnership deed partner names/ratios (SPL records showed "DRA Realty Pvt Ltd"; NDR dictation said "DRA Projects Pvt Ltd" — mismatch flagged to verify).
