# Karnataka Sale Deed — Bangalore Rural / Devanahalli Taluk Pattern

Real example: **C.R. Nagendra → DRA Realty Pvt Ltd** (July 2026)
Properties: Survey Nos. 221/2, 176/2, 223 at Byadarahalli Village, Devanahalli Taluk

## Key Conventions for Karnataka Agricultural Land Sale Deeds

### 1. Property Description Hierarchy

```
Village → Hobli → Taluk → District → State
```

Example: Byadarahalli Village, Kundana Hobli, Devanahalli Taluk, Bengaluru Rural District, Karnataka

### 2. Survey Number Notation

Karnataka documents typically use the pattern:

- `Survey No. 221/2 (Old Survey Nos. 18 and 221)` — old survey numbers in parentheses
- Extent: `03 Acres 38 Guntas of Kharab` (note: "Kharab" = inclusive of non-cultivable area)
- Always specify whether extent is gross or net (kharab = inclusive of waste/uncultivable)

### 3. Boundary Description Format

From registered mortgage deed (Feb 2024):

**Item 1 — Sy.No. 221/2 (3 Ac 38 Guntas):**
```
On the East by:    Land belonging to A.K. Poojappa
On the West by:    Land belonging to A.K. Janga and Danada Oni
On the North by:   Land belonging to Chikkamuniyappa
On the South by:   Land belonging to Ameerappa
```

**Item 2 — Sy.No. 176/2 (1 Ac 20 Guntas):**
```
On the East by:    Lands bearing Survey Nos. 176/1, 176/3, 176/4, 176/5, and 176/6
On the West by:    Land bearing Survey No. 175/6
On the North by:   Lands bearing Survey Nos. 224, 225, 226, and 227
On the South by:   Village Border
```

Note the two styles:
- **Owner-based boundaries** ("Land belonging to...") — used when adjacent land is owned by named individuals
- **Survey-based boundaries** ("Lands bearing Survey No...") — used when adjacent land is identified by survey number

### 4. Place of Execution

- **Bangalore city** — for properties in Bengaluru Urban/Rural districts
- Registration at the jurisdictional Sub-Registrar office (Devanahalli for Byadarahalli properties)
- Unlike TN deals where the place is often Bagalur (Krishnagiri)

### 5. Source of Title Reference

Standard wording from this session's deal:

> "The VENDOR has derived his title to the said Schedule Property through a Registered Partition Cum Settlement Deed dated 16 January 2024, registered as Document No. SRJ-1-10373-2023-24, executed amongst the erstwhile partners of M/s. Satvik Developers, wherein the Schedule Property was allocated to the VENDOR."

Key elements to always capture:
- Document type (Partition Deed, Sale Deed, Gift Deed, etc.)
- Date of execution
- Registration number (SRO-DocNo-Year format)
- Names of parties involved
- How the current owner derived title

### 6. Mortgage / Encumbrance Disclosure Pattern

When there's a prior mortgage that gets discharged simultaneously:

> "save and except the Mortgage Deed dated [date] executed by the VENDOR in favour of [Mortgagee] which mortgage shall be simultaneously discharged and released upon execution of this Deed"

Add a standalone Mortgage Discharge clause:

> **Mortgage Discharge Clause:**
> "The VENDOR confirms that the Mortgage Deed dated [date] executed by the VENDOR in favour of [Mortgagee] over the Schedule Property shall stand fully discharged and released upon execution and registration of this Deed. The VENDEE shall be entitled to a clear and unencumbered title free of any mortgage."

### 7. Consideration Clause Pattern

For direct purchase by a company (DRA Realty Pvt Ltd):

> "2.1 The total sale consideration for the Schedule Property is Rs. __________
> 2.2 The VENDEE has paid, and the VENDOR has received, the entire sale consideration prior to or simultaneously with the execution of this Deed."

Note: Section 194-IA TDS threshold (₹50L) governs whether TDS applies — keep the standard TDS representation from the existing template.

### 8. Parties Format

**VENDOR (Individual):**
```
Mr. [FULL NAME], aged about __ years, son of __________, 
an Indian citizen and resident, residing at [FULL ADDRESS], 
having PAN No. [PAN] and Aadhaar No. [AADHAAR]
```

**VENDEE (Company — DRA Realty):**
```
M/s. DRA REALTY PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated under 
the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, bearing CIN [CIN], 
having its registered office at [ADDRESS], represented herein by 
its Director, Mr. [NAME], Son of [FATHER'S NAME], residing at [ADDRESS], 
having Aadhaar No. [AADHAAR] and PAN No. [PAN]
```

## Comparison with TN (Thindlu/Hosur) Template

| Aspect | Tamil Nadu (Hosur) | Karnataka (Devanahalli) |
|--------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Village hierarchy | Village, Taluk, District | Village, Hobli, Taluk, District |
| Survey notation | `Sy.No.240/3A` | `Sy.No.221/2 (Old Sy.No.18 and 221)` |
| Extent unit | Hectares/Acres | Acres & Guntas |
| Place of execution | Bagalur, Krishnagiri | Bangalore, Karnataka |
| RERA compliance | TN RERA, HNTDA approval | Not applicable (agricultural land) |
| SPA reference | Often via SPA (Special Power of Attorney) | Direct owner execution |
| Title chain | Often multi-step (gift → partition → sale) | Often via Partition Deed from dissolved firm |
| Registration office | Sub-Registrar, Bagalur | Sub-Registrar, Devanahalli |

### 9. Partnership → Partition → Individual Sale — Title Flow Pattern

A common Byadarahalli pattern: the VENDOR was a partner in a dissolved firm that originally purchased the land. The title flows through three stages:

**Stage 1 — Firm Acquisition:** The partnership firm (e.g., M/s. Satvik Developers) purchases the land from original owners via registered sale deeds. Both items may come from different source deeds with different vendors.

**Stage 2 — Partition Deed:** The firm is dissolved via a Registered Partition Cum Settlement Deed. Specific survey numbers are allocated to each partner. The partition deed is the VENDOR's source of title.

**Stage 3 — Individual Sale:** The former partner (now individual owner) sells the allocated parcels to the current VENDEE.

#### Recital Structure for This Pattern

Organize the WHEREAS recitals into clear lettered sections:

```
A. [Title chain for Item 1] — Chain from original grant/proprietor → ... → firm's acquisition
B. [Title chain for Item 2] — Separate chain if different source deed
C. [Partition Deed] — Firm dissolution + allocation to VENDOR
D. [Agreement to Sell] — Consideration, encumbrances, capacity
```

**Critical wording for the Partition Deed recital:**

> "The said Partnership Firm was subsequently dissolved and the erstwhile partners of M/s. [FIRM NAME], namely [Partner 1] and [Partner 2], executed a Registered Partition Cum Settlement Deed dated [DATE], registered as Document No. [REG NO], in the Office of the Sub-Registrar, [SRO], wherein and whereby the Schedule Properties described hereinbelow, being Item No. 1 — [Sy. No.] and Item No. 2 — [Sy. No.], were allocated to the share of [VENDOR], with full and absolute rights, title and interest therein."

**Partner's contribution acknowledgment (when applicable):**

When a non-vendor partner (e.g., Ashok Kumar) originally funded the acquisition, add a separate recital clause:

> "The VENDOR acknowledges that the funds for the original acquisition of the Schedule Properties by M/s. [FIRM NAME] were advanced by the erstwhile partner [NAME], who paid a sum of Rs. [AMOUNT] towards the purchase of the Schedule Properties, and the said amount stands duly settled and accounted for under the said Partition Cum Settlement Deed."

This is a **recital of fact** (source of funds history), NOT a consideration clause. It does not form part of the current sale consideration.

#### Source Deed Metadata Extraction

For each source deed in the chain, extract and include in the recital:

| Field | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Source Deed type | Absolute Sale Deed / Sale Deed |
| Vendor in source deed | Smt. G. Bujjamma / Smt. H.C. Sudha |
| Purchaser in source deed | M/s. Satvik Developers (rep. by Partner C.R. Nagendra) |
| Document registration | DNH-1-15518-2022-23 / DNH-1-08987-2022-23 |
| SRO | Devanahalli |
| Registration date | 09-12-2022 / 20-10-2022 |
| Consideration | Rs. 2,25,00,000 / Rs. 1,35,30,000 |
| Items conveyed | Sy.221/2 (3A+0-38G kharab) / Sy.176/2 (1A 20G) among others |

#### Registration Number Format Convention

- Bangalore Sub-Registrar partition deeds: **SRJ-1-XXXXX-YYYY-YY** (SRJ series)
- Devanahalli Sub-Registrar sale deeds: **DNH-1-XXXXX-YYYY-YY** (DNH series)
- CD/Book No.: **DNHDXXXX** (for Devanahalli)
- Always include the CD storage number for the source deed

## When to Use This Pattern

Use this Karnataka pattern when:
- The property is in **Bengaluru Urban / Bengaluru Rural / Ramanagara / Kolar / Chikkaballapur** districts
- The property is **agricultural land** being sold to a company
- The title flows through a **Partition Deed** or **Family Arrangement Deed**
- The seller is an **individual** (not a firm/company)
- The buyer is **DRA Realty Pvt Ltd** or another DRA entity
