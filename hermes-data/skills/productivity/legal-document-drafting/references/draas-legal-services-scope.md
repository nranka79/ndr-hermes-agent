# DRAAS — Master List of Legal Services (9-Section Scope)

**Source:** Email "Legal Services Needed" sent to Sadananda Naganur (Sadananda & Co.) on 10 Sep 2024. Reused for Aamir Khan (aamirkhan@me.com) on 4 Jun 2026.

**Purpose:** Comprehensive scope of legal services for DRAAS real estate business — cover all due diligence, documentation, litigation, RERA, and document management needs with a single law firm retainer.

---

## 1. Preliminary Due Diligence

- Examine title-related documents and revenue-related documents
- Verify up-to-date Encumbrance Certificates (ECs) and Record of Rights, Tenancy & Crops (RTCs)
- Check RTC entries alignment with title flow
- Perform extent check (RTC, Aakar Bal/FMB, title, original grant/purchase)
- Examine village map and revenue records for land classification (e.g. lake, grazing land, Kharab lands)
- Verify property contiguity to public road through non-Kharab portions
- Conduct litigation search for property and title holders
- Online search for adverse reports or case mentions

## 2. Detailed Legal Due Diligence

- Thoroughly study and verify all critical documents for authenticity
- Obtain certified copies from relevant departments
- Check corroborated evidence from neighbouring lands
- Verify physical location and boundaries as per documents
- Apply for NOCs from government departments to check for potential land acquisition
- Analyse developability under current town planning norms
- Identify applicable buffer zones and development restrictions
- Assess potential for land use change and any impediments
- Identify relevant town planning authority and current zoning

## 3. Physical Verification

- Conduct site visit to assess soil condition
- Verify physical access to property from the road
- Check for signs of illegal development or unregistered sales
- Identify potential issues: high tension wires, village roads, nalas, water bodies, quarries, wells, burial grounds, temples, etc.

## 4. Physical Boundary Establishment

- Reestablish boundaries as per revenue records
- Ensure physical site conforms with documented boundaries
- Prepare documents for actual physical extent for purchase consideration

## 5. Documentation Services

- Advise on transaction structure to minimise costs and taxes
- Prepare MOUs, partnership/LLP/company documents
- Create Joint Development Agreements
- Prepare sale deeds, sale agreements, deferred payment arrangements
- Draft applications for change of land use
- Provide support for licensing processes
- Draft letters to statutory departments for missing records

## 6. Litigation Support Services (5 years post-acquisition)

- Create and respond to legal notices
- File objections, petitions, and interim applications
- Argue cases in court, file for stay orders and injunctions
- Handle stamp duty and income tax disputes
- Assist with market rate vs. guideline rate discrepancies

## 7. Customer Support Documentary Services

- Respond to and resolve customer legal queries
- Explain document omissions or provide relevant documentation
- Address concerns raised by customers' legal advisors

## 8. RERA-related Documentary Support

- Draft and finalise RERA-compliant documents
- Prepare sale agreements, sale deeds, allotment letters
- Create payment receipts and handover documentation
- Draft deed of declaration for apartment association / society formation
- Prepare society membership documents and common area handover documentation

## 9. Document Management & Accessibility

- Maintain online repository of all title flow documents
- Create and update master index of all documents
- Categorise documents by type, survey numbers, individual names
- Provide hyperlinked documents in title opinions
- Continuously update documents based on comments and feedback
- Track and categorise customer queries and maintain response database

## Historical Fee Reference (Sadananda & Co., Sep 2024)

- **Due Diligence:** Rs 40,000/acre or per survey number (whichever higher), depending on nature and complexity
- **Drafting (MOU, Agreement, PoA, Conveyance):** Rs 30,000/doc minimum
- **DD Period:** Minimum 60-70 years or from origin of title
- **Payment schedule:** 30% on receipt of 1st set of documents / 30% on additional documents / 40% on DDR delivery
- **Exclusions:** Out-of-pocket travel expenses, government approval procurement
- **Litigation support:** Per-case billing, not included in the above fees

When sending this scope to a new law firm, insert a brief cover note asking them to:
1. Confirm which services they can cover
2. Identify any services they specifically cannot provide
3. Share proposed fee structure / pricing breakup per service category
4. Confirm online workflow (Google Docs/Drive) compatibility — DRAAS prefers this over hardcopies
