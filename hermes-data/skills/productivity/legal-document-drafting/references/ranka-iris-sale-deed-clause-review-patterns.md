# Ranka Iris Sale Deed — Clause Review Patterns (June 2026)

These findings emerged from a comprehensive clause-by-clause analysis of the Ranka Iris Proforma Absolute Sale Deed (DRA Developers & Projects Pvt Ltd) against the RERA Notified Format, RERA 2018 Revised Format, executed Ranka Oasis and Ranka Udaya deeds, Embassy Habitat 914EH deed, and the Ranka Iris Occupancy Certificate. They form a reusable checklist for reviewing any DRAAS proforma sale deed.

## Critical Findings Checklist

### 1. BLOCKER — Blanket Discharge Clause (Clause 5.3)
Purchaser declares "no claims" and "fully and completely discharge the Promoter from all their obligations" — executed simultaneously with the sale deed, BEFORE the 5-year defect liability starts. This attempts to contract out of RERA Sections 14(3) and 18. Void under Section 23 Contract Act.

### 2. BLOCKER — Defect Liability Diluted Below RERA
Systematic exclusions in Clause 11 that RERA Section 14(3) does not permit:
- Lifts, generators, STP, gym equipment → excluded entirely (Clause 11.3)
- Basement water seepage → pre-declared "would not constitute a defect" (Clause 11.4)
- "Minor cracks on walls, paint peelings" (Clause 11.2)
- All glass, ceramic, vitrified, porcelain after possession (Clause 11.7)
- Tile shade differences, air pockets, "minor tile chipping" (Clause 11.7)
- 15-day notice requirement for defect claims (Clause 11.5) — unreasonably short

**RERA allows only one exclusion: normal wear and tear.**

### 3. BLOCKER — Super Built-Up Area as Primary Metric
Proforma lists super built-up area first, carpet area second. RERA mandates carpet area as the pricing basis. Term "exclusive common area" in Schedule C is an oxymoron — not recognized in RERA. Car parking spaces lack dimensions and type specification.

### 4. HIGH — OC Deviations Non-Disclosure
The OC revealed:
- ₹62.92 lakhs paid as compounding fee for construction deviations
- Security deposit forfeited due to deviations
- Separate Fire Clearance No. KSFES/CC/069/2026
- EV charging provision required, rainwater harvesting required, organic waste converter required
- Structural safety disclaimer

**None of these were disclosed in the proforma recitals.** The proforma says "completed in all respects" — misleading.

### 5. HIGH — Karnataka Apartment Ownership Act, 1972 Not Referenced
Every RERA-compliant Bangalore apartment sale deed should reference this Act. The 914EH Embassy deed operates under it. The proforma completely omits it.

### 6. HIGH — Undivided Share Left Blank
Schedule B has `_______ Sq. Ft.` with no formula or fraction. Purchaser cannot know their exact share.

### 7. MEDIUM — "Without Right to Question" Association Clause (Clause 8.1)
Owners bound by Deed of Declaration "without there being any right to question the same" — legally void as it attempts to contract out of statutory rights.

### 8. MEDIUM — No Deed of Adherence Mentioned
No reference to any Deed of Adherence for Association membership.

### 9. MEDIUM — Terrace Exclusive Rights (Clause 7.6)
Grants exclusive terrace rights to one apartment, but OC indicates terrace floor is for utility purposes (Lifts Machine Rooms, Staircases Head Rooms, OHT & Solar Panels).

### 10. MEDIUM — 15 Missing RERA-Mandated Clauses
Including: carpet area price basis, 70% separate account, escalation-free price, dispute resolution via RERA, conveyance timeline (3 months post-OC), promoter default consequences, no further mortgage assurance.
