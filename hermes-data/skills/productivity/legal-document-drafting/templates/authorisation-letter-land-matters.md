# Authorisation Letter — Land Revenue, Survey & Development Matters

## When to Use

Draft a letter of authority (not a registered GPA — this is a simpler authorisation letter) when a partnership firm or company needs to authorise a representative to:

- Obtain revenue records (RTCs, mutation entries, certified copies)
- Coordinate with survey department for boundary demarcation / marking
- Collect title deeds, encumbrantee certificates (ECs), legal documents
- Take possession of land, prepare panchnamas
- Engage surveyors for fencing, boundary stones
- Liaise with Revenue, Town Planning, Panchayat, and other departments
- Prepare/submit applications, affidavits, undertakings before authorities
- Appoint advocates, surveyors, engineers as needed

## Structure

```
AUTHORISATION LETTER

Date: _______________

TO WHOMSOEVER IT MAY CONCERN

SUBJECT: AUTHORISATION IN FAVOUR OF [NAME] — [LAND DESCRIPTION]

---

**1. BACKGROUND**

1.1. The [Firm/Company Name] (hereinafter "the Firm") comprises the following [partners/directors]:
    (i) [Partner/Director 1 Name], S/o [Father's Name], [Address]
    (ii) [Partner/Director 2 Name], S/o [Father's Name], [Address]

1.2. [Owner Name] is the absolute owner of the property bearing [Survey No./Sy. No.], situated at [Village], [Hobli], [Taluk], [District], Karnataka.

1.3. [Owner Name], in [his/her/its] capacity as [partner/director], has agreed to contribute/convey the Said Land to the Firm, pursuant to which the Firm shall become the owner.

1.4. For developing a residential/commercial project on the Said Land, the Firm requires various revenue/survey/legal records and departmental approvals.

**2. AUTHORISATION**

We do hereby authorise and appoint Mr./Ms. [Name] ([Nickname/alias]), S/o [Father's Name], R/o [Address], bearing [ID No.], as our lawful representative to act on behalf of the Firm in respect of the Said Land, with full power and authority to:

   (a) Obtain all revenue records, RTCs/MR, mutation entries, certified copies of records of rights from the Tahsildar, Deputy Commissioner, Village Accountant, Survey Department, and any revenue authority;

   (b) Apply for survey records, sketches, maps, field measurement books from the Department of Survey and Settlement, and coordinate with the government surveyor for demarcation and boundary marking;

   (c) Collect title deeds, encumbrance certificates (ECs), sale deeds, partition deeds, and legal documents;

   (d) Undertake land possession, prepare possession receipts/panchnamas, coordinate with concerned parties;

   (e) Engage surveyors for boundary marking, erection of boundary stones/pillars, and fencing;

   (f) Liaise with Revenue Department, Survey Department, Town Planning Authority, Village Panchayat, and other authorities to prepare the Said Land for development;

   (g) Prepare, sign, execute, verify, and submit applications, affidavits, declarations, undertakings, indemnity bonds, and documentation before any government authority, statutory body, or court;

   (h) Appoint and instruct advocates, surveyors, engineers, contractors, and other professionals as necessary;

   (i) Generally do all acts necessary or expedient to give full effect to this authorisation.

**3. UNDERTAKING**

The Firm undertakes to ratify, confirm, and be bound by all acts done by [Name] in exercise of the powers conferred herein and to hold harmless [Name] from claims/liabilities arising out of bonafide exercise of these powers.

**4. VALIDITY**

This Authorisation shall remain valid from the date hereof until revoked in writing or until the purpose is fully accomplished, whichever is later.

**5. INDEMNITY**

The Firm agrees to indemnify [Name] against all losses, costs, proceedings, damages, claims, and expenses incurred by reason of any bonafide act under this Authorisation.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the [Partners/Directors] have hereunto set their hands on the day and year first hereinabove written.

____________________________              ____________________________
[Signatory 1 Name]                         [Signatory 2 Name]
[Designation]                              [Designation]
[Aadhaar: _______________]                 [Aadhaar: _______________]
[PAN: _______________]                     [PAN: _______________]

WITNESSES:

1. ____________________________            2. ____________________________
   Name: _______________                      Name: _______________
   Address: _______________                   Address: _______________
   Signature: _______________                 Signature: _______________
```

## Key Drafting Rules

1. **Background Recitals (Section 1)**: Always include the chain — who owns the land → how the firm acquires rights → why the representative is needed. This establishes authority in the revenue/survey records.

2. **Scope of Authority (Section 2)**: List specific powers (a–i) covering revenue, survey, legal, possession, liaison, documentation, and professional appointments. The catch-all clause (i) covers anything not enumerated.

3. **Undertaking + Indemnity (Sections 3 & 5)**: These protect the authorised representative. Always include both — the undertaking says the firm will ratify acts; the indemnity protects against personal liability.

4. **Validity (Section 4)**: "Until revoked in writing or purpose accomplished" — this survives the usual "valid for N months" constraint. If a specific validity period is needed, add: "This Authorisation shall remain valid for [N] months from date hereof, renewable by mutual consent."

5. **Execution**: Signed by ALL partners/directors of the firm. Two witnesses.

## When NOT to Use

- If the authorisation involves executing registered documents (sale deeds, mortgages) — use a **General Power of Attorney** (registered, stamped). This letter is for appearances before authorities, collecting records, and departmental work only.
- If the representative needs to represent the firm in court proceedings — use a **Vakalatnama / Memorandum of Appearance** instead.
