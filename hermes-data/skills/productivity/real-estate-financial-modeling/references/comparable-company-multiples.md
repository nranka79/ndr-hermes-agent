## IPO Discount/Premium Pattern for Real Estate

When a private real estate company lists, expect:
- **Private (minority, non-marketable) value**: Base (with DLOC 10% + DLOM 22%)
- **Private (controlling, non-marketable)**: ~1.3-1.5x minority value
- **Listed (IPO) valuation**: ~2-4x private minority value (liquidity premium)
- **Post-listing (3-6 month stabilised)**: IPO price to listing gain typically 10-25% for real estate IPOs
- **Established listed peer premium**: Listed companies trade at premium to IPO price over 1-2 years as track record builds

## Notes on Reuse

- Update these figures at least quarterly — multiples shift with market conditions
- The Nifty Realty index P/E of 38x reflects a premium market. In a downturn, real estate PE can compress to 15-20x
- Always check if PAT was boosted by one-time items (land sales, JV income) before applying P/E multiples
- For companies with thin PAT but strong EBITDA (like many developers), EV/EBITDA is more reliable than P/E
