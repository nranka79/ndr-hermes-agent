## Critical Note: Battery Registration
X351 XJ requires the new battery to be **registered** in the ECU after replacement. Without registration, the BMS (Battery Management System) uses old charge parameters, leading to under/overcharging and shortened battery life. Dealer does this automatically; if buying outside, confirm the mechanic has a suitable diagnostic tool.
