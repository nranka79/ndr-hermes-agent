# Standard Marketing & Content Brief Format

This is the canonical section structure for every DRAAS product content brief. Derived from the Ranka Amber & Northstar brief (the original template).

---

### How to read this document
This is Stage 1: your input, organised into a working brief. Everything here is either:
**[Given]** — from you, treated as fact.
**[Verify]** — a claim that anchors the pitch and must be confirmed in the research round before it goes into any content.
**[Missing]** — a gap I need you or research to fill.

The hooks at the end of each section are first drafts to react to — treat these as a starting set, not a final list.

---

## Snapshot
Key project facts with [Given]/[Verify]/[Missing] tags.

Typical fields:
- Product type and size (units/plots, sq ft range)
- Location (area, landmarks, distance from highway)
- Pricing (entry price)
- Neighbours and surroundings
- Macro context (growth corridor, infrastructure)
- Missing items (timelines, unit mix, legal structure)

---

## Positioning statement
One sentence: *For [audience] who want [need] — [product] is [unique value] in [location] at [price advantage].*

---

## (Optional) The macro thesis — Why [Corridor/Area]
Context about the larger growth corridor. Include:
- Infrastructure catalysts (airport, metro, industrial corridors)
- Employment generators (Foxconn, tech parks, factories)
- Price appreciation trends (CAGR, comps)
- Corridor dynamics (last remaining corridor, etc.)

---

## Target audience
Segments with:
- Demographics (age, income, family stage)
- Psychographics (motivations, fears, aspirations)
- The hook that resonates with them

---

## Differentiators
What makes this product unique. Bullet points. Fact-based.

---

## Reasons to buy
**Rational:** Practical, logical reasons.
**Emotional:** Feelings, identity, aspirations.
**Financial:** Investment returns, appreciation, yield.

---

## Competitive angle
Who the real competition is (or isn't). How to frame the comparison. For unique products, state "No direct competitor" and give a conceptual comp.

---

## (Optional) Special factors
Unique angles specific to this project. Examples:
- "The Ashram factor" — permanent serene neighbour
- "Automation stack" — tech differentiator
- "Online management layer" — governance innovation

---

## Hooks & messaging

**Emotional triggers — [theme name]**
Taglines (3-5) with message paragraph.

**Rational/financial triggers — [theme name]**
Taglines (3-5) with message paragraph.

**Lifestyle triggers — [theme name]**
Taglines (2-3) with message paragraph.

---

## Visual & content direction
- Colour palette / mood
- Key visual elements (views, greenery, people)
- Sense of space / privacy
- Tagline direction for covers
