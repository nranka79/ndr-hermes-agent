- **Contact groups** in column Q are delimited by `:::` — e.g., `busA ::: SocialA ::: * myContacts`
- **Notes/context** in column O often contains how Nishant met the person, referral source, and relevant backstory
- The sheet has ~2000+ rows covering all of Nishant's Google contacts
