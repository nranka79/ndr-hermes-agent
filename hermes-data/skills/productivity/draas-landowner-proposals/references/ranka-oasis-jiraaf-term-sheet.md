**13.1** At end of project, compute SLP's net profit on the 6.2 ac. EXCLUDED from the profit pool: (a) all revenue from land, incl. the land-related economic interest passed by Jiraaf to Nishant/SLP (the ₹5.7 Cr/ac × extent = ₹35.34 Cr consideration, net of ₹2.00 Cr goodwill) and all associated payouts; (b) any payouts connected to that land revenue.

**13.3** **15% of that net profit shared with Jiraaf Capital or its assignees, paid at end of project only.**
