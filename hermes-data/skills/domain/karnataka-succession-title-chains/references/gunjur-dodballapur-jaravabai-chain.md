## Caveats / open verification points
1. **Will Item-2 recital is loose:** says "Sy No. 41 ... Doc 302 ... 20-5-1993" but Doc 302 is the Sy 40/2 deed (confirmed on the deed + margin annotation). MR 6/2002-03 proves what stood in her name at death (39 + 40/2 + 41) and what moved to Dharmesh. Recommended: cross-check with EC / Index of Lands (IL) for Sy 41.
2. **Death date conflict:** mutation application says 03-12-1994 (actually the Will execution date); death certificate says 25-03-1995. Later date is correct (Will signed 04-12-1994 stands valid).
3. **MR 17-09-2004 certified copy initially read as "Dinesh" on the Sy 41 row at 150 dpi** — 300-dpi Kannada character-level read confirms `ಧರ್ಮೇಶ್` (Dharmesh) on all three rows. Always zoom before asserting names.
