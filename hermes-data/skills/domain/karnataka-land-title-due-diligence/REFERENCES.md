# Reference register — Karnataka land title due diligence

This is a curated starting list for the agent. It is not a representation that every URL is the latest consolidated text. On every matter, verify current amendments, Gazette notifications, rules and binding judgments before relying on a legal proposition.

## Primary legal texts
- Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964 (official hosted PDF): https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_KA_71_596_00007_12_1551859218716&filename=12_of_1964_%28e%29.pdf&type=actfile
- Karnataka Land Revenue Rules, 1966 (India Code search): https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/1362/simple-search?query=%E0%B2%95%E0%B2%B0%E0%B3%8D%E0%B2%A8%E0%B2%BE%E0%B2%9F%E0%B2%95+%E0%B2%AD%E0%B3%82+%E0%B2%95%E0%B2%82%E0%B2%A6%E0%B2%BE%E0%B2%AF+%E0%B2%A8%E0%B2%BF%E0%B2%AF%E0%B2%AE%E0%B2%97%E0%B2%B3%E0%B3%81%2C+1966&searchradio=rules
- Karnataka SC/ST (Prohibition of Transfer of Certain Lands) Act, 1978 (PTCL), official India Code PDF: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/7812/1/2_of_1979_%28e%29.pdf
- Karnataka Land Reforms Act, 1961: locate current text and amendments through India Code / Karnataka official sources.
- Registration Act, 1908; Transfer of Property Act, 1882; Indian Succession Act, 1925; Hindu Succession Act, 1956; Limitation Act, 1963; Code of Civil Procedure, 1908; Specific Relief Act, 1963; applicable evidence law: verify official current texts and applicability.
- Karnataka Land (Restriction on Transfer) Act, 1991: investigate where a relevant acquisition/project notification may apply.
- Karnataka Town and Country Planning Act, 1961 and applicable local planning laws, zoning regulations and sanctioned plans.

## Official records and portals
- Karnataka Bhoomi / land records: https://landrecords.karnataka.gov.in/
- Bengaluru Urban District land records information: https://bengaluruurban.nic.in/en/service/land-records/
- Department of Survey, Settlement and Land Records (SSLR): use official Karnataka government district/department portals.
- Mojini / official survey services: verify current official portal and service availability.
- Kaveri / registration services: verify current official portal; obtain EC and registered-instrument copies from the relevant jurisdiction.
- e-Courts district judiciary: https://ecourts.gov.in/
- High Court of Karnataka: https://karnatakajudiciary.kar.nic.in/
- Supreme Court of India: https://www.sci.gov.in/
- India Code: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/

## Survey documents to request when relevant
- RTC/Pahani (current and historical)
- Mutation register extracts and mutation orders
- Akarband / settlement register
- Tippani / original survey measurement records
- Pakka Book / atlas / village map / survey sketch / FMB or local equivalent
- Kharab details and classification records
- 11E sketch where applicable
- Phodi/durasti/subdivision application, order, sketch and updated records
- Grant register, grant order, Saguvali Chit, conditions and grant sketch
- Certified copies of relevant court/revenue orders
- Conversion order, approved layout plan and release order where relevant

## Search protocol
For each legal issue, search official current text, amendments, applicable rules, Gazette notifications and controlling judgments. Record exact provisions and the proposition for which they are relevant. Do not rely on a generic blog as the authority for a conclusion.
