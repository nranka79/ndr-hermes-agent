# TN Dual-Entity Deed Restructure — Grouped Recital Pattern

When a project has **two entities** (VENDOR + CONFIRMING PARTY) contributing different
survey numbers to a single plotted development, the standard flat recital list is
insufficient. Use this **grouped-by-entity pattern** per Prakash's approach.

## When to use

- The project has **two separate legal entities** holding title to different surveys
- One entity is the **VENDOR** (owns the specific survey from which the sold plot is carved)
- The other is the **CONFIRMING PARTY / CO-PROMOTER** (owns other project surveys, confirms sale)
- The project is a single consolidated plotted development on land from both entities

## Structure: 8 Groups

### Group 1 — VENDOR's Absolute Title Survey
GROUP 1 — Survey No. [X] (VENDOR — [Entity Name])
(i)  [Sale Deed details — type, date, Doc.No, SRO, parties]
Description: survey number, extent, village, taluk, district
Title Flow: numbered steps from original owner → intermediate transfers → VENDOR
Conclusion: "Thus, the VENDOR became the absolute owner of Survey No. [X]. Patta No. [N]..."

### Group 2 — CONFIRMING PARTY's Bulk Acquisition
GROUP 2 — Survey Numbers acquired under [Source Deed] — CONFIRMING PARTY
(ii)  [Sale Deed details]
[List of survey numbers with extents and per-survey title flows]
Conclusion: "All the above survey numbers aggregating Ac.X.XX were conveyed to the CONFIRMING PARTY..."

### Group 3 — Exchange-acquired Surveys (VENDOR)
GROUP 3 — Survey Numbers acquired by VENDOR under Exchange Deed [Doc.No]
(iii) [Exchange Deed details — both sides (what was given, what was received)]
Title Flow
Conclusion: "Thus, the VENDOR became the absolute owner of Survey Nos. [X, Y, Z]..."

### Group 4 — Exchange-acquired Surveys (CONFIRMING PARTY)
GROUP 4 — Survey No. [X] acquired by CONFIRMING PARTY (Exchange Deed [Doc.No])
(iv)  [Exchange Deed details]
Title Flow

### Group 5 — Additional Sale-acquired (CONFIRMING PARTY)
GROUP 5 — Survey No. [X] acquired by CONFIRMING PARTY (Sale Deed [Doc.No])
(v)  [Sale Deed details]

### Group 6 — JDA/GPA Lands (VENDOR — Development Rights)
GROUP 6 — JDA/GPA Lands (Survey Nos. [X, Y, Z]) — VENDOR ([Entity Name])
(vi)  [JDA details]
(vii) [GPA details]
Survey Numbers:
• [Survey] — [Extent]
Title Flow
Note: These are development rights, not absolute ownership. Title remains with the JDA owner.

### Group 7 — JDA/GPA Lands (VENDOR — Second Parcel)
GROUP 7 — JDA/GPA Lands (Survey Nos. [X, Y]) — VENDOR ([Entity Name])
(viii) [JDA + GPA details]

### Group 8 — Existing Encumbrance
GROUP 8 — Mortgage without Possession (Existing Encumbrance)
(ix)  [Mortgage deed details]
"This mortgage shall be released prior to and/or simultaneously with the registration of this Sale Deed..."

### Consolidation Recital
CONSOLIDATION AND MUTUAL AGREEMENT
Thus, through the above-registered documents:

(a) The VENDOR, [Entity Name], has become the absolute owner of [Survey Nos.] under [Deed refs],
    and holds development rights over [JDA surveys]...
(b) The CONFIRMING PARTY / CO-PROMOTER, [Entity Name], has become the absolute owner of
    [Survey Nos.] under [Deed refs]...
(c) The VENDOR and the CONFIRMING PARTY have mutually agreed to develop the entire Schedule A
    land as one single consolidated residential plotted development under the name and style
    "[Project Name]"...
(d) The VENDOR has obtained Layout Approval... The project has been registered with TNRERA...
(e) The CONFIRMING PARTY / CO-PROMOTER, being the co-developer and co-owner of the project,
    hereby confirms, ratifies and acknowledges the sale of the Schedule C property... and confirms
    that it has no objection to the said sale.

## Schedule Mapping

When restructuring, the schedule structure expands from 2 to 3 levels:

| Original | Restructured | Content |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| Schedule A | **Schedule A** | Total project land — all survey numbers from both entities, with ownership breakdown per entity and patta numbers |
| — | **Schedule B (NEW)** | The specific survey number from which the plot is carved (e.g. 158/1C9A, Ac.0.25) |
| Schedule B | **Schedule C** | The plot itself (Plot No. 119, 1,492.70 sq. ft.) — the property being conveyed |

### Schedule A format (detailed)

```
Survey Nos. held by the VENDOR (M/s. [Entity Name]):
• [Survey] ([Extent]) — under [Deed], Patta [No]
• ...

Development rights held by VENDOR under JDA: [List]

Survey Nos. held by the CONFIRMING PARTY (M/s. [Entity Name]):
• [Survey] ([Extent]) — under [Deed], Patta [No]
• ...
```

## Execution Block Swap

When VENDOR and CONFIRMING PARTY swap entities, the execution block must also swap:

### Before (typical first draft)
```
EXECUTED BY THE VENDOR:
  For and on behalf of M/s. SEVAGANAPALLI LAND PARTNERS
  Name: NISHANT RANKA (Managing Partner)

EXECUTED BY THE CONFIRMING PARTY / CO-PROMOTER:
  For and on behalf of M/s. DRA REALTY PRIVATE LIMITED
  Name: NISHANT RANKA (Director)
```

### After (restructured per Prakash opinion)
```
EXECUTED BY THE VENDOR:
  For and on behalf of M/s. DRA REALTY PRIVATE LIMITED
  Name: NISHANT RANKA (Director)

EXECUTED BY THE CONFIRMING PARTY / CO-PROMOTER:
  For and on behalf of M/s. SEVAGANAPALLI LAND PARTNERS
  Name: NISHANT RANKA (Managing Partner)
```

## Section B and C Text Updates

The "Project and Plotted Development" section (Section B) changes from:
> "The VENDOR is the developer and owner of the project..."

To:
> "The VENDOR and the CONFIRMING PARTY / CO-PROMOTER are collectively the developers
> and co-owners of the project..."

The "Offer, Acceptance and Consideration" section (Section C) changes from:
> "The VENDOR and the Co-Promoter agreed to transfer the Schedule B property..."

To:
> "The VENDOR and the CONFIRMING PARTY agreed to transfer the Schedule C property..."

## Part II: Rename All Schedule B → Schedule C

Every "Schedule B" reference in the operative clauses (Part II) must change to "Schedule C":
- `(i) Title and Ownership: The VENDOR has a valid... title to the Schedule B property`
  → `... Schedule C property`
- `(ii) No Encumbrances: ...`
- All indemnity, possession, review clauses, etc.

This is a mechanical find-and-replace limited to Part II. The recitals section's
references to Schedule B (meaning the source survey) stay untouched.

## Key Pitfalls

1. **Cross-reference updates.** The GPA recital often says "the same property covered under
   Recital (xi) above" — after renumbering, this must become "Recital (ix) above" (or
   whatever the new number is). Match on the exact text `Recital (n) above` and update.

2. **VENDOR's representative info.** When VENDOR changes from SLP (Managing Partner) to
   DRA Realty (Director), the "The VENDOR is represented by its..." paragraph must change
   the title from "Managing Partner" to "Director" and the authorization from
   "Partnership Deed" to "Board Resolution dated [date]".

3. **CONFIRMING PARTY description.** When the confirming party flips to SLP, its description
   needs the partnership registration number and PAN, not the CIN number.

4. **Schedule A is referenced BEFORE it appears.** The Section A recitals text references
   "Schedule A" and "Schedule B" and "Schedule C" — these MUST be defined before the
   recitals section. The schedule definitions come at the end of the document, so the
   recitals use forward references. This is standard legal drafting practice.

5. **The "Schedule B property (Plot No. 119)..." note** under the original Schedule A
   listing should change to "Schedule C property (Plot No. 119)..." when the structure
   expands to 3 schedules.

6. **Duplicate paragraphs.** When inserting new content into a .docx via lxml, old
   paragraphs that were not explicitly removed can create duplicate headers. Always
   verify the final document structure by reading all paragraph text after the
   restructure operation.
