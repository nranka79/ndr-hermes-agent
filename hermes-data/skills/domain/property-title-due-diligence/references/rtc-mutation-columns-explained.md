# Karnataka RTC: Understanding Mutation Register (MR) Entries

## MR = Mutation Register, NOT a Court Order

**Critical distinction:** In Karnataka land records, "MR" stands for **Mutation Register** (also called "ಭೂಮಿಮಯ" or simply "ಮೆ" in Kannada). It is a **revenue department document**, not a judicial or court record.

An MR entry like "MR 4205/2003-04" or "94R10/88-89" or "ಮೆ ೧೦/೦೨೯" is:
- The serial number assigned by the Village Accountant / Revenue Inspector when a mutation is entered into the Mutation Register
- A record of changes in land ownership/possession triggered by: inheritance, sale, gift, partition, land acquisition, court decree, etc.
- NOT a court case number or litigation reference

## Common MR Formats in Kannada RTCs

| Kannada Entry | Meaning | Example Year |
|---|---|---|
| ಮೆ ೧೦/೦೨೯ | Mutation Entry 10/029 | Kannada numerals |
| 94R10/88-89 | MR 10, 1988-89 | English numerals |
| MR 4205/2003-04 | MR 4205, 2003-04 | Full format |
| US 619101 | Unknown format - check with VA | Various |

## Where MR Numbers Appear in RTC

- **Column 11** (ಇತರ ಹಕ್ಕುಗಳು ಮತ್ತು ಋಣಗಳು / Other Rights and Liabilities): Lists MR numbers, mortgages, litigation
- **Column 12+** (Mutation details section): Shows the MR number alongside the year of mutation
- **Column 9 remarks area**: Sometimes written near khatedar name changes

## How to Verify an MR Entry

1. The MR number alone tells you a change was RECORDED, not what kind
2. To find the nature: get the **certified copy of the Mutation Register** (MR extract) from the Tahsildar's office or Village Accountant
3. The MR extract will state: "Mutation on account of Sale / Inheritance / Gift / Partition / Court Decree / Land Acquisition"
4. In online Bhoomi/Atalji land records, the nature of mutation may appear when you view the mutation details

## Common Pitfalls (DO NOT DO THIS)

| Wrong Assumption | Correct Understanding |
|---|---|
| "MR 4205/2003-04 = Court order" | MR is a mutation number, not a court case |
| "The MR confirms land acquisition" | MR only confirms a change was recorded; check the MR extract for the reason |
| "MR number format tells me what kind of mutation" | All mutations share the same MR numbering system |
| "Blank RTC column means no issues" | Blank means the mutation was recorded in an RTC page you haven't seen yet, OR was never recorded |

## Procedure When You Find an MR Number in an RTC

1. **Note the MR number and year** exactly as written
2. **Search for it in other documents**: the Sale Deed, EC, or Confirmation Deed may reference the same MR
3. **If the MR relates to inheritance (IHR)**: look for IHR (Index of Holders of Rights) records — these are inheritance certificates issued under the Karnataka Land Revenue Act
4. **If the MR relates to Land Acquisition (LA)**: verify through EC and Tahsildar records that compensation was paid and possession was taken
5. **Never guess the nature of the mutation** from the MR number alone — always cite what you KNOW from the document you've read
