## Priority Pass Activation

**URL:** `https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/activate-your-account`

Required: membership number (starts with "PP"), found on:
- Welcome letter from bank
- Netbanking: Cards → Debit/Credit Card → Priority Pass section

**Both users (Nishant + Roshini) must activate separately** — each card = separate PP membership.

## Key Learnings This Session

- Concordia International at DPS gets full at peak hours — Flight Club DPS is the reliable backup
- "Flight Club DPS hours 05:00–01:00" means opens 5 AM, closes 1 AM next day (overnight operation)
- Priority Pass activation URL: `prioritypass.com/en-GB/activate-your-account` (not `prioritypass.com/en-GB/activate`)
- Unlimited guest policy = 1 cardholder can bring entire family — so 2 cards (Nishant + Roshini) covers 2 adults + 2 kids
